*16 spells. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*
*Format: **Name** *(School)* — T: target | Dur | Save | SR | Desc | Cast — Description. Fields omitted if empty/default.*

- **Acid Maw** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR | Desc: Acid — Your animal companion's bite attack deals an additional 1d4 points of acid damage and the acid deals another 1d4 points of acid damage on the next round. The acid continue to deal damage for 1 additional round per 4 caster levels. Acid damage doesn't stack but a newer bite resets the duration.
- **Aspect of the Falcon** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 minute / level | Desc: Polymorph — You gain a +3 competence bonus on Perception checks, a +1 competence bonus on on ranged attacks and the critical multiplier for bows and crossbows increases to 19-20/x3 (note that this does not stack with keen / Improved Critical).

Affected creatures may make a combat manoeuvre, Athletics or Mobility check against the spell's DC to free themselves. Creatures that succeed in their save can move as normal but must save again next round if they remain in the area. The area is considered difficult terrain.
