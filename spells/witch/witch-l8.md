# Witch Spells — Level 8
*15 spells. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*
*Format: **Name** *(School)* — T: target | Dur | Save | SR | Desc | Cast — Description. Fields omitted if empty/default.*

- **Cure Serious Wounds, Mass** *(Conjuration)* — T: All creatures within 30' of target point within close range | Save: Will half (if used to damage) | Desc: Cure — Affected creatures are healed 3d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level. This spell will damage undead by the same amount, in which case they receive a Will save to take only half damage.
- **Death Clutch** *(Necromancy)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Save: Fortitude partial | SR | Desc: Evil. Death — A creature with 200 HP or less failing its Fortitude save is slain. A creature with more that 200 HP suffers 1d4 Constitution damage and 1d4 Constitution bleed unless it makes a Fortitude save. If the target succeeds in its saving throw, it is staggered for 1 round regardless.
- **Destruction** *(Necromancy)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Save: Fortitude partial | Desc: Death — Delivers 10 points of damage per caster level. If the target makes its Fortitude save, it takes 10d6 damage.
- **Frightful Aspect** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 minute / level | Desc: Polymorph — You gain a +6 size bonus to Strength, a +4 Size bonus to Constitution, a +6 natural armour bonus, DR 10/magic and spell resistance 10 + half your caster level. Enemy creatures within 30' are shaken. if you hit a shaken creature with a melee attack, it becomes frightened for 1d4 rounds.
- **Horrid Wilting** *(Necromancy)* — T: Target point within long range, 40' burst | Save: Fortitude half | SR — Each creature in the area of effect takes 1d6 damage per caster level. Water elementals and plant creatures take 1d8 damage per caster level.
- **Inflict Serious Wounds, Mass** *(Necromancy)* — T: All creatures within 30' of target point within close range | Save: Will half (if used to damage) — Affected creatures rake 3d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level. A Will save halves this damage. This spell will heal undead by the same amount.
- **Mind Blank** *(Abjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within close range | Dur: 24 hours | SR — The spell grants a +8 resistance bonus against all mind-affecting spells and effects.
- **Power Word Stun** *(Enchantment)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Dur: Varies | SR | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Compulsion, Stunned — Any creature with 50 or less HP is stunned for 4d4 rounds. If the creature's current HP is between 51 and 100, the effect lasts for 2d4 rounds. If the current HP is 101-150, the effect lasts for 1d4 rounds. Creatures with more than 150HP are unaffected.
- **Prediction of Failure** *(Divination)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will partial | SR | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Fear, Curse — The target is permanently shaken and sickened. A successful will save reduces the duration to 1 round per level.
- **Resurrection** *(Conjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within any range | Cast: Full Round — The target is restored to full hit points and health with no loss of prepared spells.
- **Rift of Ruin** *(Conjuration)* — T: Target point within long range, 10' burst | Dur: 1 round + 1 round / level | Save: Reflex negates | Desc: Evil, Chaos — Create a 60' deep opening into the Abyss. Creatures falling in take 6d6 points of falling damage and 6d6 damage each round (randomly selected from acid, bludgeoning, cold, electricity, fire, piercing slashing and sonic). Each round an affected creature may make a Reflex save to take half damage. Getting out requires a successful Athletics or Mobility check (DC 35). When the rift closes, creatures inside take double damage and are knocked prone.
- **Soulreaver** *(Necromancy)* — T: All creatures within 25' of target point within close range | Save: Fortitude partial | SR | Desc: Death — Deals 1d6 points of damage per caster level (max 20d6) to living creatures in the area of effect.
- **Stormbolts** *(Evocation)* — T: All enemies within 30' of caster | Save: Fortitude partial | SR | Desc: Electricity — Enemies within the area of effect take 1d8 electricity damage per level (maximum 20d8) and are stunned for 1 round. Creatures that make a Fortitude save take half damage and are not stunned.
- **Summon Elder Elemental** *(Conjuration)* — T: Target point within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Desc: Summoning | Cast: Full Round — Summon an elder fire, water, air or earth elemental.
- **Summon Monster VIII** *(Conjuration)* — T: Target point within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Desc: Summoning | Cast: Full Round — Summon a Movanic Deva (non-evil) or Frost Giant, 1d3 Bogeymen or 1d4+1 Axiomites / Soul Eaters.
