# Witch Spells — Level 6
*19 spells. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*
*Format: **Name** *(School)* — T: target | Dur | Save | SR | Desc | Cast — Description. Fields omitted if empty/default.*

- **Banshee Blast** *(Necromancy)* — T: All creatures within a 30' cone | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Reflex half (damage); Will negates (fear) | SR | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Fear, Death, Sonic — Creatures in the area of effect take 1d4 damage per caster level (maximum 15d4). A successful Reflex save halves the damage. Creatures failing their Reflex saves must make a Will save or become panicked for 1 round / caster level.
- **Cloak of Dreams** *(Enchantment)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will negates | SR | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Compulsion | Cast: Full round — You are surrounded by an aura that causes all living creatures within 5' to make a successful Will save each round or fall asleep for 1 minute. Sleeping creatures are helpless but wake up after being wounded.
- **Cone of Cold** *(Evocation)* — T: All creatures within a 50' cone | Save: Reflex half | SR | Desc: Cold — Creatures in the area of effect take 1d6 cold damage per caster level (maximum 15d6).
- **Cure Light Wounds, Mass** *(Conjuration)* — T: All creatures within 30' of target point within close range | Save: Will half (if used to damage) | Desc: Cure — Affected creatures are healed 1d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level. This spell will damage undead by the same amount, in which case they receive a Will save to take only half damage.
- **Dispel Magic, Greater** *(Abjuration)* — T: One creature within medium range — Either removes one spell effect from everything within a 20' burst or removes multiple effects from a single creature.
- **Eyebite** *(Necromancy)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Fortitude negates | SR | Desc: Emotion, Negative Emotion — For each round of the spells duration, you may target a single living creature as a swift action. The effects depends on the target's HD:10 or greater: the target is sickened for 10 minutes per caster level5 - 9: the target is frightened and sickened4 or less: the target is paralysed, frightened and sickened.
- **Fester, Mass** *(Necromancy)* — T: Target point within close range, 30' burst | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Fortitude partial — All enemies in the area of effect gain SR 12 against healing effects for the duration of the spell. If a creature makes it save, the effect only lasts 1 round.
- **Hellfire Ray** *(Evocation)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | SR | Desc: Fire, Evil — Fire a ray as a ranged touch attack dealing 1d6 points of damage per caster level (maximum 15d6). You can create two rays at 15th level and three at 19th level. Half the damage is fire but the other half is unholy and not reduced by fire resistance.
- **Heroism, Greater** *(Enchantment)* — T: One friendly creature within close range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR — The recipient of the spell gains a +4 morale bonus on attack rolls, saves and skill checks, immunity to fear and temporary HP equal to your caster level.
- **Inflict Light Wounds, Mass** *(Necromancy)* — T: All creatures within 30' of target point within close range | Save: Will half (if used to damage) — Affected creatures rake 1d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level. A Will save halves this damage. This spell will heal undead by the same amount.
- **Phantasmal Putrefaction** *(Illusion)* — T: All enemies within 30' burst, centred on target point within medium range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will, then Fortitude partial | SR | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Fear — Enemies in the area of effect perceive their flesh to be rotting away. If they fail a Will save, they take 1d4 points of Wisdom damage. Each subsequent round that an affected creature fails its Will save, it must make a Fortitude save or faint for 1 round. A creature that faints is treated as sleeping.
- **Plague Storm** *(Necromancy)* — T: All enemies within 20' of target point within medium range | Dur: 1 minute / level | Save: Fortitude negates | Desc: Evil, Disease — Creatures in the area of effect must make a Fortitude save or contract a disease as though affected by Contagion.
- **Raise Dead** *(Conjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within any range | Cast: Full round — Restores a party member to life with HP equal to her current HD and removes the death's door condition. The raised ally gains two negative levels which must be restored by other means.
- **Slay Living** *(Necromancy)* — T: One enemy creature within touch range | Save: Fortitude partial | Desc: Death | Cast: Standard action — If you succeed on a melee touch attack, the target takes 12d6 damage +1 point per caster level. If it makes a successful Fortitude save, the damage is reduced to 3d6 +1 per caster level.
- **Stone to Flesh** *(Transmutation)* — T: One friendly creature within any range | SR — Restores a petrified creature to its normal state.
- **Summon Huge Elemental** *(Conjuration)* — T: Target point within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Desc: Summoning | Cast: Full Round — Summon a huge fire, water, air or earth elemental.
- **Summon Monster VI** *(Conjuration)* — T: Target point within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Desc: Summoning | Cast: Full Round — Summon an Axiomite (non-evil) or Soul Eater, 1d3 Bralanis / Redcaps or 1d4+1 extraplanar dire wolves.
- **Transformation** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level — You gain a +4 enhancement bonus to Strength, Dexterity and Constitution, a +4 natural armour bonus to AC, a +5 competence bonus to Fortitude saves and your base attack bonus equals your character level.
- **True Seeing** *(Divination)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR — The target of the spell sees through blur, displacement and invisibility effects.
