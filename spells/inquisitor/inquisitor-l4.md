# Inquisitor Spells — Level 4
*20 spells. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*
*Format: **Name** *(School)* — T: target | Dur | Save | SR | Desc | Cast — Description. Fields omitted if empty/default.*

- **Crusader's Edge** *(Transmutation)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 minute / level | Save: Fortitude partial | Desc: Good — The target ally's melee weapon gains the bane property against evil outsiders. Creatures struck by the weapon must make a Fortitude save or be nauseated for 1d3 rounds. If they make their save they are sickened for 1 round.
- **Chaos Hammer** *(Evocation)* — T: All creatures within 20' of target creature within medium range | Save: Will partial | Desc: Chaos — Lawful creatures within the area of effect take 1d8 damage per two caster levels (max 5d8) and are slowed for 1d6 rounds. Lawful outsiders take 1d6 damage per caster level (max 10d6). A successful will save halves the damage and negates the slow effect. Neutral creatures take half damage (or a quarter with a successful save) and are not slowed.

- **Dismissal** *(Abjuration)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Save: Will negates | SR — The spell forces an extraplanar creature back to its home plane if it fails a Will save.
- **Divine Power** *(Evocation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level — You gain +1 luck bonus on attack rolls, damage rolls, Strength checks and Strength-based skill checks for every three caster levels. You also gain temporary HP equal to your caster level. When you make a full attack, you gain an extra attack at your full AB.
- **Fear** *(Necromancy)* — T: All creatures within a 30' cone | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will negates | SR | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Fear, Emotion — Creatures in the area of effect become frightened for the spell's duration unless they make a successful Will save. If the Will save succeeds, the creature is shaken for 1 round.
- **Forced Repentance** *(Enchantment)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will negates | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Compulsion, Emotion, Negative Emotion — If the target of the spell is evil, but not of the evil subtype, it must make a Will save or repent its transgressions for the duration of the spell. Attacking the target will cancel the effect.
- **Freedom of Movement** *(Abjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 10 minutes / level — The subject is immune to all magic that hinders movement, including paralysis effects, Slow, Web and Acid Fog. Any attempts to grapple the subject fail.
- **Greater Invisibility** *(Illusion)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 round / level | SR — The target becomes invisible for the spell's duration and may attack freely without breaking the invisibility.
- **Hold Monster** *(Enchantment)* — T: One enemy creature within medium range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will negates | SR | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Compulsion — This spell affects all living creatures. If the target fails its Will save it is paralysed. The target may attempt a new save each round to end the effect.
- **Holy Smite** *(Evocation)* — T: All creatures within 20' of target creature within medium range | Save: Will partial | Desc: Good — Evil creatures within the area of effect take 1d8 damage per two caster levels (max 5d8) and are blinded for 1 round. Evil outsiders take 1d6 damage per caster level (max 10d6). A successful will save halves the damage and negates the blind effect. Neutral creatures take half damage (or a quarter with a successful save) and are not blinded.
- **Inflict Critical Wounds** *(Necromancy)* — T: One creature within touch range | Save: Will half (if used to damage) — The target receives 4d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level, taking only half damage on a successful Will save. This spell will heal undead by the same amount.
- **Neutralize Poison** *(Conjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range — You remove all poisons from the creature touched. You must make a caster level check against the DC of each poison affecting the target.
- **Order's Wrath** *(Evocation)* — T: All creatures within 20' of target creature within medium range | Save: Will partial | Desc: Law — Chaotic creatures within the area of effect take 1d8 damage per two caster levels (max 5d8) and are dazed for 1 round. Chaotic outsiders take 1d6 damage per caster level (max 10d6). A successful will save halves the damage and negates the daze effect. Neutral creatures take half damage (or a quarter with a successful save) and are not slowed.
- **Protection from Energy, Communal** *(Abjuration)* — T: All allies within 25' | Dur: 10 minutes | SR — The recipients receive temporary immunity against a chosen damage type. When the spell absorbs 12 points of damage per caster level (maximum 120 points), it is discharged.
- **Restoration** *(Conjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within close range | Cast: Full round — Restoration cures all temporary ability damage and all points permanently drained from a single ability score. It also dispels temporary negative levels or one permanent negative level. Finally, it removes the effects of fatigue and exhaustion.
- **Stoneskin** *(Abjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 10 minutes / level | SR — The target gains DR 10/adamantine. When the spell has prevented 150 HP of damage, it dissipates.
- **Unholy Blight** *(Evocation)* — T: All creatures within 20' of target creature within medium range | Save: Will partial | Desc: Evil — Good creatures within the area of effect take 1d8 damage per two caster levels (max 5d8) and are sickened for 1d4 rounds. Good outsiders take 1d6 damage per caster level (max 10d6). A successful will save halves the damage and negates the sickened effect. Neutral creatures take half damage (or a quarter with a successful save) and are not sickened.
