# Inquisitor Spells — Level 5
*16 spells. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*
*Format: **Name** *(School)* — T: target | Dur | Save | SR | Desc | Cast — Description. Fields omitted if empty/default.*

- **Banishment** *(Abjuration)* — T: Target point within close range, 30' burst | Save: Will negates | SR — The spell forces extraplanar creatures back to their home planes if they fail a Will save. Creatures totalling up to 2HD per caster level can be dismissed.
- **Break Enchantment** *(Abjuration)* — T: All allies within 30' | Cast: Full Round — Free allies from enchantments, transmutations and curses. For each such effect roll make a caster level check (maximum 15) against a DC of 11 + caster level of the effect. This spell only works against spells of 5th level and lower.
- **Castigate, Mass** *(Enchantment)* — T: One enemy creature within medium range, 30' burst | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will partial | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Fear, Compulsion, Emotion, Negative Emotion — All enemies within the area of effect must make a Will save or cower with fear. If attempt is successful, the creature is merely shaken for 1 round. Each subsequent round, a cowering creature may attempt a new save.
- **Chains of Light** *(Conjuration)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Reflex negates | SR | Desc: Good — The target is paralysed unless it makes its Reflex save. A paralysed creature may attempt a new save each round.
- **Cure Light Wounds, Mass** *(Conjuration)* — T: All creatures within 30' of target point within close range | Save: Will half (if used to damage) | Desc: Cure — Affected creatures are healed 1d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level. This spell will damage undead by the same amount, in which case they receive a Will save to take only half damage.
- **Disrupting Weapon** *(Transmutation)* — T: One friendly creature within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will negates | Cast: Standard action — This imbues a melee weapon with the ability to destroy undead. Any undead creature with HD equal to or less than your caster level must make a Will save or be destroyed when struck with the imbued weapon.
- **Flame Strike** *(Evocation)* — T: All creatures within 10' of target point within medium range | Save: Reflex half | Desc: Fire — All creatures in the area of effect take 1d6 points per caster level (maximum 15d6). Half the damage is divine damage and not subject to fire resistance.
- **Greater Command** *(Enchantment)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will negates — This spell behaves like command. It affects up to one creature per caster level and the effect lasts for 1d4 rounds. Affected creatures are allowed a new save each round.
- **Inflict Light Wounds, Mass** *(Necromancy)* — T: All creatures within 30' of target point within close range | Save: Will half (if used to damage) — Affected creatures rake 1d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level. A Will save halves this damage. This spell will heal undead by the same amount.
- **Profane Nimbus** *(Evocation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level | Desc: Evil — Any good creature striking you with a melee weapon, an unarmed attack or natural attack takes 1d6 points of damage +1 per caster level (maximum +15). You take half damage (or no damage on a successful save) from magical attacks with a good descriptor.
- **Resounding Blow** *(Evocation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Fortitude partial | Desc: Sonic — Your weapon does an additional 1d6 sonic damage. If you are using Judgment or Smite Evil against an enemy struck by the weapon, it must make a Fortitude save or be staggered for 1 round. On a critical hit, the target is stunned for 1 round and blinded for 1d6 rounds unless it makes a Fortitude save.
- **Righteous Might** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level — You increase in size by one category. You gain a +4 size bonus to Strength and Constitution and take a -2 penalty to Dexterity. You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to natural AC. You gain DR 5/evil (if good or neutral) or DR 5/good (if evil). At 15th level the DR increases to 10.
- **Sacred Nimbus** *(Evocation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level | Desc: Good — Any evil creature striking you with a melee weapon, an unarmed attack or natural attack takes 1d6 points of damage +1 per caster level (maximum +15). You take half damage (or no damage on a successful save) from magical attacks with an evil descriptor.
- **Spell Resistance** *(Abjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 minute / level | Cast: Standard action — The target gains spell resistance equal to 12 plus your caster level.
- **Stoneskin, Communal** *(Abjuration)* — T: All allies within 30' | Dur: 10 minutes | SR — All allies gain DR 10/adamantine. When the spell has prevented 150 HP of damage, the effect dissipates.
- **True Seeing** *(Divination)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR — The target of the spell sees through blur, displacement and invisibility effects.
