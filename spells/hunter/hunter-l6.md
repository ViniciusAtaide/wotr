- **Tar Pool** *(Transmutation)* — T: All creatures within 20' burst, centred on target point within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Reflex partial | Desc: Fire — The area of effect is turned into hot tar. Creatures in the area take 1d6 points of fire damage per two caster levels and must make a Reflex save or become entangled until they make a successful Reflex save, Strength check or Mobility check. The area is difficult terrain and inflicts a -5 penalty to Mobility checks. Creatures remaining in the area take 2d6 fire points of damage each round and must reattempt a Reflex save or become entangled. A creature that falls prone takes a -4 penalty on Reflex saves or skill checks. After leaving the tar, a creature is unable to act for 1 round.
