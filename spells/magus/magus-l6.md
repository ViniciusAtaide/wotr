# Magus Spells — Level 6
*17 spells. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*
*Format: **Name** *(School)* — T: target | Dur | Save | SR | Desc | Cast — Description. Fields omitted if empty/default.*

- **Acid Fog** *(Conjuration)* — T: All enemies within 20' of target point within medium range | Dur: 1 round / level | SR | Desc: Acid — Creatures in the area of effect move at half speed and take a -2 penalty on attack and damage rolls. Each round any creature in the fog takes 2d6 acid damage.
- **Bear's Endurance, Mass** *(Transmutation)* — T: All allies within 30' burst, centred on target creature within close range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR — All allies within the area of effect gain a +4 enhancement bonus to Constitution.
- **Beast Shape IV** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 minute / level | Desc: Polymorph — You become a large smilodon, a large shambling mound or a large wyvern.
- **Bull's Strength, Mass** *(Transmutation)* — T: All allies within 30' burst, centred on target creature within close range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR — All allies within the area of effect gain a +4 enhancement bonus to Strength.
- **Cat's Grace, Mass** *(Transmutation)* — T: All allies within 30' burst, centred on target creature within close range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR — All allies within the area of effect gain a +4 enhancement bonus to Dexterity.
- **Chain Lightning** *(Evocation)* — T: One enemy creature within long range | Save: Reflex partial | SR | Desc: Electricity — Casts a lightning bolt that does 1d6 electricity damage per caster level to the primary target (maximum 20d6). After it strikes, it can arc to a secondary target within 30' doing 1d6 electricity damage per caster level to the secondary target. It can arc repeatedly striking a number of targets equal to your caster level but no target may be struck more than once. Affected creatures may attempt a Reflex save for half damage.
- **Disintegrate** *(Transmutation)* — T: One enemy creature within medium range | Save: Fortitude partial | SR — Make a successful ranged touch attack to hit. Any creature struck takes 2d6 damage per caster level. If it is killed, it is disintegrated. If the target makes its Fortitude save, it only takes 5d6 damage.
- **Dispel Magic, Greater** *(Abjuration)* — T: One creature within medium range — Either removes one spell effect from everything within a 20' burst or removes multiple effects from a single creature.
- **Dragonkind I** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 minute / level | Desc: Polymorph — Become a Medium dragon-like creature. You gain a +4 size bonus to Strength, a +2 size bonus to Constitution, a +4 natural armour bonus, immunity to difficult terrain, a breath weapon and elemental resistance.You gain one bite attack (1-8), two claw attacks (1-6) and two wing attacks (1-4). The breath weapon can be used once per day and causes 6d8 damage with a Reflex save for half damage.
- **Elemental Body III** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 minute / level | Desc: Polymorph — You assume the form of a large air, earth, fire or water elemental.
- **Hellfire Ray** *(Evocation)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | SR | Desc: Fire, Evil — Fire a ray as a ranged touch attack dealing 1d6 points of damage per caster level (maximum 15d6). You can create two rays at 15th level and three at 19th level. Half the damage is fire but the other half is unholy and not reduced by fire resistance.
- **Sirocco** *(Evocation)* — T: All enemies within 20' burst, centred on target point within medium range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Fortitude partial | SR | Desc: Fire — Creatures in the area of effect take 4d6 fire damage + 1 point per caster level and are knocked prone. A successful Fortitude save halves the damage taken and avoids being knocked down. Any creature taking damage becomes fatigued (or exhausted if already fatigued). Creatures of the water subtype take a -4 penalty on saving throws.
- **Stone to Flesh** *(Transmutation)* — T: One friendly creature within any range | SR — Restores a petrified creature to its normal state.
- **Transformation** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level — You gain a +4 enhancement bonus to Strength, Dexterity and Constitution, a +4 natural armour bonus to AC, a +5 competence bonus to Fortitude saves and your base attack bonus equals your character level.
- **True Seeing** *(Divination)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR — The target of the spell sees through blur, displacement and invisibility effects.
- **Umbral Strike** *(Necromancy)* — T: One enemy creature within medium range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Fortitude partial | SR — Fire a ray as a ranged touch attack dealing 1d6 points of damage per caster level to the target and blinding them for 1 round / level. Half the ray's damage is cold, the other half negative energy. A successful Fortitude save halves the damage and negates the blindness. The ray ignores concealment (but not total concealment).
- **Walk through Space** *(Conjuration)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level — While under the effects of the spell, you can teleport up to 30' as a move action. Alternatively, you can use a move action to teleport to a standing position from lying prone. Teleporting does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
