- **Elemental Body I** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 minute / level | Desc: Polymorph — You assume the form of a small air, earth, fire or water elemental.
- **Enlarge Person, Mass** *(Transmutation)* — T: All allies within 15' burst, centred on target point within close range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR | Cast: Full round — Increases the targets' size by 1 category. - **Shout** *(Evocation)* — T: All creatures within a 30' cone | Dur: 1 round | Save: Fortitude partial | SR | Desc: Stunned, Sonic — Creatures in the area of effect are stunned for 1 round and take 5d6 sonic damage. Creatures making their Fortitude saves take half damage and are not stunned.
- **Stoneskin** *(Abjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 10 minutes / level | SR — The target gains DR 10/adamantine. When the spell has prevented 150 HP of damage, it dissipates.
