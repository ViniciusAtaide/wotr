# Paladin Spells — Level 2
*15 spells. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*
*Format: **Name** *(School)* — T: target | Dur | Save | SR | Desc | Cast — Description. Fields omitted if empty/default.*

- **Arrow of Law** *(Evocation)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Save: Will partial | Desc: Law — Casts a bolt of energy at a target. The damage depends on the target's alignment:Lawful: no effectChaotic: 1d8 damage per two caster levels (maximum 5d8)Chaotic outsider: 1d6 damage per caster level (maximum 10d6) and dazed for 1 roundNeutral: half damage.A successful Will save halves the damage taken and negates any daze effect.
- **Aura of Greater Courage** *(Abjuration)* — T: Personal | Dur: 10 minutes / level | Desc: Emotion — You strengthen your Aura of Courage, rendering all allies immune to fear.
- **Bestow Grace** *(Abjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 minute / level — The creature touched adds its Charisma modifier to its saving throws for the duration of the spell.
- **Blessing of Courage and Life** *(Conjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within close range | Dur: 1 minute / level | Desc: Emotion — Grants the target a +2 morale bonus to saves against fear and death effects. The recipient may choose to swap the save bonus to recover 1d8 HP + 1 per caster level (maximum 1d8 + 10), ending the spell's effect.
- **Blessing of Luck and Resolve** *(Enchantment)* — T: One friendly creature within close range | Dur: 1 minute / level | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Compulsion — Grants the target a +2 morale bonus to saves against fear effects. If the target fails a save it may reroll again with a +4 morale bonus, ending the spell's effect.
- **Bull's Strength** *(Transmutation)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR — The target receives a +4 enhancement bonus to Strength.
- **Delay Poison** *(Conjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within close range | Dur: 1 hour / level — The subject becomes temporarily immune to poison. If the subject is poisoned, any further effects stop.
- **Eagle's Splendor** *(Transmutation)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR — The target receives a +4 enhancement bonus to Charisma.
- **Effortless Armor** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 minute / level — Armour you wear no longer reduces your speed. Reduce any armour check penalty by 1 + 1 / five caster levels (maximum 5).
- **Litany of Eloquence** *(Enchantment)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Dur: 1 round | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Charmed | Cast: Swift Action — The target of the spell is fascinated and does nothing for 1 round.
- **Litany of Entanglement** *(Conjuration)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Dur: 1 round | Save: Will negates | Cast: Swift Action — The target of the spell is entangled unless it makes a Will save.
- **Owl's Wisdom** *(Transmutation)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR — The target receives a +4 enhancement bonus to Wisdom.
- **Protection from Chaos / Evil, Communal** *(Abjuration)* — _See Wizard spell list ("Protection from Alignment, Communal")._
- **Remove Paralysis** *(Conjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within close range — Removes paralysis from the target.
- **Resist Energy** *(Abjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 10 minutes / level | SR — The recipient receives resist energy 10 against a chosen damage type. The resistance increases to 20 points at 7th level at 30 points at 11th level.
