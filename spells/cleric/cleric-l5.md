# Cleric Spells — Level 5
*26 spells. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*
*Format: **Name** *(School)* — T: target | Dur | Save | SR | Desc | Cast — Description. Fields omitted if empty/default.*

- **Angelic Aspect** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 minute / level | Desc: Good — You gain resistance to acid and cold 10 and DR 5/evil. You gain a +2 deflection bonus to AC and saves against attacks from evil creatures. Your weapons are classed as good for the purposes of overcoming DR.
- **Boneshatter** *(Necromancy)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Dur: 1 minute per level | Save: Fortitude half | SR — The target takes 1d6 damage per caster level (maximum 15d6) and is exhausted for 1 minute / level. A successful Fortitude save halves the damage and the creature is fatigued.
- **Break Enchantment** *(Abjuration)* — T: All allies within 30' | Cast: Full Round — Free allies from enchantments, transmutations and curses. For each such effect roll make a caster level check (maximum 15) against a DC of 11 + caster level of the effect. This spell only works against spells of 5th level and lower.
- **Breath of Life** *(Conjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Save: Will half (if used to damage) | Desc: Cure — The target is healed 5d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level and the spell will bring allies slain within the last 2 rounds to life. Creatures so restored gain a negative level that lasts one day. This spell will damage undead by the same amount, in which case they receive a Will save to take only half damage.
- **Burst of Glory** *(Enchantment)* — T: All allies within 10' | Dur: 1 round / level — Allies gain +1 sacred bonus to attack rolls and saves against fear along with 1 temporary HP per caster level.
- **Cleanse** *(Evocation)* — T: Personal — Heals 4d8 points of damage +1 per caster level. Cures the following conditions: ability damage, blinded, confused, dazzled, diseased, exhausted, fatigued, nauseated, poisoned and sickened.
- **Constricting Coils** *(Enchantment)* — T: One enemy creature within medium range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will negates (save each round) | SR | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Compulsion — The spell holds the target in place and inflicts 1d6+6 points of bludgeoning damage each round.
- **Cure Light Wounds, Mass** *(Conjuration)* — T: All creatures within 30' of target point within close range | Save: Will half (if used to damage) | Desc: Cure — Affected creatures are healed 1d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level. This spell will damage undead by the same amount, in which case they receive a Will save to take only half damage.
- **Disrupting Weapon** *(Transmutation)* — T: One friendly creature within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will negates | Cast: Standard action — This imbues a melee weapon with the ability to destroy undead. Any undead creature with HD equal to or less than your caster level must make a Will save or be destroyed when struck with the imbued weapon.
- **Flame Strike** *(Evocation)* — T: All creatures within 10' of target point within medium range | Save: Reflex half | Desc: Fire — All creatures in the area of effect take 1d6 points per caster level (maximum 15d6). Half the damage is divine damage and not subject to fire resistance. *(Listed as "Flamestrike" in the guide's cleric table.)*
- **Geniekind** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level | Desc: Polymorph — You transform into a djinni, efreeti, marid or shaitan. You gain +2 racial bonus to saves against paralysis, poison, sleep and stunning effects, a +4 enhancement bonus to natural AC and +2 enhancement bonus to Constitution. You are unaffected by difficult terrain. Other bonuses depend on the type of genie.DjinniYour melee attacks deal +1d6 of electricity damage and you gain resist electricity 10.EfreetiYour melee attacks deal +1d6 of fire damage and you gain resist fire 10.MaridYour melee attacks deal +1d6 of cold damage and you gain resist cold 10.ShaitanYour melee attacks deal +1d6 of acid damage and you gain resist acid 10.
- **Greater Command** *(Enchantment)* — T: One enemy creature within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will negates — This spell behaves like command. It affects up to one creature per caster level and the effect lasts for 1d4 rounds. Affected creatures are allowed a new save each round.
- **Inflict Light Wounds, Mass** *(Necromancy)* — T: All creatures within 30' of target point within close range | Save: Will half (if used to damage) — Affected creatures rake 1d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level. A Will save halves this damage. This spell will heal undead by the same amount.
- **Life Bubble** *(Abjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 hour — The affected creature is immune to inhaled disease and poison spells like Stinking Cloud and Cloudkill.
- **Pillar of Life** *(Conjuration)* — T: All creatures within 5' of target point within medium range | Dur: 1 round / level — Living creatures adjacent to the pillar can spend a standard action to heal 2d8 points of damage +1 per caster level (max +20). If an undead creatures moves into the pillar, it takes 1d6 points of damage per caster level (max 10d6). This spell will only affect any given creature once.
- **Profane Nimbus** *(Evocation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level | Desc: Evil — Any good creature striking you with a melee weapon, an unarmed attack or natural attack takes 1d6 points of damage +1 per caster level (maximum +15). You take half damage (or no damage on a successful save) from magical attacks with a good descriptor.
- **Raise Dead** *(Conjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within any range | Cast: Full round — Restores a party member to life with HP equal to her current HD and removes the death's door condition. The raised ally gains two negative levels which must be restored by other means.
- **Righteous Might** *(Transmutation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level — You increase in size by one category. You gain a +4 size bonus to Strength and Constitution and take a -2 penalty to Dexterity. You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to natural AC. You gain DR 5/evil (if good or neutral) or DR 5/good (if evil). At 15th level the DR increases to 10.
- **Sacred Nimbus** *(Evocation)* — T: Personal | Dur: 1 round / level | Desc: Good — Any evil creature striking you with a melee weapon, an unarmed attack or natural attack takes 1d6 points of damage +1 per caster level (maximum +15). You take half damage (or no damage on a successful save) from magical attacks with an evil descriptor.
- **Serenity** *(Enchantment)* — T: All creatures within 30' burst, centred on target creature within medium range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Will negates | SR | Desc: Mind-Affecting, Compulsion, Emotion — The targets of the spell take 3d6 damage per round if they try to harm another creature.
- **Slay Living** *(Necromancy)* — T: One enemy creature within touch range | Save: Fortitude partial | Desc: Death | Cast: Standard action — If you succeed on a melee touch attack, the target takes 12d6 damage +1 point per caster level. If it makes a successful Fortitude save, the damage is reduced to 3d6 +1 per caster level.
- **Spell Resistance** *(Abjuration)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 minute / level | Cast: Standard action — The target gains spell resistance equal to 12 plus your caster level.
- **Summon Large Elemental** *(Conjuration)* — T: Target point within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Desc: Summoning | Cast: Full Round — Summon a large fire, water, air or earth elemental.
- **Summon Monster V** *(Conjuration)* — T: Target point within close range | Dur: 1 round / level | Desc: Summoning | Cast: Full Round — Summon a Bralani Azata (non-evil) or Redcap, 1d3 extraplanar dire wolves or 1d4+1 extraplanar monitor lizards.
- **True Seeing** *(Divination)* — T: One friendly creature within touch range | Dur: 1 minute / level | SR — The target of the spell sees through blur, displacement and invisibility effects.
- **Vinetrap** *(Conjuration)* — T: One enemy creature within long range | Dur: 1 round / level | Save: Reflex partial — The target becomes paralysed and entangled. A successful Reflex save negates the paralysis and the subject may attempt a new saving throw each round. An entangled subject takes 2d4 points of piercing damage per round.
