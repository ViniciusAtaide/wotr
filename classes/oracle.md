# Oracle
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Oracle has the following characteristics:

- Primary attribute: Charisma
- Medium (3/4) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast divine spells up to 9th-level
- Spells are cast spontaneously
- Uses the Cleric spell list
- 8 HP at level 1, then 5 HP per level (d8 hit die)
- High saving throws for Will, low saving throws for Fortitude and Reflex
- 3 skill points per level.

Class Skills: Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Lore (Religion), Persuasion.

#### Oracle Proficiencies
Oracles are proficient with simple weapons, light and medium armour and shields (but not tower shields).

#### Oracle's Curse
An Oracle bears a Curse which hinders him but which also comes with benefits. The level of a curse is equal to the Oracle's class level plus one for every two non-Oracle class levels. The choices are as follows:

##### Blackened
You take a -4 penalty on weapon attack rolls but add *Burning Hands* to your list of known spells. At 5th level, *Burning Arc* and *Scorching Ray* are added to your known spells. At 10th level, the weapon attack penalty is reduced to -2 and *Fire Snake* is added to your list of known spells. Finally, at 15th level, Hellfire Ray is added to your list of known spells.

##### Demonic
You take a -3 penalty on Persuasion skill checks when used for diplomacy but gain +2 to Bluff and Intimidate checks. At 5th level, you gain a +4 bonus to saving throws against fear effects. At 10th level, you become immune to poison. Finally, at 15th level, any weapon you wield is treated as chaotic and evil for purposes of overcoming damage reduction.

##### Hellbound
You take a -3 penalty on Persuasion skill checks when used for diplomacy but gain +2 to Bluff and Intimidate checks. At 5th level, you gain a +4 bonus to saving throws against charm effects. At 10th level, you become immune to fire. Finally, at 15th level, any weapon you wield is treated as lawful and evil for purposes of overcoming damage reduction.

##### Lame
Your base land speed is reduced by 10' but your speed is never reduced further due to encumbrance. At 5th level, you become immune to the fatigued condition. At 10th level, your speed is never reduced by armour. Finally, at 15th level, you become immune to the exhausted condition.

##### Plagued
You take a -1 penalty on saving throws against disease effects but you are immune to the sickened condition. At 5th level, you add *Pox Pustules* to your list of known spells as a 2nd level spell. At 10th level, you add +2 to the DC of any disease effect. Finally, at 15th level you become immune to disease.

##### Powerless Prophecy
You are staggered for the first round of combat but gain the Uncanny Dodge ability. At 5th level, you gain a +4 insight bonus to initiative checks. At 10th level, you gain the Improved Uncanny Dodge ability. Finally, at 15th level, you gain a +4 insight bonus to your saving throws and AC during the first round of combat.

##### Pranked
You take a -4 penalty to initiative checks and add *Faerie Fire* to your list of known spells. At 5th level, you add *Glitterdust* and *Mirror image* to your list of known spells. At 10th level, you add *Confusion* to your list of known spells as a 5th level spell. Finally, at 15th level, you add *Insanity* to your list of known spells.

##### Wasting
You take a -4 penalty on Charisma-based skill checks (other than Intimidate) but gain a +4 competence bonus to saves against disease. At 5th level, you become immune to the sickened condition. At 10th level, you become immune to disease. Finally, at 15th level, you become immune to the nauseated condition.

##### Wolf-Scarred Face
You have a 10% chance of spell failure but gain a 1d4 bite attack (or 1d3 if you are small). At 5th level, you add *Magic Fang* to your list of known spells and your bite attack does 1d6 (or 1d4) damage. At 10th level, your bite attack damage increases to 1d8 (or 1d6). Finally, at 15th level, you add *Greater Magic Fang* to your list of known spells and your bite attack damage increases to 2d6 (1d8).

#### Mystery
Your choice of Mystery adds to your list of known spells at even-numbered class levels and leads to a Final Revelation at 20th level. Some choices add to your list of class skills as well. The choices are listed in the Mysteries section.

#### Revelation
At 1st and 3rd levels and every 4th level thereafter, the Oracle gains an ability related to his choice of Mystery. These are also listed in the Mysteries section.

#### Final Revelation
At 20th level, an Oracle gains the capstone ability of his choice of Mystery.

#### Additional Spells
An Oracle may choose to add all "cure" spells or all "inflict" spells to his list of known spells at the appropriate levels.

##### Playing an Oracle
An Oracle plays similarly to a Cleric, swapping spontaneous casting for prepared casting and a Mystery for Domains. The obvious choice of Mystery is Nature since that grants you an animal companion from the off and Charisma to AC down the line. You can take a second Mystery as a mythic ability and Battle is the obvious one.

An Oracle also has to take a Curse. Since this can come with crippling disadvantages (-4 AB penalty, anyone?), you'll want to minimise these. *Plagued* seems to be OK; it doesn't give you much useful at later levels but it also doesn't give you anything too bad at 1st level and it (eventually) fixes itself.

A Divine Herbalist doesn't really lose too much, although most Revelations are better than the fixed abilities. The Lore bonus is OK, I guess. Note that any archetype that doesn't get a Revelation of choice at 1st level will prevent you from getting an Animal Companion until later.

A Dual-Cursed Oracle is heavily penalised by having to take on two curses, one of which never improves. The trade-off is two additional Revelations which may work for you.

An Enlightened Philosopher gains a free skill focus, which is nice, and the class-specific Final Revelation is very good. Mental Acuity has close to zero synergy, however, since an Oracle does not benefit from Intelligence. The only use will be a boost to knowledge skill checks. The archetype also interferes with the Mystery system, imposing a fixed, substandard bonus spell list. Worse, the interaction with Second Mystery is bugged, preventing you from receiving the bonus spells and the Final Revelation.

A Lone Strider gives up two Revelations for not very much. The Fade from Memory ability conflicts with itself: either you are close to enemies, in which case it does nothing, or you are attacking from range in which case you don't really need the benefits. You also have a restricted Mystery list.

A Possessed Oracle forces a specific Curse on you. Being staggered in every battle will get old.

Like many race-specific archetypes, the Purifier is quite bad. The Battle Mystery can give you heavy armour proficiency and you can simply use *Effortless Armor* to reduce the penalties of wearing armour. The other two abilities don't make up for three fewer Revelations.

The Seeker is OK. Exchanging two Revelations for three feats and a skill bonus is not bad. Note that the bonus feats are magic feats which limits their usefulness slightly since metamagic is a pain to use with spontaneous casting.

Finally, the Wind Whisperer is forced to take the Wind Mystery which isn't the best. It isn't the worst, either, but the archetype abilities are quite bad: *Haste* a couple of times a day and *Freedom of Movement* once per day (at 15th level!). Both are readily duplicated by spells.

#### Master Herbalist
A Divine Herbalist gains a competence bonus on Lore (Nature) checks equal to half his class level. In addition, he uses his Charisma modifier for Lore (Nature) checks in place of Wisdom. However, he does not gain additional class skills from his choice of mystery.

#### Healer's Way
A Divine Herbalist can heal 1d6 damage per two class levels a number of time per day equal to 1 + his Charisma modifier. This is a standard action when used others and a swift action when used on himself.

#### Master Healing Technique
From 7th level when using Healer's Way, a Divine Herbalist can remove afflictions by making a Lore (Nature) check against the DC of the condition:

| Conditions | DC |
| --- | --- |
| Fatigued, shaken, sickened | 20 |
| Dazed, staggered | 25 |
| Confused, exhausted, frightened, nauseated | 30 |
| Blinded, paralysed, stunned | 35 |

Failure by 5 or more causes the target to become sickened for 1 round (or extends the duration of an existing sickened condition by 1 round) while failure by 10 or more causes the target to become nauseated for 1 round. This ability does not work on creatures immune to the sickened and nauseated conditions.

#### Second Curse
The Oracle must choose a second curse. One of these curses doesn't change as the oracle gains levels.

#### Additional Revelations
A Dual-Cursed Oracle gains Revelations at 5th level and 13th level in addition to the ones gained normally. In addition, he can choose two Revelations unique to the archetype.

##### Misfortune
When an enemy within 20' rolls a natural 20 on an attack roll, saving throw, combat manoeuvre check or skill check, they must reroll the die. An enemy suffering misfortune cannot be the target of this revelation again for 1 day.

##### Fortune
At 5th level, when an ally within 30' rolls a natural 1 on an attack roll, saving throw, combat manoeuvre check or skill check, they can reroll the die. An ally benefiting from fortune cannot be the target of this revelation again for 1 day.

#### Dual-Cursed Oracle Bonus Spells
At 2nd level, the Oracle learns Ill Omen. At 4th level, he learns Oracle's Burden. Finally, at 6th level, he learns Bestow Curse.

#### Sacred Scourge
At 5th level, a Purifier may channel positive energy as a cleric of the same level to harm evil outsiders. He may do this a number of times per day equal to 1 + his Charisma modifier.

#### Celestial Armor
At 7th level, a Purifier gains Armor Training as a Fighter four levels lower. At 11th level, he gains Armor Proficiency (Heavy) as a bonus feat.

#### Sin Eater
At 11th level, as a full round action, a Purifier can consume a curse, enchantment or emotion effect a number of times per day equal to his Charisma modifier. The Purifier must make a dispel check with his Charisma modifier as a bonus to remove the effect. If he succeeds, the Purifier is sickened for 1d4 rounds.

At 7th level, once per day, as a swift action, a Wind Whisperer can grant all allies within 30' the benefit of the *Haste* spell for a number of rounds equal to his Charisma modifier. He gains a second use of this ability at 13th level and a third at 19th.

#### Freedom of the Wind
At 15th level, once per day, as a swift action, a Wind Whisperer can grant all allies within 30' the benefit of the *Freedom of Movement* spell for a number of rounds equal to his Charisma modifier.

##### Blood of Heroes
As a move action, you gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls and saving throws against fear affects for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier. As a standard action, you perform a melee touch attack that causes a living creature to become shaken for a number of rounds equal to half your class level (minimum 1 round). You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.

You create a 20' radius burst within 100'. Living creatures in the area of effect take 1d8 damage per two caster levels. Undead creatures take 1d8 damage per caster level. Prerequisites:
Level 11

You gain a +4 enhancement bonus to Strength, Dexterity and Constitution and a +4 natural armour bonus to AC. Your BAB is equal to your class level and equipped weapons gain the Keen property. You can use this ability for one round per two class levels.

You gain a bonus on Will saving throws equal to your Charisma modifier, blindsense up to a range of 60' and a +4 bonus to your effective caster level for Divination spells. You can cast *Foresight* as a spell-like ability once per day.
