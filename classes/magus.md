# Magus
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

A Magus has the following characteristics:

- Primary attributes: Dexterity and Intelligence
- Medium (3/4) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast arcane spells up to 6th-level
- Spells are prepared
- 8 HP at level 1, then 5 HP per level (d8 hit die)
- High saving throws for Fortitude and Will, low saving throws for Reflex
- 2 skill points per level.

Class Skills: Athletics, Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Persuasion, Use Magic Device

#### Magus Proficiencies
A Magus is proficient with simple and martial weapons. He is also proficient with light armor and may cast magus spells while wearing it.

#### Spell Combat
If you have your off-hand free and are wielding a light or one-handed weapon in the other, you may make a full attack with a -2 penalty *and* cast a spell with a casting time of 1 standard action.

The Mechanics of Spell Combat are rather unclear. It is analogous to dual wielding but instead of making an extra attack with a weapon in your offhand, you cast a spell instead. This requires the usual Concentration check (caster level + Intelligence modifier vs. 15 + spell level x 2) to avoid provoking an attack of opportunity.

#### Arcane Pool
At 1st level, the Magus gains a reservoir of points that he can use to restore spells and power his weapon. The pool has a number of points equal to half his Magus level + his Intelligence modifier.

At 1st level, the Magus can expend one point as a swift action to confer a +1 enhancement bonus to his weapon for one minute. This increases to +2 at 5th level and every four levels thereafter to +5 at 17th level. The bonus stacks with any existing weapon bonus to a maximum of +5.

Starting from 5th level, these bonuses can be used to add weapon properties. The available properties and the enchantment cost of each one are shown in the table below.

The maximum enchantment of any weapon is +5 so you will need to spend any left over enchantment points on properties. Weapon properties are detailed in the items section.

#### Spellstrike
At 2nd level, whenever a Magus casts a spell with a range of "touch", he can deliver the spell through a melee attack with a weapon at his highest attack bonus. This is in addition to other attacks.

The following spells are the melee touch attacks that you can use with this ability:

| Lvl | Spells |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Touch of Fatigue |
| 1 | Corrosive Touch, Shocking Grasp |
| 2 | Frigid Touch |
| 3 | Force Punch, Vampiric Touch |

You can cast Touch of Fatigue until the cows come home. This means that Spellstrike gives you an extra attack at least. You can use Spellstrike with a two-handed weapon, but you don't receive the extra attack. It is still worth using if you have to move to attack an enemy.

#### Spell Recall
Starting from 4th level, a Magus may expend points from his Arcane Pool to recall a spell that he has already cast. The point cost is equivalent to the recalled spell's level.

#### Arcane Medium Armor
At 7th level, a Magus gains proficiency with medium armor and may cast magic without incurring spell failure.

#### Improved Spell Combat
At 8th level (14th level for an Eldritch Scion), when using spell combat, a Magus receives a +2 circumstance bonus on concentration checks.

#### Fighter Training
At 10th level (Sword Saint level 7), a Magus counts half his class level as fighter levels for the purposes of taking feats. This stacks with *actual* fighter levels.

#### Improved Spell Recall
Starting from 11th level, the cost of Spell Recall is half that of the spell's level (rounded up).

#### Arcane Heavy Armor
At 13th level, a Magus gains proficiency with heavy armor and may cast magic without incurring spell failure.

#### Greater Spell Combat
At 14th level (Eldritch Scion level 18), when using spell combat, a magus receives a +4 circumstance bonus on concentration checks.

#### Counterstrike
At 16th level, when an enemy within reach of the magus successfully casts a spell defensively, the enemy provokes an attack of opportunity from the magus when the spell is complete.

#### Greater Spell Access
At 19th level, a Magus learns 6 spells of levels 1-6 and adds them to his spellbook. Note that these can be taken from the full Wizard spell list rather than the restricted list that a Magus normally draws from.

#### True Magus
At 20th, whenever a Magus uses Spell combat, he does not need to make a concentration check to cast the spell defensively. He may choose either to increase the DC by 2, gain a +2 bonus to overcome spell resistance or a +2 circumstance bonus to attack rolls.

#### Magus Arcana
At 3rd level and every three subsequent levels, a Magus selects a feat from the following selection. Note that Eldritch Scions use Charisma to determine bonuses and effects, not Intelligence.

##### Arcane Accuracy
The Magus can expend one point from his arcane pool as a swift action to grant an insight bonus equal to his Intelligence bonus on all attack rolls until the end of his turn.

##### Bane Blade (Level 15)
When the Magus enhances his weapon, he may spend an additional point to add the Bane special ability to the weapon.

##### Devoted Blade (Level 12)
When the Magus enhances his weapon, he has the option of choosing anarchic, axiomatic, holy or unholy.

##### Dimension Strike (Level 9)
The magus can expend 2 points from his arcane pool as a swift action to resolve all his melee attacks as melee touch attacks.

##### Empowered Magic (Level 6)
The Magus can cast three spells per day as if those were modified by the Empower Spell feat.

##### Enduring Blade (Level 6)
When the Magus enchants his weapon, he may spend an additional point to increase the enchantment duration to one minute per class level.

##### Extended Magic
The Magus can cast three spells per day as if those were modified by the Extend Spell feat.

##### Ghost Blade (Level 9)
When the Magus enhances his weapon, he has the option of choosing brilliant energy and ghost touch.

##### Hasted Assault (Level 9)
The Magus can expend one point from his Arcane Pool to cast Haste on himself with a duration equal to his Intelligence bonus.

##### Maximized Magic (Level 12)
The Magus can cast three spells per day as if those were modified by the Maximize Spell feat.

##### Prescient Attack (Level 6)
The Magus can expend one point from his Arcane Pool to deny enemies their Dexterity bonus until the end of the Magus's next turn.

##### Quickened Magic (Level 15)
The Magus can cast three spells per day as if those were modified by the Quicken Spell feat.

##### Reach Magic
The Magus can cast three spells per day as if those were modified by the Reach Spell feat.

##### Wand Mastery
When the Magus uses a wand, he calculates the DC using his Intelligence bonus rather than the minimum modifier needed to cast the spell.

##### Wand Wielder
The magus can activate a staff or wand instead of casting a spell when using Spell Combat.

#### Bonus Magus Feat
At 5th, 11th and 17th levels, a Magus may select any feat for which he qualifies.

##### Playing a Magus
Spell Combat is the class's characteristic feature but it isn't the superpower that it appears to be. There are two reasons for this. Firstly, using it - even with *Touch of Fatigue* simply for the extra attack - risks provoking attacks of opportunity until around 8th level. Secondly, getting an extra attack requires the offhand to be free. If Strength is your main damage attribute, you get an increased damage boost from wielding a weapon with two hands. You also get additional benefit from Power Attack. This means that enabling Spellstrike may actually *reduce* your damage output, particularly if you select the Mythic Power Attack ability.

Besides Spellstrike, a Magus has a number of other useful abilities. Being able to add damaging properties to your weapon gives you a great deal of flexibility. You will want to take the *Enduring Blade* arcana if this is to be properly useful, however. You can also wear heavy armor without risking spell failure. However, you do not get any form of armor training so you will need to use *Effortless Armor* if this is to be more help than hindrance. Finally, Fighter Training allows you to take Weapon Specialization (at 11th level) and Greater Weapon Focus (at 17th) which you can boost with the relevant mythic feats.

The main problem with a Magus - which also applies to most of the archetypes - is that it is merely an OK class. 3/4 BAB and 3/4 casting means that it doesn't excel at anything. If you want to play a Fighter / Mage, you may find an Eldritch Knight better since you will likely end up with a better BAB and greatly superior spellcasting.

An Arcane Rider ends up ahead on points vs. the base class. The mount is more useful overall than Spell Recall and is something that an Eldritch Knight can't reproduce.

An Armored Battlemage solves the heavy armor problem by receiving armor training. However, *Effortless Armor* also solves that problem and the inability to enchant weapons is limiting.

A Bladebound's blade doesn't really keep up with scimitars that you can find and buy which means that it's basically a Magus with fewer feats. The ability to have the blade fight alongside you is neat, I guess, but not worth building around. The 19th level ability is decent but that's very late to get something worthwhile from the class.

An Eldritch Archer improves on the base class by not giving anything up for Spellstrike. You can leave Acid Splash on automatic and use it safely from range.

An Eldritch Scion in effect trades the Spell Recall ability for the benefits of a bloodline. The Abyssal bloodline is good since the +6 to Strength helps compensate for the class's medium AB progression. If you choose a Draconic bloodline for the AC bonuses, you can take levels in Dragon Disciple (which do not hinder bloodline progression). However, this slows acquisition of Magus features (Arcane Weapon, Arcane Armor, Magus Arcana, Fighter Training) and locks you out of Greater Spell Access. Eldritch Scion (19) / Scaled Fist (1) is a solid combination.

A Hexcrafter trades Spell Recall for hexes. The trade is neutral since *Iceplant* is at least as good as a Magus Arcana and you don't have to select any more hexes.

Like the majority of race-specific archetypes, Spell Dancer is a bit of a stinker. You give up a lot (weapon enchantment and heavier armor) and don't get nearly enough in return.

Finally, the Sword Saint is the best magus archetype by a considerable margin. Despite Diminished Spellcasting, it is likely to be a better caster than the base class because it rewards investment in Intelligence in multiple ways. You will want to stick with Sword Saint all the way since Weapon Mastery is a superpower. Earlier access to Fighter training allows you to qualify for any feat with Fighter prerequisites. Since you can't equip even light armor without burning a feat (and risking arcane failure), the early game is likely to be rough. Make sure that you select *Shield* at character creation. Sword Saint is also a respectable dip, giving you - at the very least - Weapon Focus in an exotic weapon.

#### Arcane Mount
An Arcane Rider gains a horse. Any arcane pool abilities apply to the Arcane Rider's mount as well.

#### Bonus Feats (Arcane Rider)
Besides the normal Magus bonus feats an Arcane Rider may select any mounted combat feats.

#### Dimensional Ride
At 8th level, an Arcane Rider can spend a point from his arcane pool and use his move action to transfer himself and his mount to any point within 30'.

#### Greater Dimensional Ride
At 14th level, Dimensional Ride becomes a swift action. After teleporting, the Arcane Rider and his mount become hasted for a number of rounds equal to the Rider's Intelligence modifier.

#### Black Blade
A Bladebound receives a magical +1 scimitar which he can make appear as a swift action. At 5th level and every four subsequent levels, the blade gains an additional +1 enhancement bonus (maximum +5 at 17th level).

#### Sharpening the Black Blade
From 3rd level, as a free action, a Bladebound can spend one point from his arcane pool to grant the blade +1 damage for 1 minute. For every three additional levels, the damage bonus increases by +1 (maximum +6 at 18th level).

#### Changeable Gist
From 5th level, the Bladebound can spend 1 point from his arcane pool to change the blade's damage type to cold, electricity or fire. By spending 2 points, he can change the damage type to sonic or force. This effect lasts until the start of the Bladebound's next turn.

#### Independent Ego
From 12th level, as a free action, the Bladebound can release the grasp on his blade allowing it to fight independently for 1 minute. The weapon gains a damage bonus as through the Bladebound had used his Sharpening... ability and has the following characteristics:

- Ability scores are equal to the Bladebound's Intelligence score
- BAB is identical to the wielder's and it gains a Deflection bonus to AC equal to the wielder's BAB
- HP is 2 x Intelligence modifier x class level
- Traits of a Construct.

#### Lifedrinker
From 19th level if the Bladebound kills a creature with 10 or more HD while wielding the blade (or the blade kills a creature by itself), he will gain temporary HP equal to his class level + 3. If the Bladebound already has temporary HP gained from the blade, he will restore 1 point of his Arcane Pool instead.

#### Ranged Spell Combat
An Eldritch Archer must use a ranged weapon for spell combat. He doesn't need a hand free but cannot accept an attack penalty to gain a concentration bonus either.

#### Ranged Spellstrike
At 2nd level whenever an Eldritch Archer casts a spell that calls for a ranged touch attack, he can deliver the spell through a ranged weapon as a free attack at full attack bonus. This does not extend the range of the spell, however. If the spell has multiple rays (Scorching Ray at 7th level, for example), only one ray will be sent through the ranged attack, not multiple rays. However, he may imbue her other ranged attacks with the leftover rays.

You can also use Reach Spell metamagic to move melee touch attack spells up a range category, allowing them to be used with Ranged Spellstrike.

#### Spells (Hexcrafter)

#### Spell Dance
At 1st level, a Spell Dancer can expend 1 point form his Arcane Pool as a swift action to gain a +10' enhancement bonus to his movement rate and a +2 dodge bonus to AC against attacks of opportunity for 1 minute. Each four subsequent levels, the bonuses increase by +10' / +2.

At 5th level, once per spell dance as a swift action, the Spell Dancer can grant himself the benefits of *Blur* or *Haste* for 1 round. At 9th level, once per spell dance as a swift action, the Spell Dancer can instead use *Dimension Door* as a spell-like ability. Finally, at 13th level, a Spell Dancer may instead grant himself the benefits of *Freedom of Movement* for 1d4 rounds.

#### Arcane Movement
At 5th level, when a Spell Dancer casts a spell, he gains a competence bonus to Mobility and Stealth checks equal to the spell's level until the beginning of his next turn.

#### Dance of Avoidance
At 7th level, while wearing armor or no armor, a Spell Dancer gains a +2 insight bonus to AC.

#### Greater Dance of Avoidance
At 13th level, while wearing armor or no armor, a Spell Dancer gains a +4 insight bonus to AC.

#### Diminished Spellcasting
The Sword Saint may cast one fewer spell of each level than a Magus. If this reduces the number of spells to 0, he may only select a spell of that level if he has sufficient Intelligence to gain a bonus spell.

#### Sword Saint Proficiencies
A Sword Saint is proficient only with simple weapons and his chosen weapon. He is not proficient with armor and suffers arcane spell failure when wearing armor.

#### Chosen Weapon
At 1st level, a sword saint gains Weapon Focus with his chosen weapon as a bonus feat.

#### Canny Defense
When wearing light or no armor, a Sword Saint adds 1 point of Intelligence bonus per class level as a dodge bonus to his armor class.

#### Perfect Strike
At 4th level, the Sword Saint can expend one point from his Arcane Pool to maximize the damage inflicted by his chosen weapon. Alternatively, when he confirms a critical hit, he can expend two points to increase his weapon's critical multiplier by 1.

#### Lightning Draw
At 7th level, the Sword Saint adds his Intelligence bonus to initiative rolls as well as his Dexterity modifier.

#### Fighter Training (Sword Saint)
At 7th level, the Sword Saint counts his class level -3 as fighter levels for the purposes of taking feats. This stacks with *actual* fighter levels.

#### Critical Perfection
At 9th level, a Sword Saint adds his Intelligence bonus to critical hit confirmation rolls. He may use his Magus level in place of base attack bonuses to qualify for any feat for which it is a prerequisite.

#### Superior Reflexes
At 11th level, a Sword Saint can make a number of attacks of opportunity in a round equal to his Intelligence modifier. This stacks with the Combat Reflexes feat.

#### Lethal Focus
At 13th level, when a Sword Saint is attacking a flat-footed opponent, he adds his Intelligence modifier to damage with his chosen weapon.

#### Perfect Reflexes
At 19th level, a Sword Saint's initiative roll is automatically a natural 20.

#### Weapon Mastery
At 20th level, any attacks a Sword Saint makes with his chosen weapon automatically confirm all critical threats and have their damage multiplier increased by 1.
