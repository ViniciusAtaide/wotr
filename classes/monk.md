# Monk
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

A Monk has the following characteristics:

- Primary attributes: Dexterity, Constitution and Wisdom
- Full AB progression
- 10 HP at level 1, then 6 HP per level (d10 hit die)
- High saving throws for Fortitude and Reflex, low saving throws for Will
- 3 skill points per level
- Alignment must be Lawful.

Class Skills: Athletics, Mobility, Stealth, Knowledge (World), Lore (Religion), Perception, Persuasion

#### AC Bonus
When wearing no armour and shield and carrying a light load, the monk adds her Wisdom bonus to her AC and CMD. The Monk gains a +1 bonus to AC and CMD at 4th level and this increases by 1 for every 4 class levels (maximum +5 at 20th level).

#### Unarmed Strike
A Medium-sized monk's unarmed attack damage increases with level:

| Lvl | Dmg |
| --- | --- |
| 1-3 | 1d6 |
| 4-7 | 1d8 |
| 8-11 | 1d10 |
| 12-15 | 2d6 |
| 16-19 | 2d8 |
| 20 | 2d10 |

#### Monk Proficiencies
Monks are proficient with the following weapons: club, crossbow, dagger, handaxe, javelin, kama, nunchaku, quarterstaff, sai, short sword, shortspear, spear and any weapon with the *monk* special weapon quality.

#### Monk Bonus Feats
You may select from Blind Fight, Combat Reflexes, Crane Style, Deflect Arrows, Dodge and Improved Initiative. At 6th level, you may also select Trip and Disarm. At 10th level, you may further select Improved Critical and Improved Blind Fight.

#### Stunning Fist
If you choose to use Stunning Fist and your attack connects, your opponent must make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + half class level + Wisdom modifier) or be stunned for 1 round. You may make one Stunning Fist attack per day for every four class levels. Creatures immune to critical hits cannot be stunned and neither can constructs, oozes, plants, undead or incorporeal creatures.

##### Stunning Fist: Fatigue
From 4th level you may choose to make an attack that leaves the target fatigued for 1 minute rather than stunned for 1 round.

##### Stunning Fist: Sicken
From 8th level you may choose to make an attack that leaves the target sickened for 1 minute rather than stunned for 1 round.

#### Flurry of Blows
When unarmed or armed with a monk weapon, the Monk can make an additional attack at full BAB. This stacks with Haste and similar effects. At 11th level, she can make an additional flurry attack (for a total of two extra attacks).

#### Evasion
At 2nd level, if the Monk makes a Reflex save against an attack that normally deals half damage on a successful save, she instead takes no damage.

#### Fast Movement
At 3rd level, the Monk gains a +10' boost to her base speed. For every 3 levels above 3rd, the bonus improves by 10'.

#### Ki Pool
At 3rd level, the Monk gains a pool of ki points equal to half her class level + Wisdom modifier. While she has at least one point in her ki-pool, her unarmed attacks are can overcome certain types of damage resistance.

##### Ki Power - Extra Attack
When using Flurry of Blows, a Monk may use a swift action and a point from her Ki Pool to make an additional attack. This attack stacks with Flurry of Blows and Haste.

##### Ki Strike - Magic
At 3rd level, the Monk's unarmed attacks are considered *magic* for the purposes of damage reduction.

##### Ki Strike - Cold Iron and Silver
At 7th level, the Monk's unarmed attacks are considered *cold iron* and *silver* for the purposes of damage reduction.

##### Ki Strike - Lawful
At 10th level, the Monk's unarmed attacks are considered *lawful* for the purposes of damage reduction.

##### Ki Strike - Adamantine
At 16th level, the Monk's unarmed attacks are considered *adamantine* for the purposes of damage reduction.

#### Still Mind
At 4th level, the Monk gains a +2 bonus on saving throws against enchantment spells and effects.

#### Ki Power
At 4th level and every 2 levels thereafter a Monk may select a ki power which she can execute by expending points from her ki pool.

#### Purity of Body
At 5th level, a Monk becomes immune to all diseases.

#### Style Strike
At 5th level, when the Monk makes a Flurry of Blows unarmed attack, she may designate one of her unarmed strikes as a style strike giving it additional effects:

- **Defensive Spin**
If the attack hits, the Monk gains a +4 dodge AC bonus against attacks made by the target until the start of his next turn.
- **Hammerblow**
The Monk rolls damage dice twice (before applying Strength and other modifiers).
- **Leg Sweep**
The Monk makes a free trip attempt (using the BAB of the attack).
- **Shattering Punch**
A successful attack bypasses damage reduction or hardness.

She gains additional style strikes every four levels.

#### Improved Evasion
At 9th level, if the Monk fails a Reflex save against an attack that normally deals half damage on a successful save, she takes only half damage.

#### Ki Power: Perfect Self
At 20th level, the monk gains DR 10 / Chaotic.

##### Playing a Monk
A problem with playing a Monk is that all varieties are badly front-loaded: after a burst of power, their offensive growth is slow and steady unlike, say, Sorcerers and Alchemists whose growth is better than linear. If you're going to play a Monk, you may be disappointed if you expect her to lay waste to her enemies. Fortunately, Wisdom substitutes for Dexterity fairly effectively meaning that you can focus on Strength and Wisdom.

The base class, Scaled Fist and Traditional Monk play more or less identically: you adopt your preferred fighting style, punch stuff and avoid enemy attacks. The unarmed attacks of a Monk scale with character level but the wretched critical parameters (x2 on a 20) limit the damage you can put out. Adding attack and damage bonuses requires an amulet which means that you can't use the neck slot for anything else. Note that even though your character clearly has two fists, unarmed combat does not benefit at all from two-weapon fighting. Instead, you can use Flurry of Blows for additional attacks and these bonus attacks are made at full BAB.

The front-loading means that Monk varieties are the dip class par-excellence. A single level gives you many of the benefits (AC bonus and, say, Crane Style) with little cost. Characters for whom Charisma is a primary attribute will want to take a level of Scaled Fist while those for whom Wisdom is more important will want to take a level of Traditional Monk (this is better than the base class because all saving throws are high). A second Monk level is also good because you get another bonus feat out of it.

A Drunken Master is similar to the base class - you punch things - but superior in every way because of the bonuses, resistances and immunities that they get from having ki points. Many of these are untyped or alchemical meaning they stack with everything else. Replenishing the ki pool is just a matter of drinking alcohol which you find everywhere and which you can buy in large quantities in case you start running short. The capstone is pretty decent as well.

A Quarterstaff Master is better than the base class in most respects because a two-handed weapon benefits more from Strength bonuses and Power Attack. Combat manoeuvres are a poor use of a standard action, but having them added to your attacks for free is fantastic. A quarterstaff is another weapon with bad critical parameters so the damage isn't top tier. However, a Quarterstaff Master puts out damage comparable to a Two-Handed Fighter (or at least one armed with a quarterstaff) and Quarterstaff Master (12) / Two-Handed Fighter (8) is a decent combination (but be aware that most quarterstaffs in the game are for spellcasters).

A Sensei is not a good choice for the main character - you want to be punching fools, not making the rest of the party a little bit better. However, it is a good mercenary class; the ability to cast True Strike, Barkskin and Restoration on the entire party is very useful.

A Sohei gives up very little for quite a lot: an animal companion, good weapon proficiencies, an initiative bonus and weapon training. Being able to use (Greater) Flurry with, say, a fauchard is incredible. However, until receive your first rank of weapon training, you can only use Flurry with quarterstaffs since you don't get proficiency in other monk weapons. Note, also, that although a Sohei is proficient with light armour, wearing any armour cancels the Wisdom AC bonus.

Student of Stone is a race-specific archetype and not a very good one. You give up Fast Movement and (Improved) Evasion for ... not enough. Strength of Stone is decent because it basically gives you an untyped +1 bonus to attack and damage. But that's it. The designers clearly want you to explore the new unarmed feats with this archetype but Elemental Fist and Shaitan Style are awful.

Zen Archer is quite a stacked class: improved damage dice with bows, Flurry with bows and a bunch of bonus archery feats. It's not as MAD as other Monk archetypes since you only need Dexterity OR Wisdom rather than Dexterity AND Wisdom. The reason is that both attributes boost the same things: AB and AC. You'll also need decent Strength to prevent your damage output from being pathetic. Note that Flurry of Blows does not stack with Rapid Shot / Manyshot. However, since Flurry gives you bonus attacks at Full AB rather than at a penalty, it is much better. Finally, be aware that Zen Archer is not the most damaging ranged class. That honour goes to a Slayer specialised in Archery. On the flip side, Zen Archer is an incredible three or four level dip for a ranged character

#### Drunken Ki
From 3rd level, a Drunken Master gains a pool of supernatural energy which is replenished by drinking alcohol. The size of the pool is two points + 1 for each additional odd-numbered level (maximum 10 points at 19th level). As a standard action, she can consume booze to gain 1 drunken ki point. At 8th level, this becomes a swift action.

#### Drunken Combat
For every two points in her Drunken Ki pool, a Drunken Master gains a +1 alchemical bonus to attack rolls and AC but suffers a -1 penalty to Intelligence. Her movement no longer provokes attacks of opportunity.

#### Drunken Technique: Firewater Breath
From 4th level, as a standard action, a Drunken Master can spend 2 ki points to breathe fire in a 15' cone, dealing 1d6 fire damage per class level, with a Reflex save for half damage (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + WIS modifier).

#### Drunken Power
From 5th level, as a swift action, a Drunken Master can spend 1 ki point to make her melee attacks deal +1d8 damage for a number of rounds equal to her WIS modifier. The damage bonus increases by +1d8 every 3 additional levels (max +5d8 at 18th level).

#### Drunken Technique: Cayden's Trick
From 6th level, as a standard action, a Drunken Master can spend 2 ki points to bring up her stomach contents in a 15' cone. Creatures in the area of effect must make a Reflex save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + WIS modifier) or suffer a -2 penalty to AC and become blinded, flat-footed and sickened for 2-5 rounds. In addition, the area becomes slick, equivalent to the *Grease* spell.

#### Drunken Courage
From 7th level, a Drunken Master becomes immune to fear so long as she has at least one ki point. From 11th level, she also gains a +4 alchemical bonus to saves against mind-affecting and compulsion effects.

#### Drunken Resilience
From 9th level, a Drunken Master gains DR 2/- and a +4 alchemical bonus to saving throws vs. poison so long as she has at least one ki point. At 15th level, the DR increases to DR 5/- and at 19th level, it becomes DR 10/-.

#### Eternal Drunkenness
At 20th level, a Drunken Master gains a +6 alchemical bonus to Strength, Dexterity and Constitution. In addition, the capacity of her ki pool increases by +6.

At 4th level, a quarterstaff deals damage as if ir were one size category larger (1d8). At 12th level, it deals damage as if it were two size categories larger (2d6). Finally, at 20th level, it deals damage as if it were three sizes larger (3d6).

#### Advice
This gives the Sensei the same capabilities as Bardic Performance and benefits from the Lingering Performance feat. The Sensei can use this ability for a number of rounds per day equal to her character level + her Wisdom modifier.

#### Inspire Courage
This is identical to the Bard's ability.

#### Insightful Strike
At 2nd level, a Sensei may use her Wisdom bonus in lieu of her Strength or Dexterity on attack rolls and combat manoeuvre checks with unarmed strikes and monk weapons.

#### Inspire Competence
Gained at 3rd level, this is identical to the Bard's ability.

#### Mystic Powers
At 6th level, the Sensei can use Advice when spending points from her ki pool to grant the effect (Barkskin, for example) to an ally within 30'. At 10th level, she may do the same for all allies within 30' without having to expend extra ki points. This ability affects the following powers:

- Barkskin
- Diamond Body
- Diamond Soul
- Extra Attack
- Restoration
- Sudden Speed
- True Strike
- Wholeness of Body.

#### Inspire Greatness
Gained at 9th level, this is identical to the Bard's ability.

#### Mystic Wisdom
At 10th level, the Sensei may expend one point from her ki pool to provide a single ally within 30' Evasion, Fast Movement or Purity of Soul. At 14th level, she may expend 2 points to grant an ability to all allies within 30' or else provide Improved Evasion to a single ally. The imparted abilities function at the Sensei's class level and last for 1 minute.

#### Sohei Proficiencies
A Sohei is proficient with simple and martial weapons and light armour. From 3rd level, she may spend 1 point from her Ki Pool to grant her mount temporary HP equal to twice her class level for one hour per level.

#### Skills
A Sohei gains Lore (Nature) as a class skill.

#### Monastic Mount
A Sohei gains a horse animal companion.

#### Devoted Guardian
A Sohei receives a bonus to initiative rolls equal to half her class level. At 20th level, her initiative roll is an automatic 20.

#### Flurry of Blows (Sohei)
A Sohei can use Flurry of Blows with quarterstaffs and weapons that she is trained in.

A Sohei can use Flurry of Blows while wearing light armour but only with trained weapons, not with quarterstaffs.

#### Bonus Feat (Sohei)
Besides the normal Monk bonus feats a Sohei may select any mounted combat feats.

#### Ki Strike (Sohei)
A Sohei can use Ki Strike with weapons that she is trained in.

#### Ki Weapon
At 4th level, as a swift action, a Sohei may spend 1 point from her ki pool to grant her weapon a +1 enhancement bonus to attack and damage rolls until the end of her next turn. This bonus increases by +1 for every four additional levels (max +5 at 20th level).

#### Weapon Training
At 6th level, a Sohei can take weapon training in one of the following groups: bows, crossbows, monk weapons, polearms, spears or thrown weapons. She receives additional training at 12th level and 18th level.

#### Bonus Feat (Student of Stone)
At 6th level, a Student of Stone may select Elemental Fist (acid) as a bonus feat. At 10th level, she can select Shaitan Style. At 14th level, she can select Shaitan Skin. Finally, at 18th level, she can select Shaitan Earthblast.

#### Hard as Stone
At 2nd level, a Student of Stone receives a +4 AC bonus vs. critical hit confirmation rolls.

#### Strength of Stone
At 3rd level, when touching the ground, a Student of Stone gains a +1 bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, Bill Rush and Trip rolls, and CMD when resisting a bull rush or trip event.

#### Bones of Stone
At 7th level, as a swift action, a Student of Stone can spend 1 ki point to gain DR 2/magic until the end of her next turn. At 10th level, this becomes DR 2/chaotic. Finally, at 15th level, this becomes DR 5/chaotic.

#### Body of Stone
At 9th level, a Student of Stone has a 25% chance of ignoring critical hits.

#### Soul of Stone
At 12th level, as a swift action, a Student of Stone can spend 1 ki point to gain tremorsense to 15' until the end of her next turn. At 16th level, the range of this ability increases to 30'.

#### Stone Self
At 20th level, a Student of Stone becomes an outsider with the earth subtype. She gains tremorsense to 20'.

#### Zen Archer Proficiencies
A Zen Archer is proficient with all bows in addition to a Monk's normal proficiencies.

#### Bonus Feat (Zen Archer)
You may select from Combat Reflexes, Crane Style, Deflect Arrows, Dodge, Point Blank Shot, Precise Shot and Rapid Shot. At 6th level, you may also select Combat Mobility, Improved Precise Shot and Manyshot. At 10th level, you may further select Improved Critical and Clustered Shots.

#### Flurry of Blows (Zen Archer)
A Zen Archer may only use Flurry of Blows when wielding a bow. She cannot use Rapid Shot or Manyshot at the same time as Flurry of Blows.

#### Way of the Bow
At 2nd level, a Zen Archer gains Weapon Focus with Shortbows or Longbows as a bonus feat. At 6th level, she gains Weapon Specialization with the same weapon type.

#### Zen Archery
At 3rd level, a Zen Archer can use her Wisdom modifier in place of her Dexterity modifier when using a bow.

#### Ki Arrows
At 5th level, a Zen Archer deals her unarmed damage with arrows.

##### Sudden Speed
Increase base speed by 30' for 1 minute.

##### Abundant Step
The Monk may slip between spaces like Dimension Door but cannot take others with her.

##### Wholeness of Body
The Monk may heal a number of HP equal to her class level.

##### Diamond Body
The Monk becomes immune to poison.

##### Diamond Soul
The Monk gains permanent Spell Resistance equal to class level + 10.

##### Shout
The target takes 1d6 sonic damage per class level and is stunned for 1 round. A successful Fortitude save halves damage and negates the stun.

##### Quivering Palm
The target must make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + half class level + Wisdom modifier) or die.
