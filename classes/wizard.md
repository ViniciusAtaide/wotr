# Wizard
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Wizard has the following characteristics:

- Primary attribute: Intelligence
- Slow (1/2) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast arcane spells up to 9th-level
- Spells must be prepared in advance
- 5 HP per level
- High saving throws for Will, low saving throws for Fortitude and Reflex
- 2 skill points per level.

Class Skills: Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Lore (Religion)

On each level up, the Wizard may select an additional two spells to add to his spellbook. Additional spells may be added by copying them to your spellbook from a scroll in your inventory.

All Wizards must select an Arcane bond on character creation, either a bonded object or a familiar.

They may choose a Specialist school, such as Evocation, or they may choose not to specialise. If they choose a specialist school, they gain an extra spell slot per spell level (which must contain a spell of the specialist school). They must also choose two opposition schools from which any selected spells occupy two slots instead of 1.

#### Wizard Proficiencies
Wizards are proficient with the club, dagger, heavy crossbow, light crossbow and quarterstaff.

#### Bonus Feats
At 1st, 5th, 10th, 15th and 20th levels, a wizard gains a bonus magic feat.

##### Hand of the Apprentice
You can attack with your melee weapon at a range of 30' as a standard action using your Intelligence as your attack modifier. You may use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Intelligence modifier.

##### Metamagic Mastery
Starting at 8th level, you are able to apply metamagic to the next spell you cast. You can do this once per day at 8th level +1 for each two class levels to maximum of 7 times at 20th level. Initially you can only apply Extend and Reach spell which costs 1 usage of Metamagic Mastery. As you progress in levels, you can apply other metamagic, as follows:

- 12: Empower at a cost of 2 usages
- 16: Maximise at a cost of 3 usages
- 20: Quicken at a cost of 4 usages.

##### Resistance
When you prepare spells, choose an energy type and gain resistance 5. At 11th level, this resistance increases to 10. At 20th level, you become immune.

##### Protective Ward
As a standard action, create a 10' radius field of protective magic centred on yourself. All allies receive a +1 deflection bonus to AC, increasing by +1 for every five class levels. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Intelligence modifier.

##### Energy Absorption
At 6th level, you gain an amount of energy absorption equal to 3x your class level per day.

At 8th level, you can teleport up 30' as a move action. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to your class level.

Add half your class level to initiative checks. At 20th level, all initiative rolls are a natural 20.

At 8th level, you can create a 30' aura for a number of rounds per day equal to your class level. At the start of a round, you may choose to help your allies (granting a +2 luck bonus to attack rolls, caster level checks, skill checks, ability checks and saving throws) or hinder your enemies (inflicting -2 penalties instead).

##### Intense Spells
When you cast an Evocation spell that inflicts HP damage, add half your class level to the damage inflicted. This applies once, not per die, and is not amplified by Empower Spell. At 20th level when casting an Evocation spell, you can roll twice to overcome Spell Resistance.

##### Elemental Wall
At 8th level you can create a wall of energy that behaves like Wall of Fire but which does selectable energy damage. You can use this ability twice at 12th level, three times at 16th and four times at 20th.

##### Power Over Undead
You are able to turn undead. You may do so a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Intelligence modifier. The DC for this affect is 10 + half class level + Charisma modifier. At 20th level, undead may not use their channel resistance.

##### Grave Touch
Cause a creature to become shaken for a number of rounds equal to half your class level round as a melee touch attack. If you touch a shaken creature, it becomes frightened for 1 round if has fewer HD than your wizard level. The ability can be used a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Intelligence modifier.

##### Life Sight
At 8th level you gain blindsight to 10' for a number of rounds equal to your class level. You can see living creatures and undead and distinguish between them.

Gain a +1 enhancement bonus to the physical attribute of your choosing. You may change this when you memorise spells. The bonus increases by +1 for every 5 wizard levels (+5 at 20th level). At level 20, you may also boost two attributes.

##### Playing a Wizard
A Wizard is more bothersome to play than a Sorcerer because you have to anticipate what spells you might need ahead of time. However, the flexibility is to their benefit since you don't have to worry about spells becoming obsolete - when Sleep is no longer effective, simply don't select it. The benefits of specialisation outweigh the penalties, although you need to choose your specialisation wisely. Although Invokers look good, I prefer to use magic to take enemies out of the fight altogether rather than damage them en-masse. The Diviner has an indifferent spell selection (you will need metamagic to fill all the slots) but the class abilities are some of the best of any class.

The Arcane Bomber has something left in the tank when he runs out of spells, but if you want to chuck bombs, a Grenadier does it so much better. Four opposition schools sounds like a pain to manage as well.

A Cruromancer pays a very high cost for a modest boost to his spellcasting abilities. If you like the idea, an Undead Bloodline Sorcerer might suit you better.

If you prefer to blow enemies away rather than take them out, an Elemental Specialist is an Invoker+1. Three opposition schools are a pain but you're getting something in return. If you could choose Sonic as a focused element, the class would be top tier. As it is, it's OK.

An Exploiter Wizard gives up the benefits of specialisation for the dubious benefits of exploits. Potent Magic is very decent on a Wizard because you have a much greater variety of spells than an Arcanist. Whether it is as good as Foretell is another question.

The Scroll Savant loses a feat but can cast spells more effectively from scrolls. Combined with UMD, it allows him to cast Divine Magic as though he were a Cleric of the same level.

The primary attraction of a Shadowcaster is the profane bonus to Intelligence which will stack with other bonuses and which allows you to make good use of starting Intelligence 18 or 20. The capstone ability is decent but can only be used for a few rounds per day. On the other hand, the special summons is likely to end up attacking you and losing four feats and a familiar is hurtful.

The Spell Master is forced into the substandard Universalist non-specialisation and loses two feats into the bargain. The rewards for doing so are not worth the cost.

Finally, the Thassilonian Specialist has to deal with Forbidden Schools. If you can manage this, you get as many spells a Sorcerer. That said, the Abundant Spells mythic abilities makes the extra slot rather less valuable.

#### Arcane Bombs
An Arcane Bomber chooses one type of energy (acid, cold, fire, electricity) to infuse his bombs. She can use a number of bombs each day equal to his class level + Intelligence modifier.

On a direct hit, a bomb inflicts 1d6 damage of the selected energy type + additional damage equal to his Intelligence modifier. Bomb damage increases by 1d6 every odd-numbered level. Splash damage is equal to the bomb's minimal damage. A Reflex save (DC 10 + class level / 2 + Intelligence modifier) halves the splash damage.

#### Spellblast Bombs
As a swift action, sacrifice a spell to power up a bomb. You gain an attack bonus equal to the spell level and a damage bonus equal to twice the spell level.

#### Focused Element
At 1st level, an Elemental Specialist selects one element (from Acid, Cold, Electricity and Fire) and gains the ability to convert elemental spell damage to his chosen element.

At 15th level, spells of the chosen element deal half of their damage as untyped damage.

#### Arcane Reservoir
The Exploiter Wizard has a reservoir of magical energy. Its maximum capacity is her class level +3 and when he rests, the reservoir is partially filled to half his class level +3. When casting a spell, the Wizard may spend a point from his reservoir as a free action to increase either the effective caster level or the DC of the spell by +1.

#### Exploiter Exploits
At 1st level and every four levels thereafter, an Exploiter Wizard may choose an Arcanist Exploit. From 13th level, he can choose from the list of Greater Exploits.

#### Scroll Focus
A Scroll Savant adds half his class level as a bonus on Use Magic Device checks. He will always take 10 on UMD checks if 10 is sufficient for a successful skill check.

#### Scroll Specialization
At 5th level, when the Scroll Savant uses a scroll, he calculates the DC for any spell using his own Intelligence modifier rather than the minimum modifier needed to cast a spell of that level.

#### Scroll Mastery
At 10th level, a scroll savant can use his own caster level instead of the item's caster level when using a scroll.

#### Shadowsight
At 5th level, a Shadowcaster gains blindsight with a range of 30'.

#### Summon Shadow
From 7th level, once per day as a standard action, a Shadowcaster can summon a Shadow for a number of minutes equal to his caster level. The Shadow gains a +2 profane bonus to Dexterity, Charisma, AC, attack rolls, Reflex saves and Will saves. These bonuses increase at higher levels.

Each turn, the Shadow can attempt a Will save (DC 18) to break free of the Shadowcaster's control, becoming hostile.

#### Umbral Mind
At 10th level, the Shadowcaster receives a +1 profane bonus to Intelligence. This increases by an additional +1 every two class levels (maximum +5 at 18th level).

#### Shadowform
At 20th level, as a standard action, a Shadowcaster can assume the form of a shadow for a number of rounds equal to half his class level. While in this form, he is treated as both incorporeal and undead and is immune to ground-based effects. He receives a +2 bonus to Intelligence, a +30' bonus to movement speed and gains a touch attack which drains 2d8 points of Strength. He also receives the benefits of the *Transformation* spell but retains the ability to cast spells.
