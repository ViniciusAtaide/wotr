# Inquisitor
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Inquisitor has the following characteristics:

- Primary attribute: WIsdom
- Medium (3/4) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast divine spells up to 6th-level
- Spells are cast spontaneously
- Must choose a domain
- Does not receive bonus domain spells
- 8 HP at level 1, then 5 HP per level (d8 hit die)
- High saving throws for Fortitude and Will, low saving throws for Reflex
- 4 skill points per level.

Class Skills: All

#### Inquisitor Proficiencies
An Inquisitor is proficient with all simple weapons, longbow, shortbow and the favored weapon of his deity. He is also proficient with light armor, medium armor and shields.

#### Stern Gaze
An Inquisitor receives a morale bonus on all Perception and Persuasion (intimidation) checks equal to half his class level.

#### Judgment
The Inquisitor can pronounce judgment on his foes as a swift action, granting a bonus or special ability based on the type of judgment made which lasts until the end of combat. At 1st level, he can pronounce judgment once per day, rising to twice at 4th level, three times at 7th level, up to a maximum of seven times at 19th level.

These are the judgments that may be pronounced:

##### Judgment - Destruction
+1 sacred bonus on all damage rolls. This increases by +1 for every three class levels.

##### Judgment - Healing
The Inquisitor gains Fast Healing 1. The amount of healing increases by +1 for every three class levels.

##### Judgment - Justice
+1 sacred bonus on all attack rolls. This increases by +1 for every five class levels. At 10th level, this bonus is doubled on all attack rolls to confirm critical hits.

##### Judgment - Piercing
+1 sacred bonus on all concentration checks and caster level checks to overcome spell resistance. This increases by +1 for every three class levels.

##### Judgment - Protection
+1 sacred bonus to AC. This increases by +1 for every five class levels. At 10th level, this bonus is doubled on all attack rolls to confirm critical hits against the Inquisitor.

##### Judgment - Purity
+1 sacred bonus on all saving throws. This increases by +1 for every five class levels. At 10th level, this bonus is doubled against curses, diseases and poisons.

##### Judgement - Resistance
The Inquisitor gains 2 points of energy resistance against all elements. This increases by +2 for every three class levels.

##### Judgement - Resiliency
The Inquisitor gains DR 1/magic. This increases by +1 for every five class levels. At 10th level, it changes from magic to an alignment opposite the Inquisitor's.

##### Judgment - Smiting
The Inquisitor's weapons count as magic for the purpose of overcoming damage resistance. At 6th level, the Inquisitor's weapons also count as one alignment type matching the Inquisitor's alignment. At 10th level, the Inquisitor's weapons count as adamantine.

#### Cunning Initiative
At 2nd level, the Inquisitor adds her Wisdom modifier + Dexterity modifier to initiative checks.

#### Teamwork Feats
At 3rd level and every three levels thereafter, the Inquisitor may select a Teamwork Feat.

#### Solo Tactics
At 3rd level, the Inquisitor receives the benefits of any teamwork feats he has so long as his allies' positioning and actions meet the prerequisites listed in the feat description. The allies do not need to have the feats themselves and if they don't will not themselves benefit from the effects.

#### Bane
At 5th level, the Inquisitor can imbue his weapon with the Bane special ability as a swift action for a total number of rounds equal to his class level. Against a designated foe, the weapon's enhancement bonus is +2 better than its actual bonus and it deals +2d6 additional damage per hit.

#### Second Judgment
At 8th level, the Inquisitor can pronounce two judgments when he uses his ability. This only consumes one use.

#### Stalwart
At 11th level, if the character makes a Fortitude or Will save against an attack that normally deals half damage on a successful save, he instead takes no damage. This ability cannot be used if the Inquisitor is wearing heavy armor.

#### Greater Bane
At 12th level the bonus damage per hit increases to 4d6.

#### Exploit Weakness
At 14th level, when an Inquisitor confirms a critical hit, damage reduction is bypassed and any regeneration capability that the target has is interrupted for 1 round.

#### Third Judgment
At 16th level, the Inquisitor can pronounce three judgments when he uses his ability. This only consumes one use.

#### True Judgment
At 20th level when the Inquisitor uses his judgment ability, he can pronounce True Judgment on a foe as a swift action. He makes an attack which deals normal damage if it hits. In addition, the target must make a Fortitude save (DC 20 + Wisdom modifier) or die. If the target makes its save, it becomes immune to the effect.

##### Playing an Inquisitor
The base Inquisitor is one of the weaker class choices, combining 3/4 BAB with indifferent proficiencies and an indifferent spell book. Judgments are good because they provide sacred (or profane) bonuses which generally stack with other sources of attack and damage bonuses and the Everlasting Judgment mythic ability lifts the Inquisitor above the bottom shelf of class choices.

If you're going to take advantage of the free teamwork feats, you're going to be on the front line. Judgments, Divine Favor, Shield of Faith, Heroism, etc. can help you be effective and stay alive since the need for 16 Wisdom requires you to compromise physical attributes.

Good domains for the Inquisitor are Animal, Community, Liberation, Luck, Nobility, Protection and Travel. If you take Animal Domain, you'll want Boon Companion at level 5. Note that an Inquisitor does not gain spells through his domain choice.

A Faith Hunter gives up the domain for not very much: a handful of uses of a cut-down Smite ability isn't a good trade. A Judge gives up even more: all teamwork feats and the Bane ability for a cut-down Mark of Justice. Note that Everlasting Judgment doesn't give you unlimited use of Aura of Judgment.

A Living Grimoire loses the signature abilities of the base class and trades them for a weapon which will almost certainly be outclassed by weapons that he might otherwise equip. There are interesting synergies between spellcasting and attacking enemies with the book, but you're likely to be using an Inquisitor as an out-of-combat buffer rather than a DC caster. Overall, Living Grimoire feels like a joke class and only something that you'd play for flavor.

A Monster Tactician plays differently - you'll basically be throwing disposable allies at your enemies. The number of times you can summon is a function of your Wisdom score so you want this to be maximized. Since Everlasting Judgment is a thing, losing the Judgment ability altogether is quite a blow. In addition to this, the Beast Tamer (Bard) has the same summoning capabilities but a better base class.

A Sacred Huntsmaster fights on the frontline alongside a powered-up pet. This frees you to take a domain other than Animal without feeling like you're missing out and means that you don't have to take Boon Companion either. You're trading Judgment for Favored Enemy which is an unfavorable trade. This class is a good three level dip for a companion-focused build.

A Sanctified Slayer is an improvement on the base class. You trade Judgment for Studied Target and (eventually) +6d6 sneak damage. Studied Target gives you the same attack bonus and damage bonus as Judgment and the sneak attack gives you a way to apply it without consuming an action since a Sanctified Slayer never gets the swift action upgrade for Studied Target. You also get four slayer talents. Unfortunately, these are way too late to be really useful and you are probably better off simply playing a Slayer.

Finally, Tactical Leader is quite a bad choice. Being able to grant teamwork feats to allies is a weak ability anyway and does not compensate for the loss of three teamwork feats in the first place.

All Inquisitor classes fizzle out at higher levels and grant very little after, say, class level 17 or 18. You will probably want to spend the remaining levels elsewhere.

#### Sworn Enemy
A Faith Hunter chooses an alignment axis opposed to the alignment of his deity - Chaotic, Evil, Good or Lawful - as a particular one to be hated.

For example, a deity choice of Erastil allows you to choose Chaotic or Evil while a choice of Gozreh allows you to choose any axis.

#### Sworn Enemy - Smite
At 6th level, once per day as a swift action, a Faith Hunter can smite a creature of his hated alignment, gaining a +4 bonus to attack rolls and a damage bonus equal to half his class level. The bonuses last until the enemy is dead. At 10th level and every four subsequent evils, the Faith Hunter gains an additional daily use of this ability (max 4 at 18th level).

#### Judgment Aura
At 3rd level, a Judge can share his judgments with all allies within 30' as a swift action. The bonuses last until the end of combat. You can use this ability once per day plus one additional time every six levels.

#### Sentence
At 5th level, once per day as a swift action, a Judge can sentence an enemy. All the party's attacks against sentenced enemy do an additional +1 damage per three class levels. You receive another use of this ability every five levels.

#### Greater Sentence
At 12th level, passing Sentence allows your party's attacks to ignore concealment.

#### Spellbook
A Living Grimoire receives the same number of spell slots per day as a Warpriest of the same level and may prepare any spells that he knows. At 1st level, he knows three 1st level spells plus a number equal to his Intelligence modifier. At each new level up, he may select an additional two spells to learn.

The Living Grimoire uses Intelligence in place of his Wisdom to determine the DC and number of uses of domain abilities.

#### Holy Book
A Living Grimoire can manifest a large, heavy book in his hand and can use it as a weapon. It deals damage as a cold iron light mace and the character gains a +1 bonus to attack rolls. The Living Grimoire gains the bonuses of the Warpriest Sacred Weapon ability with the book.

#### Sacred Word
From 4th level, a Warpriest can enhance his holy book as a swift action. For every four additional levels, he can add an additional +1 enhancement (maximum +5 at 20th level) He can use this ability for a total number of minutes per day equal to half his class level + his Wisdom modifier. Rather than adding an enhancement bonus, a Living Grimoire can add properties to the book for a certain point cost, as follows:

#### Blessed Script
From 5th level, after hitting an enemy with his holy book, the caster level and DC of a Living Grimoire's next spell are increased by +1. At 10th and 16th levels, this total increases by an additional +1.

Whenever the Living Grimoire casts a spell, his holy book gains the bane weapon special ability for 2 rounds. At 10th level, the critical multiplier increases to x3 for 2 rounds. Finally, at 16th level, each successful hit with the holy book within the next 2 rounds after casting a spell applies the effects of the *Crushing Despair* spell for one minute.

#### Word of God
At 20th level, up to 7 times per day, a Living Grimoire can smite an enemy within 50'. The target must make a Fortitude save (DC = 20 + INT modifier or die). If the Living Grimoire cast a spell in the previous round, the DC increases by +4. If the target makes its save, it takes 10d6 divine damage.

If the Living Grimoire kills an enemy with this ability, he gains a stacking +1 bonus to spell resistance checks and damage rolls with the holy book for the rest of the day.

A Tactical Leader receives a morale bonus equal to half his class level to Persuasion (diplomacy) checks.

#### Swift Tactician
At 3rd level, as a standard action, a tactical Leader can grant his teamwork feats to all allies within 30'. They gain the benefit for 3 rounds + 1 round per two class levels. The ability can be used once per day plus one additional time per day at 6th, 9th, 15th and 18th levels. At 12th level, the Tactician ability becomes a swift action.

#### Battle Acumen
At 14th level, the Tactical Leader can grant his Judgment bonuses to a single ally within 30' for 1 minute. He can use this ability a number of times per day equal to his Wisdom modifier.
