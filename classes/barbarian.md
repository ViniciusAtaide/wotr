# Barbarian
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Barbarian has the following characteristics:

- Primary attributes: Strength and Constitution
- Full AB progression
- 12 HP at level 1, then 7 HP per level (d12 hit die)
- High saving throws for Fortitude, low saving throws for Reflex and Will
- Rage ability
- 3 skill points per level
- Alignment must be non-Lawful.

Class Skills: Athletics, Mobility, Lore (Nature), Perception, Persuasion

#### Barbarian Proficiencies
A Barbarian is proficient with all simple and martial weapons, light armour, medium armour and shields.

#### Rage
At 1st level a Barbarian can rage for a number of rounds per day equal to 4 + his Constitution modifier. For each class level after 1st, he can rage for 2 additional rounds per day. Rage is a free action.

When enraged, the Barbarian gains +2 to attack rolls, melee damage rolls, thrown weapon damage rolls and Will saving throws along with a -2 penalty to AC. He also gains 2 temporary HP per hit die. Ending the rage is a free action and the Barbarian is fatigued for 1 minute.

#### Fast Movement
A Barbarian's speed increases by 10' when not in heavy armour and not carrying a heavy load.

#### Rage Power
At 2nd level, the Barbarian can learn new abilities to apply when raging.

"Totem" powers are exclusive. Selecting one "Totem" power closes off the other categories.

##### Animal Fury
The Barbarian gains a bite attack at his full base attack bonus -5. The attack hits for 1d4 damage + half the Barbarian's Strength modifier.

##### Beast Totem, Lesser
The Barbarian gains two claw attacks. These are made at the Barbarian's full attack bonus and deal 1d6 damage + Strength modifier.

##### Beast Totem
Prerequisites: Beast Totem, Lesser; Barbarian level: 6

The Barbarian gains +1 natural armour bonus + 1 for every four levels.

##### Beast Totem, Greater
Prerequisites: Beast Totem; Barbarian level: 10

The Barbarian gains the pounce ability allowing a full attack at the end of a charge. His claw damage increases to 1d8 and the claws deal x3 damage on a critical hit.

##### Celestial Totem, Lesser
The Barbarian heals +1 HP per character level when subject to a healing effect.

##### Celestial Totem
The Barbarian triggers an invisibility purge effect around him. This only affects non-good creatures.

##### Celestial Totem, Greater
The Barbarian gains spell resistance equal to 11 + his class level against spells with the evil descriptor. He also gains a +2 bonus to saves against spells and spell-like effects cast by evil creatures.

##### Clear Mind
Prerequisites: Barbarian level: 8

Once per rage, the Barbarian rerolls the first failed Will saving throw and takes the better result.

##### Come and Get Me
As a free action, the Barbarian can take a -4 penalty to AC but each attack against him provoke an attack of opportunity from him.

##### Daemon Totem, Lesser
The Barbarian gains a +2 bonus to saves against acid, death effects, disease and poison. This bonus increases by +1 if the Barbarian selects Daemon Totem and by +2 if he also selects Daemon Totem, Greater.

##### Daemon Totem
The Barbarian imposes a negative level on his opponent on a critical hit.

##### Daemon Totem, Greater
The Barbarian heals 5 HP if he kills a living creature with CR equal to half his character level.

##### Fearless Rage
Prerequisites: Barbarian level: 12

The Barbarian is immune to the shaken and frightened conditions.

##### Fiend Totem, Lesser
The Barbarian gains a 1d8 gore attack. This is a secondary attack if the Barbarian is using weapons.

##### Fiend Totem
An enemy striking the Barbarian with a melee attack, unarmed strike or natural weapon takes 1d6 piercing damage.

##### Fiend Totem, Greater
Good creatures adjacent to the Barbarian are shaken and take 2d6 slashing damage at the start of the Barbarian's turn. Neutral creatures are merely shaken and evil creatures are unaffected.

##### Guarded Stance
The Barbarian gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC, + 1 for every 4 levels.

##### Reflexive Dodge
Prerequisites: Guarded Stance; Barbarian level: 6

While in the guarded stance, the Barbarian can apply his dodge bonus to AC as a bonus to Reflex saves.

##### Rolling Dodge
Prerequisites: Guarded Stance; Barbarian level: 6

While in the guarded stance, the Barbarian's dodge AC bonus is doubled against ranged attacks.

##### Protect Vitals
Prerequisites: Guarded Stance; Barbarian level: 8

While in the guarded stance, the Barbarian gains an additional +4 dodge bonus to AC against critical hit confirmation rolls.

##### Increased Damage Reduction
Prerequisites: Barbarian level: 8

The Barbarians DR increases by 2/-. This power may be selected up to three times.

##### Internal Fortitude
Prerequisites: Barbarian level: 8

The Barbarian is immune to the sickened and nauseated conditions.

##### Lethal Stance
The Barbarian gains a +1 competence bonus to attack rolls, + 1 for every 4 levels.

##### Deadly Accuracy
Prerequisites: Lethal Stance; Barbarian level: 4

If the Barbarian scores a critical hit while in lethal stance, he applies double the stance's attack bonus to the confirmation roll.

##### Lethal Accuracy
Prerequisites: Deadly Accuracy; Barbarian level: 16

While in lethal stance, the critical multiplier for the equipped weapon increases by 1.

##### Powerful Stance
The Barbarian gains a +1 bonus to melee damage rolls and thrown weapon damage rolls. The bonus increases by +1 for every four class levels.

##### Crippling Blows
Prerequisites: Powerful Stance; Barbarian level: 8

While in powerful stance, the first successful melee attack in a round inflicts a -1 penalty to attack rolls and Fortitude saves for 1 round. At 12th, 16th and 20th level, the penalty increases by -1 and the duration increases by 1 round.

##### Wrecking Blows
Prerequisites: Powerful Stance; Barbarian level: 12

While in powerful stance, the Barbarian's melee attacks and thrown weapon attacks deal additional damage equal to his Constitution modifier. At 16th and 20th level, this bonus increases by +1. The damage bypasses DR but doesn't multiply on a critical hit.

##### Reckless Stance
The Barbarian gains +1 to attack rolls but takes a -1 penalty to AC. The bonus and penalty increase 1 for every 4 class levels.

##### Inspire Ferocity
Prerequisites: Reckless Stance

When in reckless stance, the Barbarian imparts the attack bonus and AC penalty to allies within 30'.

##### Renewed Vigor
Prerequisites: Barbarian level 4

As a standard action the Barbarian heals 1d8 damage + his Constitution modifier. For every 4 levels beyond 4th, an additional 1d8 damage is healed (5d8 at level 20). This ability can be used once per day.

##### Scent
The Barbarian can detect opponents within 30' as if he had blindsense.

##### Swift Foot
The Barbarian gains +10' speed. This ability may be selected up to three times.

#### Uncanny Dodge
At 2nd level, the Barbarian cannot be caught flat-footed or lose his Dexterity bonus to AC if the attacker is invisible.

#### Danger Sense
At 3rd level, the Barbarian gains a +1 bonus on Reflex saves to avoid traps and a +1 dodge bonus to AC against attacks made by traps. These bonuses increase by +1 every 3 levels (maximum +6 at 18th level).

#### Improved Uncanny Dodge
At 5th level, the Barbarian cannot be flanked. An enemy Rogue cannot hit you with a sneak attack unless he has at least four more Rogue levels than your class level.

#### Damage Reduction
Starting at 7th level, the Barbarian gains DR 1/-. This increases by 1 point at levels 10, 13, 16 and 19.

#### Greater Rage
At 11th level, the bonuses the Barbarian gains from raging increase to +3 and he gains +3 temporary HP per hit die.

#### Indomitable Will
At 14th level, the Barbarian gains an additional +4 bonus to Will saves to resist enchantment effects while raging. This stacks with the bonuses to Will saves from raging.

#### Tireless Rage
At 17th level, the Barbarian no longer becomes fatigued after raging.

#### Mighty Rage
At 20th level, the bonuses the Barbarian gains from raging increase to +4 and he gains +4 temporary HP per hit die.

##### Playing a Barbarian
A Barbarian is somewhat feat-starved and defensively weak. However, the Limitless Rage mythic ability means that it's not a total wash. Given that it's not really possible to build a Barbarian to avoid hits and HP tanking isn't an effective strategy, you'll probably want to use a two-handed weapon with reach. Rage notwithstanding, a Barbarian is a long way behind a Two-Handed Fighter in damage output without really offering many compelling reasons to pick it. However, if you're looking for a dip, the Barbarian gives you Uncanny Dodge in two levels without losing BAB.

Rather than feats, a Barbarian receives Rage Powers. There is a definite hierarchy here. You want to take the Beast Totem line and the full Lethal Stance line. Place anything left over in Swift Foot and Increased Damage Reduction. You may find it tempting to take Reckless Stance and Inspire Ferocity. However, the massive AC hit to the entire party outweighs the AB bonus. If you want rage powers on everyone, a Skald does this better. Powerful Stance + Wrecking Blows is only better than Lethal Stance + Deadly Accuracy if you use a weapon with a threat range of 20 (e.g. scythe). Even then, the difference is slight.

Several of the Barbarian archetypes are very much worse than the base class. An Armored Hulk loses Uncanny Dodge in exchange for the ability to wear heavy armour. Since a Fighter does the heavily armoured thing better, this is a very bad trade.

A Beastskin Berserker trades Rage Powers for the ability to assume (eventually) three beast forms. Even the bear form will greatly reduce your damage output and given that the form only lasts for the duration of the rage, it is worse than other wild shape abilities.

A Flesheater gives up a lot for the ability to feed on enemies. This would have been a much better class in *Kingmaker* where there was a variety of enemies to consume. In *this* game, you're trading Uncanny Dodge, Damage Reduction and a bunch of rage powers for Protection from Good since pretty much all enemies are demons. Needless to say, this is a poor trade. The size increase when raging is OK.

An Instinctual Warrior trades Damage Reduction for terrific AC stacking - as much as +12 when raging. Needless to say, not being hit in the first place >> reducing damage from being hit. Damage output is barely affected - you trade three Rage Powers for three useful feats so you have room for Beast Totem and your choice of stance. The combination of Focused Rage and Cunning Elusion makes the Instinctual Warrior not just the best Barbarian choice but one of the best choices full stop.

An Invulnerable Rager loses Uncanny Dodge. However, it trades it for something worthwhile: 10/- DR is not to be sniffed at. The Mad Dog trades it for something even better. Mad Dog may not be the best class with an animal companion but it is probably the class with the best animal companion. The cost is quite steep, however: a Mad Dog only has half as many Rage Powers as the base class and less DR.

Finally, the Pack Rager is the worst Barbarian archetype by some margin, trading Rage Powers and Damage Reduction for teamwork feats. When it comes to teamwork feats, you want Outflank on everyone and most of the others on no-one. The class doesn't even get something like solo tactics to make the teamwork feats less terrible. Instead at 7th level (which is a long time to wait), a Pack Rager can share one teamwork feat *while raging*. If he could share Rage Powers that might lift the Pack Rager above the Armored Hulk, but instead, it is one of the very worst class choices.

Some of the archetypes can be improved with multiclassing. Something like Mad Dog (17) / Sacred Huntsmaster (3) or Invulnerable Rager (15) / Tower Shield Specialist (5) are good combinations.

#### Indomitable Stance
An Armored Hulk gains a +1 bonus on combat manoeuvre checks, CMD and Reflex saves against trample attacks. She also gains a +1 bonus to AC and attack and damage rolls against charging creatures.

#### Improved Armored Swiftness
At 5th level, an Armored Hulk moves 10' faster than normal in medium and heavy armour.

#### Crush and Tear
A Flesheater has a secondary bite attack which deals 1d4 damage (1d3 if small) + half his Strength modifier. At 5th level, the damage increases to 1d6 (1d4) and is treated as silver and cold iron for the purposes of damage reduction. At 11th level, the damage increases to 1d8 (1d6) and deals an additional 1d6 points of bleed damage. Finally, at 17th level, the damage increases to 1d10 (1d8). If the Flesheater has the Animal Fury rage power, the bite damage increases by one step while raging.

#### Power from Flesh
From 2nd level, a Flesheater must occasionally consume the flesh of a dragon, fey, magical beast, outsider or undead to satisfy his hunger. Until he does so, he gains a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls against creatures that he can consume while raging but also suffers a -2 penalty to Intelligence and a 20% chance of becoming confused each round. Feeding is a full round action and for 24 hours, the Flesheater gains one point of Essence and a minor trait based on the creature devoured. Only one point of Essence can be held at a time.

At 9th and 18th levels, the bonus against edible enemies increases by +1.

#### Devour Essence
From 2nd level, a Flesheater can use the stored Essence to restore a number of HP equal to 1d8 + his Constitution modifier. At 8th level and every 5 additional levels, the amount of HP restored increases by +1d8 (max 4d8 at 18th level).

#### Changeable Form
From 7th level, Devour Flesh grants a major trait and any abilities granted can be used outside Rage. The number of Essence points that can be stored increases to 2.

#### Unbound Rage
From 11th level, the Flesheater grows in size when entering Rage as long as the last creature devoured was at least one size category larger than him. The effect is identical to *Enlarge Person* but the Flesheater gains a +4 size bonus to Strength and a -2 penalty to intelligence.

#### You Are What You Eat
From 14th level, after devouring a creature, a Flesheater gains a special ability that he can use as a standard action by spending two Essence points. The number of Essence points that can be stored increases to 3.

#### Unbound Form
At 20th level, Unbound Rage increases the Flesheater's size by two categories and the Strength bonus is +12. The number of Essence points that can be stored increases to 4.

#### Instinctual Warrior Proficiencies
An Instinctual Warrior is proficient with all simple and martial weapons and light and heavy shields.

#### Fervent Reflexes
An Instinctual Warrior adds his Wisdom modifier to initiative checks.

#### Focused Rage
At 1st level an Instinctual Warrior can rage for a number of rounds per day equal to 4 + his Wisdom modifier. For each class level after 1st, he can rage for 2 additional rounds per day. Rage is a free action.

When enraged, the Barbarian gains +2 bonus to attack rolls and Wisdom. He also gains 2 temporary HP per hit die. Ending the rage is a free action and the Barbarian is fatigued for 1 minute.

#### Cunning Elusion
When unarmoured and not wielding a shield, an Instinctual Warrior adds his Wisdom bonus to his AC and CMD. At 4th level and every four subsequent levels, this bonus increases by +1 (maximum +5 at 20th level).

#### Rage Power (Instinctual Warrior)
Besides the normal Barbarian rage powers, an Instinctual Warrior can select a number of Monk abilities and Ki powers: Deflect Arrows (requires *Improved Unarmed Strike*), Purity of Body (requires 8th level), Diamond Body (requires 12th level), Diamond Soul (requires 16th level) and Perfect Self (requires 20th level).

#### Greater Focused Rage (Instinctual Warrior)
At 11th level, the Instinctual Warrior's bonus to attack rolls and Wisdom while raging increases to +3 and he gains +3 temporary HP per hit die.

#### Unparalleled Senses
At 18th level, an Instinctual Warrior gains blindsense to a range of 30' and blindsight to a range of 15'.

#### Mighty Focused Rage (Instinctual Warrior)
At 20th level, the Instinctual Warrior's bonus to attack rolls and Wisdom while raging increases to +4 and he gains +4 temporary HP per hit die.

#### Pack Tactics
At 2nd level, a Mad Dog and his companion gain +4 on attack rolls when flanking the same opponent.

#### Ferocious Fetch
At 5th level, a Mad Dog's companion gains Greater Trip as a bonus feat.

#### Throat Cutter
Whenever a Mad Dog's companion succeeds at a bull rush, overrun or trip combat manoeuvre against an opponent that he also threatens, he can make an attack of opportunity against that opponent.

### Pack Rager
A Pack Rager uses tactics as well as brute force.

- Gains bonus teamwork feats
- Gains fewer Rage Powers
- Loses Damage Reduction.

The character creator shows a point of DR applied at 10th level. This is both not very useful and a bug.

#### Bonus Teamwork Feat
At 2rd level and every four levels thereafter, a Pack Rager may select a Teamwork Feat.

You cannot select *Shake It Off*.

#### Raging Tactician
At 7th level, when entering rage, the Pack Rager grants a single teamwork feat to all allies within 50'. At 13th level, this increases to two teamwork feats and at 19th level, three.
