# Fighter
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Fighter has the following characteristics:

- Primary attributes: Strength and Constitution
- Full AB progression
- 10 HP at level 1, then 6 HP per level (d10 hit die)
- High saving throws for Fortitude, low saving throws for Reflex and Will
- 2 skill points per level

Class Skills: Athletics, Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Persuasion

#### Fighter Proficiencies
Fighters are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, all types of armor and shields (including tower shields).

#### Bonus Combat Feat
The Fighter may select any feat from the list of Combat Feats.

#### Bravery
At 2nd level, the Fighter gains a +1 bonus to Will saves against fear. This bonus increases by +1 for every four additional class levels (maximum +5 at 18th level).

#### Armor Training
Starting at 3rd level, a Fighter reduces his armor penalty by 1 and increases the maximum Dexterity bonus allowed by his armor by +1. Every four additional class levels, these bonuses increase by 1 (maximum -4 / +4 at 15th level).

A Fighter can move at normal speed while wearing medium armor. At 7th level, a Fighter can move at normal speed while wearing heavy armor.

#### Weapon Training
At 5th level, a Fighter can select a group of weapons, gaining a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls when using weapons from that group. Every four levels thereafter (up to level 17), the Fighter may choose an additional weapon group and gains +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this group. In addition, the bonuses for any previously selected groups increase by +1 each.

The Fighter adds this bonus to any combat maneuver checks made with weapons from a given group and adds a bonus to his CMD when attempting to resist disarm attempts.

#### Armor Mastery
At 19th level, a Fighter gains DR 5/- when wearing armor or using a shield.

#### Weapon Mastery
At 20th level, the Fighter chooses a specific weapon (such as a longsword). Any attacks made using that weapon automatically confirm critical hits and the critical multiplier increases by 1. The Fighter cannot be disarmed when wielding this type of weapon.

##### Playing a Fighter
The big draw of a Fighter is that you can basically build him how you like because of the number of feats he accumulates over his career, including a few specific to the class. Weapon Training is the unique class feature. Rather than becoming expert in multiple classes, I would be inclined to use it to plug weaknesses and grant enhancements that cannot be achieved through feats - Armed Bravery and Fighter's Tactics, for example.

The class superpowers - Armor Mastery and Weapon Mastery - come late but are extremely powerful which makes playing a pure Fighter a valid class option. However, you may find the Aldori Defender rather more interesting to play since Steel Net and Counterstrike are very good abilities. The Aldori Defender is also a good dip, giving you a bonus combat feat and an Exotic Weapon Proficiency.

An Armiger trades two feats for an improved Bravery ability and minor benefits from membership of an order. The ability granted by Order of the Scourge is quite good and the Armiger keeps Armor and Weapon Mastery, so choosing between this and the base class is a matter of taste.

A Defender of the Hearth's abilities cost one bonus feat and one one rank of Weapon Training which is a good trade even if the core ability reduces damage from being hit rather than avoiding being hit in the first place. You also lose the extremely powerful Weapon Mastery capstone ability but the replacement, Defender's Gift, is quite good. Equip a heavy shield (for Shield Block) and invest in TWF and Shield Bash for improved damage output.

A Dragonheir Scion gives up the class superpowers for a draconic bloodline. The loss of Armor Training is somewhat compensated by natural armor bonuses gained through the bloodline but you may question whether blindsense to 60' is worth giving up Weapon Mastery for. The answer is: no, it really isn't.

A Mutation Warrior swaps Armor Training for a Mutagen. Given that a mutagen gives you an AC boost on top of everything else, this isn't a bad trade at all. Note that the mutagen never becomes persistent and you will have to wait a long time - until 15th level to be precise - to get an upgrade. In the meantime, I prefer Preserve Organs to Feral Mutagen / Feral Wings.

A Titan Fighter's signature feature is being able to dual-wield (however poorly) two-handed weapons. However, for most of the game you will have similar damage output to regular Fighter dual-wielding, say, longswords because a regular Fighter gets Effortless Dual Wielding and can benefit from Gloves of Dueling. It's only with Unstoppable Strike at 17th level that a Titan Fighter edges ahead and that's a long time to wait. Note that Titan Fighter makes a great 1-level dip for arcane casters, allowing them to dual-wield quarterstaffs.

The Tower Shield Specializt is a strange class and the tower shield focus feels like the tail wagging the dog, although the armor boosts and 9th-level abilities make it defensively strong. However, losing Weapon Training and Weapon Mastery means that damage output suffers greatly.

The Two-Handed Fighter on the other hand does what it says on the tin. It does big damage numbers consistently and reliably. However, it is defensively weak so you may want to use a reach weapon (Glaive or Fauchard).

#### Defensive Parry
At 3rd level, when the Defender makes a full attack with a dueling sword, he gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC against melee attacks until the beginning of his next turn. This increases by +1 every four levels after 3rd (maximum +5 at 19th level).

#### Disarming Strike
At 6th level, when the Defender successfully disarms an opponent using the dueling sword, he also deals normal damage (but doesn't apply his Strength modifier).

#### Steel Net
At 8th level when fighting defensively with a dueling sword, the Defender's penalties to attacks are reduced by 2 and the dodge bonus to AC is increased by 2.

#### Counterattack
At 10th level, a Defender may make an attack of opportunity as an immediate action against an opponent who hits him with a melee attack so long as the attacker is within reach.

#### Order
An Armiger may choose from one of seven orders.

##### Order of the Chain
At 1st level, you gain a +2 bonus to CMB and CMD. This increases by +1 at 5th and 9th levels.

##### Order of the Gate
While wearing armor, the chance of arcane spell failure is reduced by 5% as 1st level, 10% at 5th and 20% at 9th.

##### Order of the Godclaw
You gain an aura which lasts for 1 minute and grants all allies within 30' a +1 morale bonus to AC, attack and damage rolls against chaotic creatures. This bonus increases by +1 at 5th and 9th levels. You can use this ability three times per day.

##### Order of the Nail
At 1st level you gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls against a category of creature taken from the Ranger's Favored Enemy list. At 5th and 9th levels, the bonus increases by an additional +2.

##### Order of the Pyre
At 1st level, you gain fast healing 5 for a number of rounds equal to your class level after receiving fire damage. At 5th level, you gain fast healing 10 and at 9th level, you gain fast healing 15.

##### Order of the Rack
Once per day, as a standard action, you can prevent a single creature from casting spells for a number of rounds equal to your class level unless it makes a Will saving throw (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + Charisma modifier). You can use this ability three times per day. At 5th and 9th levels, you gain an additional use. The same effect occurs when you confirm a critical hit.

##### Order of the Scourge
When you land a killing blow, enemies within 15' receive a -2 penalty to Will saves for three rounds. This penalty increases to -3 at 5th level and -4 at 9th.

#### Studious Squire
An Armiger receives 1 additional skill point per level.

#### Ardent
At 2nd level, an Armiger gains a +1 bonus to saves against charm and compulsion effects. This increases by +1 for every additional four levels (max +5 at 18th level). Once per day, when an Armiger fails a save against a charm or compulsion effect, he may reroll.

#### Shield Block
When using a heavy shield or a tower shield, the damage of the first successful attack against the character is reduced by 20%. At 5th level and every 5 subsequent levels, the number of attacks affected increases by 1 (max 5 at 20th level).

From 5th level, when wielding a weapon and shield, the Defender of the Hearth can deal weapon damage to all enemies within 20' once per combat. This action does not require an attack roll.

#### Defender's Gift
At 20th level, the Defender of the Hearth deals double weapon damage for 1 round after using Shield Block.

#### Draconic Bloodline
A Dragonheir Scion has a Draconic bloodline.

#### Fearful Might
At 2nd level, a Dragonheir Scion gains a +1 bonus on Intimidate skill checks. This increases by +1 every four additional class levels (max +5 at 18th level).

#### Draconic Defenses
At 3rd level, a Dragonheir Scion gains a +1 natural armor bonus to AC and energy resistance 5 against the bloodline's energy type. At 7th level, the AC bonus increases to +2 and the energy resistance to 10. At 13th level, these increase agains to +3 AC and energy resistance 20.

#### Draconic Strike
At 4th level, whenever the Dragonheir Scion damages a creature with Arcane Strike, the attack does an additional 1d4 damage of the bloodline's energy type.

#### Draconic Presence
At 6th level, a Dragonheir Scion receives Dazzling Display as a bonus feat and can use it as a standard action.

#### Might of Wyrms
At 20th level, you gain immunity to paralysis, sleep and the bloodline's element. You also gain blindsense with a range of 60'.

#### Giant Weapon Wielder
A Titan Fighter can wield a two-handed weapon in one hand. It is treated as a one-handed weapon for purposes of calculating the damage bonuses from Strength and Power Attack. In addition, the fighter receive a -2 penalty to attack rolls when using a two-handed weapon in one hand.

#### Massive Attacks
At 2nd level and every four subsequent levels, a Titan fighter selects a feat from the Ranger's Two-Weapon Combat Style feats.

#### Incredible Heft
At 3rd level and every four subsequent levels, a Titan Fighter gains +1 to attack and damage rolls when wielding a two-handed weapon in one hand.

#### Giant's Might
From 9th level, while wielding a two-handed weapon in one hand, a Titan Fighter adds 1½ times his Strength modifier to weapon damage rolls.

#### Unstoppable Strike
From 17th level, while wielding a two-handed weapon in one hand, a Titan Fighter adds twice his Strength modifier to weapon damage rolls.

#### Tower Shield Improved Evasion
At 20th level and when using a tower shield, if the Specializt fails a Reflex save against an attack that normally deals half damage on a successful save, he takes only half damage.

#### Strong Grip
At 2nd level, the Fighter gains a +1 to CMB and CMD on sunder and disarm attempts. This increases by +1 for every four additional levels (maximum +5 at 18th level).

#### Overhand Chop
At 3rd level, when the Fighter makes a single attack (with the attack action or a charge) with a two-handed weapon, he adds double his Strength bonus to damage rolls.

#### Two-Handed Weapon Training
At 5th level, the Fighter gains +1 to attack and damage rolls when attacking with a weapon from the two-handed weapon group. The bonuses increase by +1 for every four additional class levels (maximum +4 at 17th level).

#### Backswing
At 7th level, when the Fighter makes a full attack with a two-handed weapon, he adds double his Strength bonus to damage rolls for all attacks after the first.

#### Piledriver
At 11th level, as a standard action, the Fighter may make a single melee attack with a two-handed weapon. If the attack hits, he may make a bull rush or trip attempt against the target as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity.

#### Greater Power Attack
At 15th level when using Power Attack, the bonus damage is doubled instead of increased by half.

#### Devastating Blow
At 19th level, as a standard action, the Fighter may make a single attack with a two-handed weapon at a -5 penalty to hit. If the attack hits, it is treated as a critical threat. If the hit is confirmed, special weapon abilities that are activated on critical hits do not activate.
