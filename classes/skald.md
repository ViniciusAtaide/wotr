# Skald
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Skald has the following characteristics:

- Primary attribute: Strength, Charisma
- Medium (3/4) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast arcane spells up to 6th-level
- Spells are cast spontaneously
- Uses the Bard spell list
- 6 HP per level
- High saving throws for Fortitude and Will, low saving throws for Reflex
- 3 skill points per level.

Class Skills: Athletics. Mobility, Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Lore (Religion), Perception, Persuasion, Use Magic Device

A Skald shares a number of abilities with a Bard: Bardic Knowledge, Well Versed, Lore Master and Dirge of Doom. (Improved) Uncanny Dodge is the same as the Barbarian ability.

#### Skald Proficiencies
A Skald is proficient with all simple and martial weapons, light armour, medium armour and shields. She can cast magic while wearing light or medium armour and using a shield without risking arcane spell failure.

#### Raging Song
The Skald can sing for a total of three rounds + her Charisma modifier per day. She can sing for an additional 2 rounds per class level.

#### Inspired Rage
At 1st level, affected allies gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls and a +1 bonus to Will saves. They also take a -1 penalty to AC. While under the effects of Inspired Rage, only the Skald herself can use magic. When the song ends, those affected are not fatigued.

At 4th level and every four levels thereafter, the Will save bonus increases by +1 (max +6 at 20th level). At 8th level the attack and damage bonuses increase to +2. At 16th level, they increase to +3.

You can turn off Inspired Rage for any party member.

#### Bonus Feat
At 1st level, a Skald gains a bonus magic feat.

#### Skald Talents
The Skald can select from the Rogue Talent list at 2nd, 7th, 12th and 17th levels.

#### Rage Powers
From 3rd level, a Skald can add the effects of a Barbarian's Rage Powers to Inspired Rage.

#### Song of Strength
From 6th level, allies within 50' may add half the Skald's class level to Athletics checks.

#### Raging Song (Move Action)
At 7th level, a Skald can start a song as a move action instead of a standard action.

#### Damage Reduction
At 9th level, the Skald gains DR 1/-. The DR is granted to allies affected by Inspired Rage. At 14th and 19th levels, the DR increases by +1.

#### Raging Song (Swift Action)
At 13th level, a Skald can start a song as a swift action.

#### Song of the Fallen
At 14th level, the Skald's song can bring dead allies within 50' back to life for as long as the song lasts.

#### Master Skald
At 20th level, Inspired Rage no longer inflicts a penalty to AC and affected allies can cast spells. In addition, they gain the benefits of *Haste*.

##### Playing a Skald
The Skald is a stacked class, that's for sure. Compared to a Bard, you lose the useful skill bonuses from Inspire Competence and the hard crowd control of Fascinate. Inspire Courage is traded for Inspired Rage which is comparable. However, the Skald has a superpower: granting all members of the party pounce (through Beast Totem) and better criticals (through Lethal stance).

Besides this, you receive martial weapon proficiency, five bonus feats, Improved Uncanny Dodge and a bit of DR for everyone. The capstone ability is so good that taking anything other than the full 20 levels is a waste. As I said: stacked. You may not want your main character to occupy the party support slot, but a Skald mercenary isn't a bad idea at all.

The Battle Scion has the *option* of taking teamwork feats in place of rage powers. Doing so would be wasteful so the question is: Intimidate bonus vs. cut-down Bardic Knowledge.

The similarly named Battle Singer gives up half her rage abilities for an increasingly powerful sonic damage ability. Sonic damage is good (because enemies typically don't have resistance) but if you think about the amount of damage (something like 60 damage at 15th level, and that after two rounds of build-up), you'll probably conclude that Lethal Stance on everyone would be better.

The Court Poet replaces Inspired Rage with Insightful Contemplation. Morale bonuses to Intelligence and Charisma can be good if you build your party around it - after all, a lot of people think that cheese-dipping Scaled Fist and stacking Charisma to silly degrees is the only way to play the game. If nothing else, it can make *Mark of Justice* bite that much harder. However, if an archetype is going to give up the class superpower, it needs to bring something really good to the table. A piffling bonus to spell damage isn't good. It's more like a rounding error.

The Demon Dancer is a terrible choice because Greater Fiend Totem is terrible. Between that and Demonic Conquest, a Demon Dancer is likely to do more harm to your party than to enemies. Bite attacks for everyone don't even begin to make up for the shortcomings.

A Herald of the Horn basically trades a DC bonus on sonic spells (there are six in the Bard spellbook) for the ability to take 10 on knowledge checks. I can't imagine that you'd pick the archetype for the Crumbling Blast ability which is probably a waste of a standard action. At least it doesn't cost you anything.

The Hunt Caller is kinda useless. You are trading two rage powers for two useless abilities (I mean, would you *choose* the Scent ability?). Turning your front-liners into wolves, leopards or bears will simply make them weaker.

Finally, an Inciter gives up two Rage Powers (not good) for sneak attacks and the ability to grant sneak damage to allies. The sneak damage is modest (maximum +4d6 for the Inciter and +3d6 for her allies) but this becomes weaponised from 14th level when you can grant Advanced Talents to the party. All your attackers having Dispelling Attacks or Crippling Blows is extremely good. She also trades any extra songs for the ability to debuff enemies. Given that Inspired Rage is the only song really worth using, losing others is neither here nor there. The capstone ability makes enemies debuffed without a save which is also decent. Whether it's better than hasting the party is another question.

#### Bardic Knowledge (Battle Scion)
A Battle Scion gets a bonus equal to half her class level on Knowledge (World) checks.

#### Courtly Prowess
A Battle Scion gets a bonus equal to half her class level on Intimidate checks.

#### Battle Prowess
From 3rd level, whenever a Battle Scion can add a Rage Power to her song, she can instead take any teamwork feat for which she meets the prerequisites. She can add the teamwork feat to Inspired Rage at a cost of 3 rounds of raging song. Allies must meet the prerequisites to benefit, however.

#### Battle Song - First Verse
As a standard action, a Battle Singer can spend 1 round of Raging Song to inflict 1d6 sonic damage per 2 class levels to all enemies in a 15' cone. Affected creatures can attempt a Fortitude save for half damage. Failure leaves them staggered for 1 round.

#### Battle Song - Second Verse
As a standard action, a Battle Singer can spend 2 rounds of Raging Song to inflict 1d6 sonic damage per 2 class levels to all enemies in a 50' radius. Affected creatures can attempt a Fortitude save for half damage. Failure leaves them stunned for 1 round and then staggered for 1 round.

If Second Verse is used within 2 rounds of First Verse, it costs 1 round of Raging Song and is empowered.

#### Battle Song - Final Verse
As a standard action, a Battle Singer can spend 2 rounds of Raging Song to inflict 1d8 sonic damage per class level to a single enemy. The enemy can attempt a Fortitude save for half damage. If it fails its saving throw, it is also stunned for 1 round and staggered for 1d4 + 1 rounds. If it succeeds, it is staggered for 1 round.

If Final Verse is used within 2 rounds of First Verse, it costs 1 less round of Raging Song, it is empowered and the DC increases by +2.

If Final Verse is used within 2 rounds of Second Verse, it costs 1 less round of Raging Song, it is maximised and the DC increases by +2.

#### Insightful Contemplation
At 1st level, affected allies gain a +2 morale bonus to Intelligence and Charisma and a +1 bonus to Will saves. They also take a -1 penalty to AC.

At 4th level and every four levels thereafter, the Will save bonus increases by +1 (max +6 at 20th level). At 8th level the attribute bonuses increase to +4. At 16th level, they increase to +6.

#### Handling the Crowd
At 2nd level, a Court Poet gains a +1 bonus to AC when adjacent to two or more creatures. She also gains a bonus equal to half her class level to Persuasion checks.

#### Insightful Spell
At 4th level, whenever the Court Poet casts a spell that does HP damage, she adds +1 to the damage. Every four levels the damage increases by +1 (max +5 at 20th level). Allies affected by Insightful Contemplation also gain this bonus damage.

#### Song of Inspiration
Allies within 30' may add half the Skald's class level to Wisdom-based skill checks.

The Bonded Object can be used once per day to restore a spell slot.

#### Horn Call
At 7th level, the DC of any spells with the sonic descriptor increases by +1. The DC increase is +2 at 13th level and +3 at 19th level.

At 17th level, the Herald can use this ability twice per day and it deals 10d6 sonic damage. A failed save against the stronger ability leaves affected creatures stunned for 1 round.

#### Serpent's Tongue
An Inciter adds half her class level to Persuasion (Bluff and Intimidate) checks.

#### Insult
Enemies hearing an Inciter's Raging Song must make a Will save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + CHA modifier) or become enraged, taking a -2 penalty on attack rolls and skill checks until they make a successful melee or ranged attack against the Inciter or damage her with a spell. This is a sonic, mind-affecting, emotion effect.

#### Mind Games
An Inciter gains a +1 bonus to saving throws vs. mind-affecting, compulsion, fear and emotion effects. This bonus increases by +1 every six additional class levels (maximum +4 at 19th level).

#### Sneak Attack
From 2nd level an Inciter's attacks deal an additional +1d6 sneak damage when a target is flanked or denied its Dexterity bonus to AC. The Inciter gains an additional +1d6 sneak damage at 7th level, 12th level and 17th level.

#### Prompted By Backstabbing
From 8th level, allies hearing the Inciter's Raging Song add +1d6 sneak damage to attacks. This increases by +1d6 at 13th level and 18th level.

#### Caustic Ridicule
From 10th level, enemies affected by the Inciter's Insult ability suffer a -1 penalty to AC and Will saving throws. At 15th level, this penalty increases to -2.

#### Sneaky Tricks
From 12th level, an Inciter can choose Advanced Talents which modify sneak attacks and grant the bonus to allies through her Raging Song (note that she does not get to choose a talent at this level).

#### Hit a Nerve
The Inciter's Raging Song no longer inflicts a penalty to allies' AC or limits what abilities they can use. Enemies suffer half the penalties from Insult and Caustic Ridicule even when making their saving throws.
