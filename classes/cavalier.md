# Cavalier
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Cavalier has the following characteristics:

- Primary attributes: Strength and Constitution
- Full AB progression
- 10 HP at level 1, then 6 HP per level (d10 hit die)
- High saving throws for Fortitude, low saving throws for Reflex and Will
- 3 skill points per level
- Gains a Mount.

Class Skills: Athletics, Mobility, Persuasion

#### Cavalier Proficiencies
Cavaliers are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, all types of armour and shields (excluding tower shields).

#### Order
A Cavalier may belong to one of five orders: Order of the Cockatrice, Order of the Lion, Order of the Shroud, Order of the Star or Order of the Sword. These play like subclasses and are detailed below

#### Tactician
As a standard action, a Cavalier may grant a teamwork feat to allies within 30'. Allies retain this bonus for 3 rounds + 1 round per two class levels. The Cavalier can use this ability an additional time at 5th level and every 5 levels thereafter (max 5 times at 20th level).

#### Animal Companion
A Cavalier gets a horse.

#### Challenge
Once per day, a Cavalier may issue a Challenge as a swift action. The Cavalier's melee attacks against the target of the challenge do additional damage equal to her class level but he takes a -2 penalty to AC against attacks from enemies other than the target. The Cavalier can use this ability once per day plus an additional time for every three class levels beyond 1st (max 7 times at 19th level). The Cavalier's choice of Order may add additional effects.

#### Tactician - Bonus Feat
At 1st, 9th and 17th levels, a Cavalier receives a bonus teamwork feat. She must satisfy any prerequisites.

#### Cavalier's Charge
At 3rd level, while mounted, a Cavalier receives a +4 bonus on melee attack rolls when charging. In addition, she does not receive any AC penalty.

#### Banner
At 5th level, the Cavalier's banner grants a +1 morale bonus to charge attacks and a +2 morale bonus to saves against fear to allies within 60'. At 10th level and every five subsequent levels, the bonuses increase by +1 (max +4/+5 at 20th level).

#### Bonus Feat
At 6th, 12th and 18th level, a Cavalier receives a bonus combat feat. She must satisfy any prerequisites.

#### Greater Tactician
At 9th level, use of the Tactician ability is a swift action.

#### Mighty Charge
At 11th level, the threat range of the Cavalier's weapons are doubled when making a charge while mounted. If the attack is successful, the Cavalier makes a free bull rush and trip attempt.

Increased threat range does not stack with the Keen weapon property or other abilities that increase threat range, such as *Improved Critical*.

#### Demanding Challenge
The target of Cavalier's challenge takes a -2 AC penalty to attacks from anyone other than the Cavalier who issued the challenge.

#### Greater Banner
At 14th level, the Cavalier's banner additionally grants a +2 morale bonus to saving throws against charm and compulsion effects.

#### Supreme Charge
At 20th level, a Cavalier's mounted charge attack does double damage. If she confirms a critical hit, the target is stunned for 1d4 rounds. A Will save (DC = 10 + Cavalier's BAB) reduces this to staggered for 1d4 rounds.

This does ... something. The wording is lifted verbatim from the tabletop rules so ignore the thing about lances - that is simply telling you that the double damage from using a lance while mounted stacks with this ability. This ability is *supposed* to be additive with the critical hit multiplier and not multiply damage from sources like sneak attacks. What appears to happen is that total damage from all sources is added up and then doubled. Needless to say, this leads to big numbers filling the combat log.

##### Challenge (Order of the Cockatrice)
As long as the Cavalier is the only creature threatening the target of the challenge, she receives a +1 morale bonus to damage rolls against the target, increasing by an additional +1 every four class levels.

##### Braggart
At 2nd level, the Cavalier receives Dazzling Display as a bonus feat. She gains a +2 morale bonus to attack rolls against creatures affected by fear effects.

##### Steal Glory
At 8th level, the Cavalier may make an attack of opportunity against a target that she is threatening if an ally scores a critical hit against it.

##### Moment of Triumph
At 15th level, as a free action, the Cavalier may grant herself a bonus equal to her Charisma modifier to all ability checks, attack rolls, damage rolls, Saving throws and skill checks for 1 round. She also adds her Charisma modifier to her AC and any critical threats are automatically confirmed. She can use this ability once per day.

The Cavalier adds Knowledge (World) to her list of class skills.

The Cavalier receives a +1 dodge bonus to AC against attacks made by the target of the challenge. This bonus increases by +1 for every four class levels.

When the Cavalier makes a charge attack while mounted, she adds her mount's Strength modifier to her damage rolls alongside her own. She also receives a bonus mounted combat feat. Note that she must satisfy any prerequisites of the selected feat.

At 15th level, the Cavalier can make a Knight's Challenge once per day. This functions like a normal challenge but the Cavalier adds her Charisma bonus to all attack rolls and damage rolls against the target and gains a +4 circumstance bonus to confirm critical hits.

##### Playing a Cavalier
A Cavalier opens up mounted combat from the outset and gives large single-target damage through the Challenge mechanic and boosted charge attacks. While Challenge increases weapon damage by up to +20, it does not in and of itself grant any attack bonuses. This is fixed by modifications to Challenge granted by the Cavalier's order. Note that these are typically morale bonuses and easily replicated (e.g. by *Heroism*).

A Cavalier receives bonus teamwork feats and the ability to grant allies the occasional use of them. As always, this is a weak ability. With three bonus teamwork feats, you're not forced to take anything really bad and if you take the same feats on your mount, you are guaranteed to benefit from them.

When choosing an order, the Order of the Shroud grants some powerful abilities against undead enemies. Alternatively, the Order of the Sword and Order of the Cockatrice grant free feats. The latter gives you Dazzling Display which is otherwise a feat tax for Shatter Defenses.

A Beast Rider is more or less identical to the base Cavalier. You lose proficiency in heavy armour, which is neither here nor there, and receive a choice of animal companion which is better than only being able to select a horse. Unless you're a gnome or halfling, you won't be able to ride your companion until it grows at 7th level.

A halfling Beast Rider could choose a dog mount which is better than choosing Cavalier of the Paw, simply because a choice of order is better than the Order of the Paw.

A Disciple of the Pike loses the animal companion altogether in exchange for some Weapon Training. Being able to choose training in polearms (i.e. glaives and fauchards) makes this archetype one of the stronger Cavalier options.

A Fearsome Leader gives up a bunch of stuff for ... not very much. You can get free Dazzling Display by choosing Order of the Cockatrice. Even with a Good Hope effect, spending a full round using Dazzling Display is a poor use of your time.

A Gendarme makes a very favourable trade of all the Tactician nonsense for five additional combat feats. This makes a Gendarme quite easy to build. Unfortunately, it doesn't gain anything like Weapon Specialization which means that you're not getting much more damage than the base class. This changes at 20th level; triple damage on a charge is quite formidable.

A Ghost Rider's special mount is just a paint job on a normal horse. The abilities that the archetype loses (Tactician and Banner) are nothing special but the class doesn't replace them with anything all that special either. The Frightful Gaze ability which replaces Tactician is a poor use of a front-line fighter's time.

A Knight of the Wall tries to make wielding a shield a thing. It doesn't really work since you don't even receive Greater Shield Focus, for example.

Finally, a Standard Bearer pretends that Banner is something you can build around. Getting your Banner at 1st level and your mount at fifth basically means that you're going to have a rougher time in the early part of the game and then waste a feat on Boon Companion. The capstone ability gives morale bonuses which are easily replicated.

#### Challenge (Order of the Paw)
When the Cavalier issues a Challenge, her companion gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC as long as it is threatening the target and the Cavalier is riding it. This bonus increases by +1 every four levels.

#### Animal Companion (Order of the Paw)
The Cavalier can choose between a Dog and a Wolf.

#### Danger Ward
At 2nd level, as a standard action, the Cavalier can ready all allies within 30' for danger. If an ally fails a saving throw within the next minute, they can reroll it with a +4 competence bonus as an immediate action. She can use this ability three times per day.

#### Canine Ferocity
At 8th level, when the Cavalier uses her mount to perform a bull rush or overrun manoeuvre, it receives a +4 bonus to its CMB. The Cavalier also receives a bonus mounted combat feat. Note that she must satisfy any prerequisites of the selected feat.

#### Giant Slayer
At 15th level, when challenging a creature of large size or greater, the Cavalier gains a bonus to damage rolls equal to half her class level. This damage is multiplied on a critical hit.

#### Bigger They Are
A Disciple of the Pike gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC against creatures larger than she is. This bonus is +2 if the creature is two size categories larger. At 6th level, the bonus increases to +3 when the creature is three size categories larger. Finally, At 12th level, the bonus increases to +4 when the creature is four size categories larger.

#### Pike Charge
At 3rd level, a Disciple of the Pike gains +4 bonus to melee attacks when charging with a polearm or spear and does not take an AC penalty.

#### Monster Hunter
At 4th level, a Monster Hunter gains a bonus equal to her class level on Knowledge (Arcana) and Lore (Nature) checks.

#### Weapon Training (Spears / Polearms)
At 5th level, a Disciple of the Pike can choose to take training in spears (javelin, longspear, shortspear, spear, trident) or polearms (bardiche, fauchard, glaive). Weapon Training gives a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls, increasing by an additional +1 every four levels (max +4 at 17th level).

#### Agile Charger
At 11th level, a Disciple of the Pike can ignore difficult terrain when charging.

#### Deadly Charge
At 20th level, a Disciple of the Pike's charge attack with a spear or polearm does double damage. If she confirms a critical hit, the target is stunned for 1d4 rounds. A Will save (DC = 10 + Cavalier's BAB) reduces this to staggered for 1d4 rounds.

#### Frightful Gaze
As a standard action, a Ghost Rider can fix her gaze on an opponent within 30' causing it to become paralysed for 1 round unless it makes a Will save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + CHA). This is a mind-affecting fear effect and the Ghost Rider can use it a number of times per day equal to her Charisma modifier.

From 9th level, this ability can affect creatures which are immune to fear or mind-affecting effects and creatures making their saving throws are staggered.

#### Ghost Mount
A Ghost Rider's horse is ectoplasm rather than flesh and bone and is immune to bleeding and other effects that require living tissue.

#### Etheric Tether
A Ghost Rider can create a link with her mount as a free action. If her mount's HP would be reduced to 0, it is healed to 1 HP but the rider loses HP equal to the number restored to the mount.

#### Magic Attacks
From 3rd level, a Ghostly Mount's attacks are treated as magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction. From 10th level, they are treated as the same alignment as the Ghost Rider.

#### Spirited Mount
From 5th level, the Ghostly Mount ignores difficult terrain. From 8th level, it gains concealment (20% miss chance).

#### Ghost Wind
At 11th level, the Ghostly Mount's speed increases by +40'. At 14th level, it gains immunity to trip and ground-based effects.

#### Spiritual Bond
At 14th level, a Ghost Rider can create a link with her mount as a free action. If the rider's HP would be reduced to 0, it is healed to 1 HP but the mount loses HP equal to the number restored to the rider. This ability cannot be used at the same time as Etheric Tether.

#### Shared Minds
At 16th level, once per round, when the Ghostly Mount fails a Will saving throw, the Ghost Rider can attempt a Will save against the effect's DC. If the rider's save is successful, the effect is negated.

A Standard Bearer gains a mount at 5th level.

At 11th level, once per day as a full round action, a Standard Bearer can grant allies within 60' temporary HP equal to half her class level for 10 minutes or until depleted. While the temporary HP last, allies gain a +2 morale bonus to damage rolls, increasing to +3 at 15th level and +4 at 19th.
