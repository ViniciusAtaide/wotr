# Alchemist
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Alchemist has the following characteristics:

- Primary attributes: Dexterity and Intelligence
- Medium (3/4) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May use "extracts" to cast spell-like effects up to 6th-level
- Spells are prepared
- 6 HP per level
- High saving throws for Fortitude and Reflex, low saving throws for Will
- 3 skill points per level.

Class Skills: Trickery, Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Perception, Use Magic Device

Until you unlock the Infusion discovery, extracts may only be used on yourself. Alchemist extracts do not count as arcane spells for the purposes of unlocking prestige classes.

#### Alchemist Proficiencies
Alchemists are proficient with simple weapons and bombs. They are also proficient with light armour (but not shields).

#### Bomb
An alchemist can use a number of bombs each day equal to her class level + Intelligence modifier. Bombs have a range of 30' and benefit from feats like Weapon Focus and Point-Blank Shot.

On a direct hit, a bomb inflicts 1d6 fire damage + additional damage equal to her Intelligence modifier. Bomb damage increases by 1d6 every odd-numbered level. Splash damage is equal to the bomb's minimal damage. A Reflex save (DC 10 + class level / 2 + Intelligence modifier) halves the splash damage.

#### Mutagen
The Alchemist can prepare a concoction that enhances his body but diminishes his mental capabilities. It always grants a +2 natural AC bonus and one of the following effects:

- +4 Strength, -2 Intelligence
- +4 Dexterity, -2 Wisdom
- +4 Constitution, -2 Charisma.

The effects of the mutagen last for ten minutes per class level.

#### Discovery
At 2nd level and every subsequent even numbered level, the Alchemist gains a class-specific feat. The DC of any saving throw is equal 10 + class level / 2 + Intelligence modifier.

These are the discoveries that you can make. Note that some require you to be a certain level and have made prior discoveries:

##### Acid Bomb
The bomb inflicts acid damage rather than fire damage. Creatures that receive a direct hit take an additional 1d6 damage 1 round later.

##### Choking Bomb
The bomb releases choking gas. Creatures caught in the blast must make a Fortitude save or become nauseated for 1 round for each die of damage the bomb would normally inflict. Affected creatures may attempt another save each round.

##### Cognatogen
The Alchemist's mutagen may have its effects reversed - for example, +4 Intelligence and -2 Strength. When the effect ends, the Alchemist takes 2 points of ability damage to the score that was penalised.

##### Enhance Potion
Any potion the Alchemist drinks functions at a caster level equal to his class level.

##### Explosive Bombs
The splash radius of the Alchemist's bombs increases to 10'. Creatures that take a direct hit catch fire, taking an additional 1d6 points of fire damage per round.

##### Extend Potion
The effects of potions last for twice as long as normal.

##### Feral Mutagen
When the Alchemist drinks a mutagen, he gains two claw attacks and one bite attack. Attacks are made using his full AB bonus. Claws deal 1d6 damage and the bite deals 1d8 damage. While this effect is active the Alchemist gains a +2 competence bonus on Persuasion (Intimidate) checks.

##### Frost Bomb
The bomb inflicts frost damage rather than fire damage. Creatures that receive a direct hit take are staggered for 1 round unless they succeed on a Fortitude save.

##### Infusion
The Alchemist may use extracts on others. This includes effects that otherwise only affect you anyway, such as Shield,

##### Nauseating Flesh
Any creature that bites the Alchemist must make a Fortitude saving throw or be nauseated for 1d4 rounds.

##### Precise Bomb
The Alchemist's bombs do not affect allies.

##### Preserve Organs
When the Alchemist is subjected to a critical hit or sneak attack, there is a 25% chance that the extra damage is negated. This may be taken up to three times, increasing the chances of negating the effects of critical and sneak attacks to 50% and 75%.

##### Shock Bomb
The bomb inflicts electricity damage rather than fire damage. Creatures that receive a direct hit are dazzled for 1d4 rounds.

##### Spontaneous Healing
The Alchemist may heal 5 HP as a free action and may do so a number of times per day equal to half his class level.

##### Tanglefoot Bombs
The target must make a Reflex save or be entangled and glued to the floor. Creatures in the splash area that fail their saves are merely entangled.

#### Poison Resistance
At 2nd level, the Alchemist gains a +2 bonus to saves vs. poison. This increases to +4 at 5th level and +6 at 8th level.

#### Poison Immunity
At 10th level, the Alchemist becomes immune to poison.

#### Persistent Mutagen
At 14th level, the effects of the mutagen last for 1 hour per class level.

#### Grand Discovery
At 20th level, the Alchemist makes two normal discoveries and something awesome:

##### Awakened Intellect
Gain +2 points of Intelligence.

##### Fast Healing
Gain Fast Healing 5.

##### True Mutagen
Requires Grand Mutagen. Your Mutagen adds +8 to all physical abilities.

##### Playing an Alchemist
The short answer is "don't" - play a Grenadier instead. Precise Bombs is one of the first discoveries you would take so getting it for free effectively gives you a two-level lead. The Grenadier works best as a single class - closing off the possibility of a Grand Discovery is a mistake. Something to note is that there is very little alchemist gear in the game - nothing that boosts your bombs, for example.

The Chirurgeon is not as good as a Grenadier but slightly better than the base class: trading poison immunity for a Skill Focus is good because poison immunity is easily acquired.

Similar to a Chirurgeon, a Preservationist plays like the base class. You have no poison resistance at all but gain summoning extracts. However, these are weak until *Bottled Ally V* at level 14 which is a long time to wait.

The Incense Synthesizer is very different, trading the signature Mutagen for the ability to create AoE buffs and debuffs. Greater Sacred Incense is interesting because it can apply the nauseated status to poison-immune enemies. The alchemical attack and damage bonuses from the standard incense stack with other sources. However, the use is too limited (maximum 20 rounds per day or thereabouts) to recommend the class.

A Metamorph gives up bombs entirely in exchange for the ability to assume beast forms. This is OK, but a Vivisectionist is a much better close-up damage dealer.

A Reanimator plays like the base class. You're unlikely to see much benefit from the Corpse Studying ability because the greatly reduced bomb damage makes the Reanimator very much a support character. The boosted Animate Dead / Create Undead are OK, but this is a class you'd only play because you like the word "reanimator".

Like the Grenadier, the Vivisectionist is also very powerful as a pure class. Because you don't throw bombs, you don't need to pump up Intelligence; 16 (or 18 if you're going to abuse Mutagens) is enough. Since you can take most feats as discoveries through Combat Trick, the class is very feat-rich. Vivisectionist is also an overpowered 1-level dip, granting Mutagen and a d6 sneak attack.

#### Alchemical Weapon
At 2nd level, the Grenadier may infuse a bomb or other weapon with an alchemical liquid or powder such as an acid flask as a standard action. At 6th level this becomes a swift action and at 15th level it becomes a free action.

#### Directed Blast
At 6th level, the Grenadier may detonate a bomb as a 30' cone.

#### Staggering Blast
At 10th level, whenever a Grenadier scores a successful critical hit with a bomb, the creature taking the direct hit is staggered for 1d4+1 rounds. A successful Fortitude save (DC 10 + class level / 2 + Intelligence modifier) reduces the duration to 1 round.

#### Adaptive Physiology
At 3rd level, the character gains a 25% chance to negate critical hits and precision damage. This increases to 50% chance at 6th level and 75% chance at 18th level.

#### Shapechanger
At 4th level, the metamorph gains the ability to change into a different form. This lasts for 1 hour per class level and can be used twice a day. The number of uses increases by 1 for each 2 additional class levels (i.e. three times at 6th level increasing to ten times at 20th level).

#### Bomb (Reanimator)
A Reaminator's bombs deal base 1d4 damage and there is no damage increase at 5th, 11th and 15th levels.

#### Corpse Studying
After killing an enemy, a Reanimator gains a +1 alchemical bonus to attack and damage rolls until the end of combat. At 5th level and every 6 additional levels, this bonus increases by +1 (maximum +4 at 17th level).

#### Simple Reanimation
At 5th level, a Reanimator adds Animate Dead, Lesser to his formula book as a 2nd level extract. The undead created by the spell gain a +4 alchemical bonus to Strength and Charisma.

#### Improved Reanimation
At 11th level, a Reanimator adds Create Undead to his formula book as a 4th level extract. Undead creatures raised by the Reanimator gain a +6 alchemical bonus to Strength and Charisma.

#### Greater Reanimation
At 15th level, a Reanimator adds Create Greater Undead to his formula book as a 5th level extract. Undead creatures raised by the Reanimator gain a +6 alchemical bonus to Strength and Charisma and can make one extra attack per round.
