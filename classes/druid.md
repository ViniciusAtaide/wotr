# Druid
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Druid has the following characteristics:

- Primary attribute: Wisdom
- Medium (3/4) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast divine spells up to 9th-level
- Spells must be prepared in advance
- 8 HP at level 1, then 5 HP per level (d8 hit die)
- High saving throws for Fortitude and Will, low saving throw for Reflex
- 3 skill points per level
- Gains an Animal Companion or may choose a domain instead.
- Alignment must be within one place of Neutral.

Class Skills: Athletics, Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Perception.

If you choose a domain, you can choose from: Air, Animal, Earth, Fire, Ice, Plant, Water and Weather.

If you choose a domain, you can't take the Impossible Domain mythic ability.

#### Druid Proficiencies
Druids are proficient with the club, dagger, dart, quarterstaff, scimitar, scythe, sickle, shortspear, sling and spear as well as natural attacks with the forms they assume with wild shape. They are proficient with light and medium armor and shields, but they are prohibited from wearing metal armor and may only use wooden shields.

#### Spontaneous Summoning
A Druid may lose any prepared spell and cast a Summon Nature's Ally spell of the same level in its place.

#### Nature Sense
A Druid gains a +2 bonus on Lore (Nature) checks.

#### Woodland Stride
At 2nd level, a Druid can move through difficult terrain at normal speed, suffering no damage or other impairment.

#### Resist Nature's Lure
At 4th level, a Druid gains a +4 bonus on saving throws against the spell-like and supernatural abilities of fey and plants.

#### Wild Shape
Starting at 4th level, a Druid may change herself into a wolf and back again once per day for 1 hour per druid level. Changing form is a standard action and does not provoke an attack of opportunity. A Druid can use her Wildshape ability an additional time for every two levels above 4th level up to maximum of 8 times at 18th level. As she gains levels, she can assume progressively more powerful forms.

#### Venom Immunity
At 9th level, a Druid is immune to all forms of poison.

##### Playing a Druid
Druid proficiencies are very debilitating. Slings aren't even implemented in-game which means that the only thing keeping a druid from the front line are darts which are few and far between. They cannot use reach weapons and are limited to leather or hide armor which doesn't offer much in the way of protection.

You'll want to take Animal Companion rather than a domain. Yes, you *can* select Animal domain but then you'll have to wait for your pet, making the early game harder, and waste a feat on Boon Companion instead of something useful.

One problem with the class is that it pretends to be martial-capable through wild shaping. However, this simply isn't true: the Strength bonus is modest, the natural AC bonus won't be high enough to solve the problem of your catastrophically poor defense and natural attacks do low damage and have bad critical hit parameters. Wild Shape is weak on any class with the feature but weakest on a Druid. If you *are* going to use it, you'll have to waste a feat on Natural Spell.

The Blight Druid gives up the very valuable animal companion for some questionable class abilities, including one that requires you to be hit which is never something you want.

The Defender of the True World is in the wrong game. Strong abilities against an entire category of enemies are no use if you don't fight them.

A Drovier gives up the Wild Shape ability which is no great loss. However, it trades it for a weak group buffing ability which is no great shakes.

The Elemental Rampager gives up the companion for further pretence of being a frontliner. If you think you're going to get damage output comparable to a raging Barbarian, you will be disappointed. The attack and damage bonuses during rampage and the extra damage dice from Elemental Rampage make Wild Shape less weak but it's still not strong. If you are planning on playing a Rampager, the Chain Discharge ability from Electricity Rampage appears to be the best.

The Feyspeaker gives up a lot for the ability to cast 8 Wizard spells, including AB progression. At least there's no pretence of being frontline-capable and the improved spellbook makes it better than the base class. If you pretend that Wild Shape isn't a thing, you don't need 13 Wisdom and you can play the class as a cut-down Sylvan Sorcerer with better saving throws. A Gnome is the ideal racial choice.

A Primal Druid is limited in her choice of animal companions but if you were planning on taking a smilodon anyway, that's no loss. The class-specific *Enlarge Person* spell is another pretence of being frontline-capable.

A Winter Child has a couple of things going for her: despite coming late, the Blizzard Servant companion is quite powerful and later on, she can convert all elemental damage to cold which has decent gear affinity, notably the Coldbite light shield and the Armor of Elemental Carnage. Finally, the 20th level capstone grants a ton of immunities, making the Winter Child one of the better Druid archetypes.

#### Miasma
At 5th level, if a Blight Druid is adjacent to a creature at the beginning of its turn, the creature must make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + half class level + Wisdom modifier) or become sickened for 1 round. Animals, fey and plants are nauseated for 1 round and sickened for one minute. If the creature makes its save, it becomes immune to the Miasma ability. Miasma has no effect on creatures immune to disease.

#### Blightblooded
At 9th level. the Blight Druid gains immunity to all diseases and any effects that cause him to become sickened or nauseated.

#### Plaguebearer
At 13th level, any creature that strikes a Blight Druid with a touch attack, unarmed strike or natural weapon must make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + half class level + Wisdom modifier) or become nauseated for 1 round and contract bubonic plague (1d4 Constitution damage, fatigued). If the creature makes its save, it becomes immune to the Plaguebearer ability.

#### Enemy of the Fey
A Defender of the True World gains +2 bonus on weapon attack and damage rolls against fey. In addition, she may sacrifice a prepared spell of 7th level or lower to cast any Summon Nature's Ally spell of the same level. When she is able to cast 8th spells, she may sacrifice an 8th level spell to summon an Elder Earth Elemental and when she is able to cast 9th level spells, she may sacrifice a 9th level spell to summon an Elder Worm.

#### Fey Stalker
At 3rd level, the Defender of the True World's animal companion and any creatures summoned with Summon Nature's Ally gain a +1 morale bonus on attack and damage rolls against fey. This bonus increases at 8th, 13th and 18th levels.

#### Feybane
At 4th level, the Defender of the True World gains a +4 bonus on saving throws against the spell-like and supernatural abilities of the fey. Her natural attacks count as cold iron for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction and she gains a +2 bonus on caster level checks to overcome the spell resistance of fey creatures.

#### Beguiling Immunity
At 9th level, the Defender of the True World becomes immune to the mind-affecting effects of the fey.

At 4th level, a Drovier can grant the effects of the Animal Aspect spell to all allies within 30' for 10 minutes per class level per day. At 6th level, this is upgraded to Animal Aspect, Greater. #### Claws
At 1st level, the Rampager gains a 1d6 claw attack. At 9th level, the damage increases to 1d8 and finally at 15th level, the damage increases to 1d10.

#### Rampage
At 1st level the Druid can rampage for a number of rounds per day equal to 4 + her Constitution modifier. For each class level after 1st, she can rampage for 2 additional rounds per day. The Rampage ability benefits from the Limitless Rage mythic ability.

While rampaging, the Druid gains +2 to attack and damage rolls with natural weapons along with a -2 penalty to AC. The attack and damage bonuses increase by +1 at 6th level and every five levels thereafter (max +5 at 16th level).

#### Elemental Rampage
At 4th level, the Rampager can add an elemental aspect to her rage, adding energy damage to her attacks, gaining energy resistance and immunity along with abilities at 11th and 20th level.

##### Elemental Damage
Your natural attacks deal 1d6 damage of the chosen element. This increases to 2d6 at 11th level and 3d6 at 20th level.

##### Energy Resistance
At 4th level, you gain resistance 5 against the chosen element. At 9th level this increase to 10 and at 15th level to 20. Finally at 20th level, you become immune to the element.

#### Acid Rampage
##### Sickening Acid
At 11th level, every time you attack an enemy with natural attacks, the enemy must make a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + Constitution modifier) or become sickened until a successful saving throw is made.

##### Putrid Acid
At 20th level, sickened enemies force all enemies within 5' to make the same saving throw or become sickened as well.

#### Cold Rampage
##### Entangling Cold
At 11th level, every time you attack an enemy with natural attacks, the enemy must make a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + Constitution modifier) or become entangled until a successful saving throw is made.

##### Freezing Cold
At 20th level, entangled enemies force all enemies within 5' to make the same saving throw or become entangled as well.

#### Electricity Rampage
##### Chain Discharge
At 11th level, every time you attack an enemy with natural attacks, you deal your additional elemental damage to two more enemies within 15'.

##### Greater Chain Discharge
At 20th level, every time you attack an enemy with natural attacks, you deal your additional elemental damage to four more enemies within 15'.

#### Fire Rampage
##### Burn
At 11th level, every time you attack an enemy with natural attacks, the enemy must make a Reflex save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + Constitution modifier) or start burning, receiving 2d6 fire damage each round.

##### Scorching Heat
At 20th level, burning enemies deal the burn damage to all other enemies within 5'.

#### Ice in the Blood
A Winter Child must select the Ice Subdomain.

#### Call a Feast
All creatures summoned by a Winter Child gain cold resistance 5. This increases to 10 at 10th level and the creatures gain the effects of the *Blink* spell. At 15th level, cold resistance increases to 15 and the creatures' attacks deal an additional 2d6 cold damage.

#### Blizzard Servant
At 4th level, a Winter Child forms a bond with a Blizzard Servant which functions like an animal companion. The Winter Child's effective druid level is her class level - 3.

You must choose a companion archetype for the Blizzard Servant and it benefits from feats and abilities that affect animal companions, such as Boon Companion and Mythical Beast. Besides the attribute increases at 6th, 11th and 16th level, they gain attribute increases on leveling up just like any other animal companion.

#### Arctic Native
At 4th level, a Winter Child gains cold resistance 5 and is immune to being dazzled. The cold resistance increases by +5 every four additional class levels (maximum 25 at 20th level).

#### Blizzard Servant - Winter's Advance
At 6th level, a Winter Child's companion grows to medium size, gaining a +2 bonus to Strength, +4 bonus to Dexterity, +2 bonus to Constitution and cold resistance 10.

#### Snowcaster
From 9th level, a Winter Child can convert the damage type of her spells to cold as a swift action.

#### Blizzard Servant - Crystallized Affinity
At 11th level, a Winter Child's companion grows to large size, gaining a +4 bonus to Strength, +4 bonus to Dexterity, +2 bonus to Constitution, a +2 natural armor bonus to AC, DR 5/- and cold resistance 20. Its Whirlwind of Hail no longer affects allies.

#### Blizzard Servant - Bound Souls
At 16th level, the companion grows again to huge size, gaining a +4 bonus to Strength, +6 bonus to Dexterity, +2 bonus to Constitution, a +2 natural armor bonus to AC, the numbing cold ability and cold resistance 30. When an ally hits an enemy affected by the servant's Whirlwind of Hail ability, the targets takes +2d6 cold damage.

#### Flurry Form
At 20th level, a Winter Child gains the elemental subtype and immunity to cold. She also gains immunity to ground-based effects and trip. She is treated as a native outsider when determining the effects of spells and spell-like abilities.
