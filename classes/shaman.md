# Shaman
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Shaman has the following characteristics:

- Primary attributes: Wisdom, Charisma
- Average (3/4) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast divine spells up to 9th-level
- Spells must be prepared in advance
- 8 HP at level 1, then 5 HP per level (d8 hit die)
- High saving throws for Will, low saving throws for Fortitude and Reflex
- 2 skill points per level.

Class Skills: Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Lore (Religion), Persuasion

#### Shaman Proficiencies
Shamans are proficient with simple weapons and light and medium armour.

#### Spirit Animal
A Shaman must select a familiar.

#### Spirit
A Shaman must choose a guiding spirit to bond with. See the Spirits section below for the choices. The spirit confers abilities at 1st, 8th and 16th levels.

#### Wandering Spirit
From 4th level, the Shaman may form a temporary bond with a second spirit. Unlike the first choice of spirit, the choice is not fixed and can be changed each time you rest. While bonded to the spirit, you temporarily gain the associated ability. At 12th and 20th levels, you also gain the Greater and True abilities of the Wandering Spirit.

#### Hex
A Shaman may select basic hexes from the Witch's hex list along with Shaman-specific hexes and special hexes granted by his guiding spirit. Whenever a saving throw is required, the Shaman uses his Wisdom bonus in the calculation.

The following hexes are shared with the Witch: Ameliorating, Aura of Purity, Evil Eye, Fortune, Healing, Iceplant, Misfortune, Nails, Protective Luck, Slumber and Ward.

##### Air Barrier
Prerequisites:
Wind

The Shaman creates a barrier that grants him a +4 armor bonus to AC. At 7th level and every 4 levels thereafter, the bonus increases by +2 (+12 at 19th level). At 13th level, the barrier causes incoming rays, arrows and other ranged attacks requiring an attack roll to have a 50% miss chance. The barrier lasts for one hour per class level.

##### Battle Master
Prerequisites:
Battle

The Shaman can make an additional attack of opportunity each round and this ability stacks with Combat Reflexes. At 8th level, the Shaman gains Weapon Specialization in a weapon of choice. At 16th level, the Shaman gains Greater Weapon Focus in the chosen weapon.

##### Battle Ward
Prerequisites:
Battle

The touched creature is granted a ward granting it a +3 deflection bonus to AC. Each time the warded creature is attacked, the AC bonus decreases by -1 and the effect expires when the bonus is reduced to 0. At 8th level, the starting deflection bonus is +4 and at 16th level it is +5.

##### Beckoning Chill
Prerequisites:
Waves

The Shaman causes a creature within 30' to become susceptible to cold for 1 minute. When an affected creature takes cold damage, it becomes entangled for 1 round. If it receives cold damage while entangled, the duration of the entangled condition increases by 1 round.

##### Bone Lock
Prerequisites:
Bones

The Shaman causes a creature within 30' to be staggered for 1 round with the target receiving a Fortitude save to negate the effect. At 8th level, the staggered condition lasts for a number of rounds equal to the Shaman's class level. An affected creature receives a saving throw every round to shake off the condition. At 16th level, the target only receives the initial saving throw.

##### Bone Ward
Prerequisites:
Bones

The warded creature receives a +2 deflection bonus to AC that lasts a number of rounds equal to the Shaman's class level. At 8th level, the bonus increases to +3 and lasts for 1 minute. At 16th level, the bonus increases to +4 and lasts for 1 hour.

##### Chant
This is identical to the Witch's Cackle ability.

For rules-lawterly reasons, Chant does not prolong Protective Luck. Chant only works with Shaman hexes and Protective Luck is a Witch hex that a Shaman can select. I'm sure that this makes sense to someone.

##### Draconic Resilience
The touched creature is immune to sleep for a number of rounds equal to the Shaman's class level. At 7th level, the creature is also immune to paralysis.

##### Entangling Curse
Prerequisites:
Nature

The Shaman causes a creature within 30' to become entangled for a number of rounds equal to his charisma modifier. A successful Reflex save negates the effect.

##### Fearful Gaze
Prerequisites:
Bones

The Shaman causes a target creature within 30' to become shaken for one round. The target is allowed a Will save to resist the effect. At 8th level, the Shaman makes the target frightened while at 16th level, he makes that target panic. This is mind-affecting for the purposes of determining immunity.

##### Fire Nimbus
Prerequisites:
Flame

The Shaman causes a creature within 30' to glow, preventing it from gaining any benefit from concealment or invisibility. The target also receives a -2 penalty to saves against fire. The nimbus lasts for a number of rounds equal to the Shaman's class level and the target is allowed a Will saving throw to resist the effect.

##### Flame Curse
Prerequisites:
Flame

The Shaman causes a creature within 30' to become vulnerable to fire until the end of the Shaman's next turn. At 8th level and 16th level, the duration of the effect increases by 1 round.

##### Friend to Animals
Prerequisites:
Nature

The Shaman can spontaneously cast Summon Nature's Ally spells as if he were a Druid. Animals within 30' of the Shaman receive a sacred bonus to saving throws equal to the Shaman's Charisma modifier.

##### Fury
The target receives a +2 morale bonus to attack rolls and a +2 resistance bonus to saving throws against fear for a number of rounds equal to the Shaman's Wisdom modifier. At 8th and 16th levels, the bonuses increase by an additional +1.

##### Hampering Hex
Prerequisites:
Battle

The Shaman causes a creature within 30' to take a -2 penalty to AC and CMD for a number of rounds equal to the Shaman's level. A successful Will saving throw reduces the duration to 1 round. At 8th level, the penalty becomes -4.

##### Hypothermia
Prerequisites:
Frost

The target creature must make a Fortitude save or become fatigued for 2 rounds. At 8th level and 16th level, the duration of the effect increases by 1 round.

##### Intimidating Display
The Shaman gains the Dazzling Display feat and does not need to wield a weapon to use it.

##### Metal Curse
Prerequisites:
Stone

The target creature suffers a -2 AC penalty to metal weapons until the end of the Shaman's next turn. At 8th level and 16th level, the duration of the effect increases by 1 round and the penalty increases by -2.

##### Secret
The Shaman receives a metamagic feat.

##### Stone Stability
Prerequisites:
Stone

The Shaman receives a +4 bonus to his CMD when resisting bull rush or trip attempts. At 5th level, the Shaman gains Trip as a bonus feat and Greater Trip at 10th level.

##### Vortex Spells
Prerequisites:
Wind

Whenever the Shaman confirms a critical hit with a spell, the target is staggered for 1 round. At 11th level, the duration increases to 1d4 rounds.

##### Ward of Flame
Prerequisites:
Flame

When the recipient of the ward is struck by a melee attack, the attacker receives 1d6 fire damage + half the Shaman's class level. The effect lasts for 1 minute or until the recipient is struck. At 8th and 16th levels, the effect lasts for one more attack.

##### Wind Ward
Prerequisites:
Wind

Ranged attacks requiring an attack roll against the recipient of the ward have a 20% miss chance. The effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to the Shaman's class level. At 8th level, the duration increases to 1 minute per level and at 16th level, the miss chance increases to 50%.

#### Wandering Hex
At 6th and 14th levels, a Shaman gains a hex slot that can be temporarily filled with a hex granted by either his main spirit or his current Wandering Spirit.

#### Manifestation
At 20th level, a Shaman fully unlocks the power of his guardian spirit.

##### Playing a Shaman
A Shaman plays similarly to a Cleric since both are 3/4 AB classes with full caster progression and spirits are similar to domains. Choosing the Battle spirit can instantly turn you into a capable warrior while the Wind Spirit can give you a decent amount of tankiness. Alternatively, the Life spirit can turn you into a capable healer. With the Wandering Spirit ability, you can interchange these depending on the most pressing need. The Wandering Spirit feature and the full allotment of hexes makes the base Shaman one of the better options.

Although Wisdom is your casting attribute, Charisma determines how often you can use most spirit abilities. Since some of the abilities are very good (Enemies' Bane, for example), this makes the Shaman slightly MAD. However, until the later levels at least, the Shaman's spell book is more geared towards support than taking out enemies so you may wish to deprioritise Wisdom.

A Possessed Shaman loses three hexes to gain ranks in skills. Skills make the game more pleasant. However, a Shaman is an unskilled class so you're starting from a low base. If you wanted to make a skilled character, a half-elf Bard might be a better starting point.

A Prophet of Pestilence gives up a whole bunch of class features for the ability to infect enemies with an ever-worsening disease. The problem with disease attacks is that they're asymmetric. They're seriously annoying when used against you because you expect to survive the fight and repairing ability damage is a pain. However, doing ability damage to enemies is less effective because the only really effective enemy status is "dead" and anything that doesn't help make them dead is a waste of time.

A Shadow Shaman gives up the Wandering Spirit ability for 6d6 sneak damage which isn't a bad trade-off. Use the Second Spirit mythic ability to get whichever of Wind and Battle that you didn't take at the start and you have everything: Weapon Specialization, sneak damage and great defence. You won't be the *best* at everything but you'll be pretty decent.

A Spirit Hunter gives up Wandering Spirit for the ability to enchant weapons. It's not *bad* but it's not amazing either. A Spirit Warden keeps Wandering Spirit but gives up two hexes for - pretty much - nothing.

An Unsworn Shaman has a minor advantage that all hex slots are Wandering Hex slots. However, fundamentally the archetype simply delays spirit abilities by two levels.

The Wildland Shaman forces you to take the Battle Spirit. Since you'd probably take Battle anyway, this is no big imposition. You also give up Wandering Spirit but in return you get an animal companion. Yes, you can get an animal companion with the Nature spirit but that's not until level 16 (or even 20) which is a long time to wait. Half-Orc is an OK racial choice for a Shaman because Orc Weapon Familiarity gets around proficiency limitations without having to burn a feat. Note that a Wildland Shaman cannot take the Second Spirit mythic ability.

Finally, the Witch Doctor is OK. You lose two hexes for one decent ability (Counter Curse) and an underpowered channel energy. I prefer the base Shaman, myself.

#### Shared Skills
At 1st level, the Shaman selects two skills. His effective rank is equal to his class level and he uses his Wisdom modifier in place of the modifier the skill would normally use. This does not stack with any points the Shaman takes on levelling up.

This replaces Spirit Magic which means that your spirit won't give you additional spells.

#### Crowded Vessel
Whenever the Shaman fails a saving throw against a charm or compulsion effect, he can attempt another saving throw and if this one succeeds, the effect is resisted.

#### Wandering Skills
When the Shaman forms a temporary spirit bond, he can choose one skill. His effective rank in that skill is equal to his class level and he uses his Wisdom modifier in place of the modifier the skill would normally use. He can choose different skill each time he rests.

#### Pestilence Spirit
Only a Prophet of Pestilence may bond with the Pestilence Spirit, gaining unique abilities

#### Plague's Caress
You gain a melee touch attack that causes the target to become sickened for a number of rounds equal to your class level / 2. This is a disease effect and you can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.

#### Plague of Abaddon
From 2nd level, a Prophet of Pestilence can infect a creature failing a Fortitude save with plague. The plague lasts a number of rounds equal to the shaman's class level or until the target succeeds at a Fortitude save. This is a disease effect.

#### Carrier of Disease
At 2nd level, and every four subsequent levels, a Prophet of Pestilence can select a mutation for the Plague of Abaddon.

#### Mutating Spirit
At 4th level, a Prophet of Pestilence becomes immune to the sickened condition and gains a +4 bonus to saving throws against effects that cause disease or the nauseated condition.

At 12th level, he becomes immune to the nauseated condition, poison and disease. In addition, he gains a +2 bonus to the DC of any disease effects.

#### Rapid Spread
From 8th level, an enemy attacking you in melee without a reach weapon must make a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + WIS) or be affected by the Plague of Abaddon. In addition the range of Plague of Abaddon increases to 30'.

#### Fatal Disease
From 16th level, any creature attempting a saving throw against one of your spells or abilities with the disease descriptor must roll twice and take the worse result.

#### A Thousand Diseases
At 20th level any enemies within 30' must make a Fortitude save or be affected by the Plague of Abaddon. Enemies that are already diseased do not receive a saving throw.

#### Sneak Attack
At 3rd level a Shadow Shaman's attacks deal an extra +1d6 damage when his opponent is denied Dexterity bonus or when he flanks his opponent. This increases by +1d6 every three additional levels, to a maximum +6d6 at 18th level.

#### Shadows
At the 4th level, a Shadow Shaman can gain the benefits of the *Blur* spell a number of times per day equal to his Charisma modifier. At 9th level, he gains the benefits of *Displacement* instead and at 14th level, he receives the effect of *Greater Invisibility*.

### Spirits
A Shaman's choice of Spirit grants him abilities, bonus spells and special hexes.

#### Battle
A Shaman who selects the Battle spirit gains the following abilities:

##### Battle Spirit
Allies within 30' of the Shaman receive a +1 morale bonus to attack and damage rolls. At 8th and 16th levels, these bonuses increase by +1. The Shaman can use this ability for a number of rounds per day equal to 3 + his Charisma modifier.

##### Enemies' Bane
At 8th level, as a swift action, the Shaman imbues a weapon she is holding with the Bane Weapon ability against a chosen creature type for 1 minute. If the weapon already has the bane ability against the chosen creature type, the bonus damage increases to +4d6. The Shaman can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + his Charisma modifier.

##### Paragon of Battle
At 16th level, as a standard action, the Shaman assumes a form combining the effects of *Enlarge Person* and *Divine Power* for 1 minute. The Shaman can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + his Charisma modifier.

##### Manifestation (Battle)
At 20th level, the Shaman gains the pounce ability and when confirming a critical hit, ignores the target's DR. When an enemy attempts to confirm a critical hit, the Shaman gains a +4 insight bonus to AC for the check. Finally, the Shaman's maximum number of HP increases by twice his Constitution modifier.
