# Warpriest
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Warpriest has the following characteristics:

- Primary attributes: Strength, Constitution and Wisdom
- Medium (3/4) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast divine spells up to 6th-level
- Spells are prepared
- 8 HP at level 1, then 5 HP per level (d8 hit die)
- High saving throws for Fortitude and Will, low saving throws for Reflex
- 2 skill points per level.

Class Skills: Athletics, Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Lore (Religion), Persuasion

#### Warpriest Proficiencies
Warpriests are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, all types of armour and shields (except tower shields).

For the purposes of unlocking class-specific feats, Warpriest levels count as Fighter levels. Even though Warpriest is a 3/4 BAB class, it counts as full BAB for the purpose of unlocking feats with a BAB prerequisite.

#### Bonus Combat Feat
At 3rd level and every three subsequent levels, the Warpriest may select any combat feat, assuming he meets the prerequisites.

#### Blessings
A Warpriest can select two Blessings from among those granted by the domains of his chosen deity. Each Blessing grants a minor power at 1st level and a major power at 10th level. A Warpriest can use these powers three times per day + 1 per two class levels (maximum 13 at 20th level). If a power requires a saving throw, the DC is 10 + class level / 2 + the Warpriest's Wisdom modifier.

##### Air Domain
At 1st level, you can touch an ally and for one minute, his ranged attacks don't provoke an attack of opportunity.

At 10th level, you can grant an ally the ability to fly (as the Wings feat) for 1 minute. When the ally succeeds at a charge attack, he deals an additional amount of electricity damage equal to your class level.

##### Animal Domain
At 1st level, you can grant an ally two claw attacks for 1 minute. The claws deal 1d6 damage (or 1d4 if the ally is small).

At 10th level, you can summon an ally (as *Summon Nature's Ally V*) for 1 minute. Every two additional levels, the strength of the summon increases (up to *Summon Nature's Ally IX* at 18th level).

##### Chaos Domain
At 1st level you can imbue an ally with chaos for 1 minute. His attacks deal an additional 1d6 damage against lawful creatures and his attacks count as chaotic for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction.

At 10th level, you can summon an ally (as *Summon Monster IV*) for 1 minute. Every two additional levels, the strength of the summon increases (up to *Summon Monster IX* at 20th level).

##### Darkness Domain
At 1st level, you can grant an ally 20% concealment for 1 minute.

At 10th level, you can blind an enemy within 30' for 1 minute unless they make a Will save.

##### Death Domain
At 1st level, you can take on a frightening appearance for 1 minute granting you a +4 bonus on Intimidate checks and a +2 profane bonus to saves against diseases, mind-affecting effects, paralysis, poison and stun.

At 10th level, you can make a melee touch attack which inflicts 1 negative level on the target for 1 minute. Alternatively, as a swift action, you can make your next melee attack apply the same effect.

##### Destruction
At 1st level, you can grant an ally a morale bonus on damage rolls equal to half your class level for 1 minute.

At 10th level, you can grant an ally a +4 insight bonus on attack rolls to confirm critical hits. Critical hits or sneak attacks made against the blessed ally have a 50% chance of being treated as normal hits.

##### Earth
At 1st level, you can bless a weapon to do an additional 1d4 acid damage for 1 minute.

At 10th level, you can grant an ally DR 1/- for 1 minute. For every two additional levels the DR increases by +1 (maximum DR 5/- at 18th level).

##### Evil Domain
At 1st level you can imbue an ally with evil for 1 minute. His attacks deal an additional 1d6 damage against good creatures and his attacks count as evil for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction.

At 10th level, you can summon an ally (as *Summon Monster IV*) for 1 minute. Every two additional levels, the strength of the summon increases (up to *Summon Monster IX* at 20th level).

##### Fire
At 1st level, you can bless a weapon to do an additional 1d4 acid damage for 1 minute.

At 10th level, you can grant an ally cold resistance equal to your class level for 1 minute.

##### Good Domain
At 1st level you can imbue an ally with good for 1 minute. His attacks deal an additional 1d6 damage against evil creatures and his attacks count as good for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction.

At 10th level, you can summon an ally (as *Summon Monster IV*) for 1 minute. Every two additional levels, the strength of the summon increases (up to *Summon Monster IX* at 20th level).

##### Healing Domain
At 1st level, as a swift action, you can make your next cure spell empowered.

At 10th level, you can grant an ally fast healing 3 for 1 minute.

##### Law Domain
At 1st level you can imbue an ally with law for 1 minute. His attacks deal an additional 1d6 damage against chaotic creatures and his attacks count as lawful for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction.

At 10th level, as a swift action, you can grant all allies within 30' a +4 sacred bonus to AC and saving throws against chaotic opponents.

##### Liberation
At 1st level, as a swift action, you can grant yourself the benefits of *Freedom of Movement* for 1 round.

At 10th level, as a swift action, you can grant all allies within 30' the benefits of *Freedom of Movement* for 1 round.

##### Luck
At 1st level, you can touch an ally and allow them to roll twice on ability checks, attack rolls, saving throws and skill checks for 1 round.

At 10th level, you can curse an enemy, forcing it to take the worse of two rolls on ability checks, attack rolls, saving throws and skill checks for 1 round.

##### Madness
At 1st level, as a swift action, target a frightened, panicked or paralysed creature within 30' and make them confused for 1 round instead. The confused action will be "attack self" or "attack nearest enemy".

At 10th level, you can direct the behaviour of all confused creatures within 30' for 1 round (so that, for example. all affected creatures attack themselves).

##### Magic
At 1st level, you can throw your melee weapon at a target within 30', adding your Wisdom modifier to the attack roll.

At 10th level, as a swift action, the Warpriest can recall any spell that he has cast.

##### Nobility
At 1st level, you can grant an ally a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls, skill checks and saving throws for 1 minute.

At 10th level, all allies within 30' gain a +4 morale bonus on attack rolls, skill checks and saving throws for 1 round.

##### Protection
At 1st level, you gain a +1 sacred bonus to AC and saving throws for 1 minute. This bonus increases to +2 at 10th level and +3 at 20th level.

At 10th level, all allies within 30' gain 10 resistance against acid, cold, electricity, fire and sonic damage for 1 minute. At 15th level, the energy resistance increases to 20.

##### Repose
At 1st level, you can strike a target with a melee touch attack and cause it to become staggered for 1 round. If the target is already staggered, it falls asleep for 1 round. Undead creatures are staggered for a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier.

At 10th level, as a swift action, when using channel energy to heal creatures you can deal damage to undead creatures simultaneously. The damage inflicted is equal to half the amount of damage healed.

##### Strength
At 1st level, gain an enhancement bonus equal to half your class level on all rolls that rely on Strength (attack rolls, combat manoeuvre checks, skill checks, ability checks) for 1 round.

At 10th level, ignore movement penalties caused by wearing medium or heavy armour for 1 minute. During this time, add your Strength modifier to saving throws against effects that would cause you to be become staggered, entangled or paralysed.

##### Sun
At 1st level, you can blind an opponent for 1 round. If it makes a Reflex save, it is dazzled instead.

At 10th level, you can imbue a weapon with the flaming or undead bane abilities for 1 minute. You can spend two blessings and apply both properties.

##### Travel
At 1st level, you can ignore difficult terrain for 1 round.

At 10th level, as a move action, you can teleport up to 20'.

##### Trickery
At 1st level, you can create an illusory duplicate which lasts a number of rounds equal to your class level or until destroyed.

At 10th level, as a swift action, you can gain the effects of *Greater Invisibility* for 1 round.

##### Water
At 1st level, you can bless a weapon to do an additional 1d4 cold damage for 1 minute.

At 10th level, you can grant an ally fire resistance equal to your class level for 1 minute.

##### Weather
At 1st level, you can bless a weapon to do an additional 1d4 electricity damage for 1 minute.

At 10th level, you can create a barrier lasting for 1 minute that causes ranged attacks against you to have a miss chance equal to twice your class level.

#### Spontaneous Casting
You may choose the ability to spontaneously convert spells to "cure" spells of the same level or "inflict" spells of the same level.

#### Fervor
The Warpriest can use this ability on an ally as a standard action or on himself as a swift action. A Warpriest who worships a good deity can heal 1d6 points of damage +1d6 per 3 additional class levels (maximum 7d6 at level 20). A Warpriest who worships an evil deity instead makes a melee touch attack to harm living creatures by the same amount. Warpriests of neutral deities heal or harm depending on their Spontaneous Casting choice. Against undead, the effect will harm (good) or heal (evil). Using the ability to harm provokes an attack of opportunity.

Rather than using Fervor's heal / harm ability, a Warpriest may instead expend one use of Fervor to cast any prepared spell on *himself* as a swift action.

A Warpriest can use this ability a number of times per day equal to half his class level + his Wisdom modifier.

#### Channel Energy
From 4th level, a Warpriest can channel energy at a cost of two uses of Fervor. He heals (or inflicts) 1d6 damage + 1d6 for every 3 class levels above 2nd (maximum 7d6 at 20th level).

#### Sacred Weapon
At 1st level, a Warpriest gains *Weapon Focus* as a bonus feat in a weapon of his choosing. Alongside, his deity's favoured weapon, this weapon becomes blessed and the weapon does damage as a function of class level, as follows:

| Lvl | Damage |

| 1 | 1d6 |
| 5 | 1d8 |
| 10 | 2d6 |
| 15 | 2d8 |

#### Sacred Armor
From 7th level, a Warpriest can additionally enchant his armour for a total number of minutes per day equal to his class level. As an alternative to an enhancement bonus, he can add additional properties which come with an enhancement cost, as follows:

| Property | Amount | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fortification | 25 | +1 |
| Energy Resistance | 10 | +2 |
| Spell Resistance | 13 |  |
| Fortification | 50 | +3 |
| Spell Resistance | 15 |  |
| Energy Resistance | 20 | +4 |
| Spell Resistance | 17 |  |
| Energy Resistance | 30 | +5 |
| Fortification | 75 |  |
| Spell Resistance | 19 |  |

"Fortification" confers the ability to ignore sneak attacks and critical hits. Fortification 50 means that there is a 50% chance of treating a sneak attack or a critical hit as a normal attack. The description of the ability also lists "glamered" at a cost of +1 point which - in case you're interested - means the ability to appear like a set of normal clothing.

#### Aspect of War
Once per day, as a swift action, the Warpriest's BAB increases to 20, he gains DR 10/- and can move at full speed regardless of armour and encumbrance. The effect lasts for 1 minute and during this time, calling upon blessings does not count towards your daily total.

##### Playing a Warpriest
A Warpriest has quite a lot going for it: bonus feats, access to Fighter-only feats, easier access to feats with BAB requirements, and weapons with the base damage of enlarged greatswords. The Fervor ability allow a Warpriest to self-buff as a swift action which is by far the best way to use it since the healing it provides is comparatively weak.

The scaling damage of the sacred weapon is an outstanding feature. If you make, say, a kukri, your weapon of choice you can even dual-wield it. The ability to add enhancements is not so great since the duration of the ability (a maximum of 20 rounds per day) is so short. The Sacred Armor ability is rather better since the duration is ten times as long and the ability to ignore critical hits can be invaluable against the right sort of enemy.

The main reason not to play a Warpriest is the existence of the Crusader archetype which makes the Warpriest slightly redundant. A Warpriest gets more feats earlier but the Crusader, diminished spellcasting notwithstanding, is a *much* better caster. If you're planning on taking the Angel Mythic Path, you will want to play a Crusader regardless.

The base class, with the possible exception of Champion of the Faith and Cult Leader, is the best one to play. Other archetypes give up important features for no good returns. A Champion of the Faith gives up most of the Sacred Weapon enhancements and the channel energy ability (which is no great loss) for a genuinely useful Smite ability. However, a Warpriest is likely to have a low Charisma modifier so this is not nearly good as a Paladin's Smite Evil ability.

A Cult Leader loses five feats (four bonus feats and the free Weapon Focus at level 1) in exchange for 6d6 sneak damage. A Cult Leader also has much worse proficiencies than other Warpriest archetypes. This can be fixed, to a degree, by selecting Lamashtu as a deity so that you get kukri as a sacred weapon. However, you will struggle to fit two weapon fighting alongside weapon focus and specialization feats because a Cult Leader is comparatively feat starved.

A Disenchanter is a disaster. The bonus feats (Great Fortitude and the like) are feats you would normally only select when you'd somehow managed to run out of decent choices. A sacred bonus to saves is OK but if you want to play an anti-caster, an Arcane Bloodrager is much better.

Feral Champion is a weak choice because wild shape is a weak class feature. Unlike the tabletop rules, your claws aren't sacred weapons. This means that you have to take Weapon Focus - Claw so that your natural weapon attacks scale and you won't benefit from this until 9th level which is a long time to wait. You will also have to burn a feat on Natural Spell.

A Mantis Zealot loses a number of combat feats but free feats with the sawtooth saber compensate and the passive crowd control ability is a bonus on top. The use of the signature Red Shroud ability is somewhat limited (a maximum of 11 or 12 times per day) but it is quite powerful, particularly once you unlock the Fading ability at 7th level.

A Proclaimer trades the useful Sacred Armor ability for the not-very-useful Cleanser of Evil which uses Fervor charges to do indifferent damage. Note that his abilities do not work on creatures that are merely evil but instead affect only evil outsiders.

Finally, Shieldbearer is a mess. Shield Bash is worse than useless without significant investment in Dexterity and TWF. Damage scaling only applies to the shield in the offhand which means that a Shieldbearer will do less damage to enemies than a regular Warpriest with a weapon and shield.

#### Chosen Alignment
A Champion of the Faith must choose one of law, good, chaos or evil as his chosen alignment. The chosen alignment must correspond to his own alignment and an alignment offered by his deity. The Warpriest automatically gains the Blessing for his chosen alignment even if it is not one of the domains offered by his choice of deity.

#### Smite
At 4th level, once per day, a Warpriest may Smite as a swift action. If the target is of the opposed alignment, the Warpriest adds his Charisma modifier to attack rolls and his class level to damage rolls until the enemy dies. Smite bypasses any DR the foe might possess and the Warpriest also gains an AC bonus equal to her Charisma modifier against attacks made by the target of the smite.

The Warpriest gains an additional use of Smite every four levels beyond 4th (maximum 5/day at 20th level). Using this ability consumes two uses of Fervour.

#### Sacred Weapon - Aligned Weapon
At 4th level, any weapon wielded by a Champion of the Faith counts as having his chosen alignment for the purposes of damage reduction.

At 12th level, once per day as a swift action, he can imbue a sacred weapon with the property corresponding to his chosen alignment (Axiomatic, Holy, etc.) for 1 minute. He can use this ability one additional time per day as 16th and 20th levels.

#### Cult Leader Proficiencies
Cult Leaders are proficient with simple weapons, light armour and light shields along with rapiers, shortswords and shortbows. They do not get the free Weapon Focus that other Warpriest archetypes receive.

#### Well Hidden
A Cult Leader gains +2 bonus on Stealth checks.

#### Sneak Attack
At 3rd level a Cult Leader's attacks deal an extra +1d6 damage when his opponent is denied Dexterity bonus or when he flanks his opponent. This increases by +1d6 every three additional levels, to a maximum +6d6 at 18th level.

#### Enthrall
At 4th level, a Cult Leader can try to fascinate all enemies within 30'. If the enemies fail a Will save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + Wisdom modifier), they are fascinated for a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier. Using this ability consumes two uses of Fervor.

#### Hide in Plain Sight
At 12th level, a Cult Leader can use the Stealth skill while being observed.

#### Bonus Feat (Disenchanter)
A Disenchanter may choose from Great Fortitude, Iron Will, Lightning Reflexes, Skill Focus - Knowledge (Arcana) and Spell Focus - Abjuration.

From 9th level, he can choose Dispel Synergy, Improved Great Fortitude, Improved Iron Will and Improved Lightning Reflexes.

From 12th level, he can choose Destructive Dispel and Ray Shield.

#### Mystic Interference
At 4th level, a Disenchanter can grant allies within 30' a +1 sacred (or profane) bonus to saving throws against spells and spell-like abilities for a number of rounds equal to his class level. At 8th level and every four levels thereafter, the bonus increases by +1 (maximum +5 at 20th level). Using this ability costs two uses of Fervor.

#### Banish Enchantment
From 6th level, a Disenchanter can consume two uses of Fervor to cast Dispel Magic on a single creature within 30'.

#### Mantis Zealot Proficiencies
Zealots are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, the Sawtooth Saber exotic weapon and light armour.

#### Red Shroud
At 1st level, as a move action, a Mantis Zealot can summon a cloud of flying mantises, granting him a +1 dodge bonus to AC and fast healing equal to his BAB. The AC bonus increases by +1 every four levels up to a maximum of +6 at 20th level. He can use this ability for a number of minutes per day equal to his Dexterity modifier (minimum 1 minute). At 7th level, the zealot can create the shroud as a swift action. At 13th level, creating the shroud becomes a free action.

#### Saber Fighting
At 1st level, a Mantis Zealot gains Weapon Focus (Sawtooth Saber) as a bonus feat. At 4th level, he gains Weapon Specialization. At 8th level, he gains Greater Weapon Focus. Finally, at 12th level, he gains Greater Weapon Specialization.

#### Deadly Fascination
From 3rd level, whenever a Mantis Zealot kills an enemy, other enemies within 30' must make a Will save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + Dexterity modifier) or become fascinated. If he is wielding two sabres, the DC increases by +2. If Red Shroud is active, the difficulty increases by an additional +2. Fascinated enemies may attempt a new save each time they take damage. This is a mind-affecting ability for purposes of determining immunity.

#### Mantis Swarm
From 5th level, when using Red Shroud, enemies within 5' must make a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + Wisdom modifier) or begin taking 1d6 bleed damage per turn. Multiple applications stack but affected creatures may attempt a new save each round to stop the bleeding. Bleeding may also be stopped by any effect which restores HP. When wielding sawtooth sabers, the Zealot deals +1d6 damage to bleeding enemies. This increases by an additional +1d6 at 11th and 18th levels.

#### Fading
From 7th level, when using Red Shroud, a Mantis Zealot becomes partially ethereal, granting him a 20% chance to avoid attacks and targeted spells. This chance increases by +10% at 10th level and every three subsequent levels (max 60% at 19th level).

#### Aspect of the Mantis
At 20th level, once per day as a swift action, a Mantis Zealot can call upon the power of Achaekek for 1 minute. During this time, he gains DR 10/good and a 2d6 bite attack which inflicts 1 negative level, In addition, his sawtooth saber attacks deal bleed damage equal to his Dexterity modifier and if he kills an enemy with a saber, he can teleport next to another creature within 30' and make a single attack against that enemy. Finally, calling upon blessings does not count towards your daily total.

#### Shield Adept
A Shieldbearer adds his shield bonus to concentration checks when casting defensively.

#### Sacred Shield
A Shieldbearer's shield is classed as a sacred weapon and Warpriest damage scaling applies to the shield but not to other weapons. From 4th level, he can add a +1 enhancement bonus to his shield as a swift action gaining an additional +1 enhancement bonus every four levels thereafter (maximum +5 at 20th level). The enhancement bonus only applies to defence - it is not added to attack and damage rolls when shield bashing. A Shieldbearer can also add the Spell Resistance and Fortification properties to his shield (for an enhancement cost of +1 to +5).

#### Channel Energy (Shieldbearer)
A Shieldbearer can only channel energy when wielding a shield and the effect is a 30' cone.

#### Sacred Weapon (Shieldbearer)
From 7th level, a Shieldbearer can add a +1 enhancement to any weapons for which he has taken Weapon Focus. The enhancement bonus increases by +1 every three subsequent levels (maximum +5 at 19th level).
