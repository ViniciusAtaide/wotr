# Witch
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Witch has the following characteristics:

- Primary attribute: Intelligence
- Slow (1/2) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast arcane spells up to 9th-level
- Spells must be prepared in advance
- 5 HP per level
- High saving throws for Will, low saving throws for Fortitude and Reflex
- 2 skill points per level.

Class Skills: Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Persuasion, Use Magic Device

#### Hex
At 1st level and each even numbered level thereafter, the Witch gains the uses of hexes. Using a hex is a standard action and does not require a Concentration check or provoke an attack of opportunity. Where the Hex requires a saving throw, the DC is 10 + half the Witch's level + her Intelligence modifier.

Hexes don't have a usage limit. However a creature can generally benefit from or be affected by a given hex once per day.

##### Ameliorating
The Witch can use this hex to protect the target from the dazzled, fatigued, shaken or sickened conditions. If the touched creature is suffering from the chosen condition, it is suppressed for a number of minutes equal to Witch's level. Additionally, the target gains a +4 circumstance bonus to saving throws against effects that cause the chosen condition. A creature can benefit from this hex twice per day but each time it must be for a different condition.

##### Aura of Purity
Diseases, inhaled poisons and noxious gases are negated in a 10' radius around the Witch for a number of minutes per day equal to her class level. The duration does not need to be consecutive. Effects caused by spells whose level is more than half the Witch's class level are unaffected.

##### Cackle
Cackle is a move action. Creatures within 30' under the effects of any of the Evil Eye, Fortune, Misfortune or Protective Luck hexes caused by the Witch have the duration extended by 1 round.

##### Evil Eye
The target takes a -2 penalty on one of the following: AC, attack rolls or saving throws. The hex lasts for a number of rounds equal to 3 + the Witch's Intelligence modifier. If the target makes a Will save, the effect only lasts for 1 round. At 8th level, the penalty increases to -4. Creatures immune to mind-affecting effects are not affected.

##### Fortune
The Witch grants a creature good luck for 1 round, allowing them to reroll any ability check, attack roll, saving throw or skill check twice, taking the better result. At 8th and 16th level, the effect is extended by 1 round.

##### Healing (Hex)
The hex acts like *Cure Light Wounds* cast at the Witch's class level. At 5th level, the hex acts like *Cure Moderate Wounds*.

##### Iceplant
The Witch gains a permanent +2 natural armor bonus to AC.

##### Misfortune
The Witch gives a creature bad luck for 1 round, forcing them to reroll any ability check, attack roll, saving throw or skill check twice, taking the worse result. A successful Will save negates the effect. At 8th and 16th level, the effect is extended by 1 round.

##### Nails
The Witch's nails count as natural weapons that deal 1d3 points of damage (1d2 if small).

##### Protective Luck
When the recipient of the hex is targeted by an attack, the attacker must roll twice, taking the worse result. The effect lasts for 1 round, increasing to 2 rounds at 8th level and 3 rounds at 16th level.

##### Slumber
The target of the hex must make a Will save or fall asleep for a number of rounds equal to the Witch's class level or until the creature takes damage.

##### Vulnerability Curse
The target's energy resistances or immunities are suppressed for a number of rounds equal to the Witch's class level. A successful Will save reduces the duration to 1 round.

##### Ward
A warded creature gains a +2 deflection bonus to AC and a +2 resistance bonus to saving throws which lasts until the warded creature is hit or fails a saving throw. Casting Ward on another creature removes an existing Ward. A Witch cannot use this hex on herself. At 8th and 16th level, the bonus increases by +1.

#### Major Hexes
From 10th level, a Witch can select more powerful hexes.

##### Agony
The target creature within 60' becomes nauseated for a number of rounds equal to the Witch's class level. If the target makes a Fortitude save, it is not affected. If it fails its saving throw, it may make a fresh attempt each round.

##### Animal Skin
The Witch transforms into a leopard (equivalent to *Beast Shape II*).

##### Beast's Gift
The recipient of the hex gains natural attacks for a number of minutes equal to the Witch's class level, either a 1d8 bite attack or two 1d4 claw attacks.

##### Delicious Fright
The target becomes shaken for a number of rounds equal to 3 + the Witch's Intelligence modifier. While the Witch remains within 30' of the target, she gains a +1 morale bonus on attacks and a +1 morale bonus on saving throws. A successful Will save reduces the duration of the effect to 1 round and creatures immune to mind-affecting effects are not affected.

##### Hoarfrost
Unless the target makes a Fortitude save, the target takes 1 point of Constitution damage per minute until it dies or is cured (via Break Enchantment or Remove Curse). The target can attempt a Fortitude save each minute to throw off the effect.

##### Major Ameliorating
The Witch can use this hex to protect the target from the blinded, cursed, diseased or poisoned conditions. If the touched creature is suffering from the chosen condition, it is suppressed for a number of minutes equal to Witch's level. Additionally, the target gains a +4 circumstance bonus to saving throws against effects that cause the chosen condition. A creature can benefit from this hex twice per day but each time it must be for a different condition.

##### Major Healing
The hex acts like *Cure Serious Wounds* cast at the Witch's class level. At 15th level, the hex acts like *Cure Critical Wounds*.

##### Regenerative Sinew
The target gains fast healing 5 for a number of rounds equal to half the Witch's class level. Alternatively, the effect heals up to 4 points of ability score damage from two ability scores.

##### Restless Slumber
Prerequisites:
Slumber

The target of the hex must make a Will save or fall asleep for a number of rounds equal to the Witch's class level. While sleeping, the affected creature takes 1d10 damage each round. When it awakes, the creature is confused for a number of rounds equal to half the Witch's class level.

#### Grand Hexes
From 18th level, a Witch can select the most powerful hexes.

##### Animal Servant
This hex works on a humanoid target, turning it into a beast (as though it were the recipient of *Beast Shape II*) and placing it under the control of the Witch. A successful Will save negates the effect.

##### Death Curse
The target becomes fatigued unless it makes a Will save. If it fails, it becomes exhausted on the next round. On the third round, it must make a Fortitude save or die. Creatures making the Fortitude save take 4d6 points of damage + 1 point per class level.

##### Lay to Rest
This has the effect of *Undeath to Death* on the targeted undead creature which may attempt a Will save to resist the effect.

##### Life Giver
Once per day, as a full round action, a Witch can touch a dead creature and bring it back to life. Besides not requiring a material component, the effect is identical to *Resurrection*.

##### Playing a Witch
Compared to a Wizard, a Witch has a greatly reduced spell selection, although her choice of Patron can improve matters. Since she does not get any bonus feats, the DC of her spells is likely to be significantly below that of a wizard. What she receives instead are a total of 11 hexes. These can be extremely powerful - the combination of Protective Luck and Cackle on the right party member may justify a Witch's place in the party on its own. There is a problem, however: there are few ways to boost the DC of hexes which means that when you *really* want to hit an enemy with Evil Eye, you may struggle to make it stick. That said, there are enough weak-willed enemies to make (Restless) Slumber extremely useful. For bonus spells, the Deception Patron seems to offer the best selection. Note that the Agility Patron is badly bugged.

The draw of an Elemental Witch is that she has a spammable attack. However, the cost of her Elemental Ray ability is steep: she loses five hexes and has a greatly reduced choice of patrons.

A Hag of Gyronna trades three hexes for the unique abilities and a powerful companion. However, you have no choice of patron and you may find the alignment choice restrictive. If you were planning on going down the Lich route anyway, this may not be relevant.

The restricted Patron list of a Hagbound isn't too bad and the ability to Bestow Curse at will is quite good. However, the Hag transformation costs several hexes and is redundant both with a Witch's slow BAB progression and spells like Enlarge Person and Legendary Proportions. This makes Hagbound quite a bad choice.

Not as bad as a Hex Channeler, however, who swaps a hex for a completely worthless channel energy ability. To make the ability other than worthless, the Witch will have to give up her other hexes. Play a Cleric instead.

A Keen-Eyed Adventurer gives up three hexes to gain level-scaling cantrips. Given the number of ways to increase per-die damage on spells, you can end up doing semi-respectable damage with them. However, a Witch already has a powerful at-will ability in the form of Evil Eye and losing the level 1st and 6th level hexes will affect how quickly you can unlock the essential ones. The capstone ability (which increases the damage of each Scorching / Hellfire Ray) is much better than yet another hex but going for that prevents you from taking levels in, for example, Loremaster or Arcane Trickster.

A Ley Line Guardian gives up two hexes and her familiar for the ability to empower a number of spell casts per day. However, when she does so, she must make a *Fortitude* save or become staggered for a very long time. Given that a Witch has a weak Fortitude save, you're liable to spend a long time in the staggered state. On the plus side, she casts spells spontaneously if you prefer that playstyle. However, if you prefer that playstyle, a Sorcerer is plain better.

A Stigmatized Witch is also a spontaneous caster. She doesn't lose out on hexes but she does lose out on a Patron which means that her spell list is going to be restricted. She must also take a Curse. These aren't altogether bad (at least in the later levels) but most are better on an Oracle who is more suited to front-line duties. The only curse that *really *synergises with a Witch is Blackened but the -4 AB penalty that comes with it is horrible.

Finally, the Witch of the Veil gives up a bunch of hexes for a bona fide superpower: the ability to turn invisible at will *as a swift action*. This is somewhat useful in a party context since the rest of your party are unaffected but some people are masochistic enough to try and solo the game and Shrouded Step is the basis of many solo unfair strategies.

#### Elemental Ray
At 1st level, an Elemental Witch chooses an energy type. She gains the ability make a ranged touch attack doing 1d6 energy damage + 1d6 for every 3 Witch levels. She can use this ability at will. At 11th level, she can select another energy type.

An Elemental Witch can choose either the Elements patron or the Winter patron.

#### Hag Claws
The Hagbound Witch's hands are natural weapons, inflicting 1d4 points of damage (1d3 for a small creature).

#### Hagbound Patron
A Hagbound Witch can only choose Deception, Shadow, Strength or Winter as her patron.

#### Hunched Muscle
At 2nd level, a Hagbound Witch gains a +2 size bonus to Strength. This increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 14th level.

#### Vile Curse
At 10th level, a Hagbound Witch can use *Bestow Curse* at will. If the target of the curse makes its save, it becomes immune to the Vile Curse ability for 24 hours.

#### Hag Transformation
At 20th level, the Hagbound Witch becomes a monstrous humanoid and gains spell resistance 31 and immunity to charm and fear effects.

#### Channel Energy
At 2nd level, a Hex Channeler can channel positive energy (if good-aligned) or negative energy (if evil). A neutrally aligned character must choose an energy type. She can choose whether to include herself in the effect. Initially she heals (or harms) 1d6 of damage. Every time the Hex Channeler acquire a new hex, she can instead choose to increase the ability by +1d6. She can use the ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + her Charisma modifier.

#### Improved Cantrips
A Keen-Eyed Adventurer gains access to the Acid Splash, Ignition, Jolt and Ray of Frost cantrips. Cantrips which deal damage and which require a ranged touch attack to hit deal an additional die of damage for every two witch levels.

This does not work with spells (such as *Snowball*) turned into cantrips with the Completely Normal Spell metamagic feat.

#### Elemental Vortex
From 6th level and once per combat, a Keen-Eyed Adventurer can summon a vortex which lasts one round per 6 witch levels (max 3 rounds at 18th level). While the vortex is active, she may attempt a combat manoeuvre check (using her witch level in place of BAB and Intelligence in place of Strength) against enemies within 10'. A successful check pulls affected enemies towards the vortex and deals 1d8 slashing damage per two witch levels (Reflex save for half damage).

#### Cantrip Mastery
At 20th level, when casting a spell which requires a ranged touch attack, a Keen-Eyed Adventurer deals bonus damage equal to her Intelligence modifier.

#### Ley Line Powered
A Ley Line Guardian knows the same number of spells and receives the same number of spell slots per day as Sorcerer of the same level. Bonus spells granted by the Guardian's patron are added at the appropriate levels.

#### Conduit Surge
As a swift action, a Ley Line Guardian can increase her effective caster level for the next spell she casts by 1d4-1. After using a Conduit Surge, she must make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + level of spell cast + number of additional caster levels granted) or become staggered for a number of minutes equal to the level of the spell. At 8th level, the caster increase becomes +1d4. A Ley Line Guardian can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + her Intelligence modifier.
