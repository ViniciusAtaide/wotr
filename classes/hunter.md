# Hunter
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

A Hunter has the following characteristics:

- Primary attributes: Strength, Dexterity and Wisdom
- Medium (3/4) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast divine spells up to 6th-level
- Spells are cast spontaneously
- 8 HP at level 1, then 5 HP per level (d8 hit die)
- High saving throws for Fortitude and Reflex, low saving throws for Will
- 4 skill points per level.

Class Skills: Athletics, Stealth, Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Perception, Persuasion.

#### Hunter Proficiencies
Hunters are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, light and medium armor and shields.

#### Animal Companion
A Hunter chooses an animal companion at 1st level.

#### Animal Focus
A Hunter can grant an animal aspect to her companion. As a swift action, she can apply the same benefits to herself for a number of minutes per day equal to her class level.

##### Animal Focus - Bear
The creature gains a +2 inherent bonus to Constitution. This increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 15th.

##### Animal Focus - Bull
The creature gains a +2 inherent bonus to Strength. This increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 15th.

##### Animal Focus - Falcon
The creature gains a +4 competence bonus to Perception checks. This increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 15th.

##### Animal Focus - Monkey
The creature gains a +4 competence bonus to Athletics checks. This increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 15th.

##### Animal Focus - Mouse
The creature gains Evasion. At 12th level, the creature gains Improved Evasion.

##### Animal Focus - Owl
The creature gains a +4 competence bonus to Stealth checks. This increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 15th.

##### Animal Focus - Stag
The creature gains a 5' enhancement bonus to speed. This increase to 10' at 8th level and 20' at 15th.

##### Animal Focus - Tiger
The creature gains a +2 inherent bonus to Dexterity. This increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 15th.

#### Nature Sense
A Hunter gains a +2 bonus on Lore (Nature) checks.

#### Precise Companion
At 2nd level, a Hunter chooses either Precise Shot or Outflank as a bonus feat. She does not need to meet the prerequisites.

#### Teamwork Feat
At 3rd level and every 3 levels thereafter, a hunter gains a bonus teamwork feat. She may also choose Precise Shot.

#### Hunter Tactics
At 3rd level, a Hunter grants her teamwork feats to her animal companion.

#### Woodland Stride
At 5th level, a Hunter can move through difficult terrain at normal speed, suffering no damage or other impairment.

#### Second Animal Focus
At 8th level, whenever a Hunter uses her Animal Focus ability, she can choose two aspects.

#### Raise Animal Companion
At 10th level, a Hunter may cast Raise Dead on her animal companion as a spell-like ability. At 16th level, this functions like Resurrection.

#### One with the Wild
At 17th level, a Hunter and her companion can attempt to demoralize all animals within 30' as a swift action.

#### Master Hunter
At 20th level, the Hunter can receive the Animal Focus benefits any number of times per day.

##### Playing a Hunter
A Hunter feels like a weak class, albeit one with an animal companion. However, combat performance of a Hunter character is indifferent (3/4 BAB and no bonus combat feats) and the spell selection isn't great, although *Lead Blades* is a notable inclusion.

The Animal Focus ability is OK because it confers inherent bonuses which stack with enhancement bonuses from gear. You'll want to take *Outflank* at 2nd level; not only is this 5 levels earlier than you can get the feat otherwise, but the class is all about teaming up with the companion and you won't be doing this with a longbow, making *Precise Shot* a waste. Instead, you'll want to use a reach weapon and fight behind your sturdier companion. Hunter Tactics from 3rd level allows you and your companion to share teamwork feats. Coordinated Maneuvers, for example, is not normally a feat that you would bother with. However, with an auto-tripping companion, it becomes quite good.

A Hunter gets a lot from her first three levels: full companion progression, immunity to difficult terrain, *Outflank*, *Lead Blades*, a teamwork feat of choice and the ability to share it with a companion. This makes a 3-level dip for a Mad Dog Barbarian, for example, quite decent.

Despite having the silliest name of any archetype, a Colluding Scoundrel is better than the base class. The Pathfinder rules do not provide many ways of drawing aggro onto a party member built to take it, but the Scapegoat ability allows you to do just that. The Backstabber ability gives you bonus precision damage which stacks with sneak damage so that something like a Colluding Scoundrel 16 / Vivisectionist 4 split is not bad.

A Divine Hound gives up Animal Focus for Judgments. Since Animal Focus is quite weak and Judgments are quite strong (particularly with the *Everlasting Judgment* mythic ability), this is a very favorable trade. Being able to select only a dog or wolf as a companion is neither here nor there since a dog is one of the best companions anyway.

The very similarly named Divine Hunter swaps an awful lot for a domain, including all the teamwork feats and being able to share them with her companion. There *are* strong domain choices but none that are this strong. Something like Sacred Huntsmaster feels like a better choice.

If the base Hunter is a weak class with an animal companion, the Forester is simply a weak class. It plays similarly to a cut-down Ranger or Slayer which begs the very obvious question: why not play a Ranger or Slayer instead? Note that the Tactician ability is a sure sign of a weak class. *Automatically* sharing teamwork feats with a companion is good because it makes you and them better. Spending an action to share a teamwork feat with your party members is simply weak because, assuming you have Outflank on everyone anyway, it never gives you a good enough bonus to be worth trading any other class feature for.

A Tandem Executioner gives up spellcasting in exchange for Studied Target and some unique combat abilities. Given that a Hunter's spellcasting is indifferent and that some of the techniques are quite strong, this isn't a bad trade.

An Urban Hunter trades teamwork feats for bonus feats (mostly combat maneuvers) which are shared with the companion. Automatic tripping is good because it doesn't cost your animal companion its action to do it. However, using your actions or your companion's actions to trip or shove or disarm your opponent is a very poor use of your time. Free *Combat Reflexes* is nice but waiting until 12th level to get it is not so nice.

Finally a Wandering Marksman trades away all her teamwork feats for a modest circumstance bonus against enemies that her companion is fighting. While this doesn't punish you for making a ranged build, a Ranger simply does this and much, much more.

#### Scapegoat
As a swift action, the Scoundrel can declare a willing ally as a scapegoat and force an adjacent enemy to concentrate its attacks on them. For one minute, the target takes -2 penalty to attacks against any target other than the scapegoat and the target has 10% chance of spell failure with spells which don't target the scapegoat or include them within the area of effect. The attack penalty increases to -4 at 8th level and -6 at 15th level.

The scoundrel can use this ability a number of times per day equal to her class level. Alternatively, she can make her animal companion the scapegoat without it counting against her daily uses.

#### Backstabber
At 8th level, the scoundrel deals an additional 2d6 damage against an enemy threatened by the designated scapegoat. This increases to 3d6 points at 15th level. This is precision damage and is not multiplied on a critical hit.

#### Master Backstabber
At 20th level, as a standard action, the scoundrel can make an attack against the enemy penalized by the scapegoat ability. If the attack hits, the enemy must make a Fortitude save (DC = 20 + Wisdom modifier) or be killed.

#### Domain
A Divine Hunter can choose from the domains granted by her deity. The domain spells are added to her spellbook.

#### Otherworldly Companion
At 3rd level, a Divine Hunter's companion becomes otherworldly, either Celestial or Fiendish, depending on alignment.

##### Otherworldly Companion - Celestial
The companion gains spell resistance equal 5 + its level and resistance 5 to cold, electricity and acid. At 5th level, the resistances increase to 10 and it gains DR 5/evil. At 11th level, the resistances increase to 15 and the DR increases to 10. Once per day, the companion can Smite Evil.

##### Otherworldly Companion - Fiendish
The companion gains spell resistance equal 5 + its level and resistance 5 to cold and fire. At 5th level, the resistances increase to 10 and it gains DR 5/good. At 11th level, the resistances increase to 15 and the DR increases to 10. Once per day, the companion can Smite Good.

The bonus only applies to the Forester herself.

At 2nd, 7th, 13th and 19th levels, a Forester can choose any combat feat so long as she meets the prerequisites.

#### Tactician
At 3rd level, as a standard action, a Forester can grant the benefits of a single teamwork feat to allies within 30' for a number of rounds equal to her character level + 3. She can use this ability once per day, gaining an additional use at 7th, 12th and 17th levels.

#### Favored Terrain
At 5th level, a Hunter may select a favored terrain like a Ranger. She may specify another favored terrain every four additional levels.

#### Camouflage
At 7th level, a forester can use Stealth in her favored terrain without requiring cover.

#### Breath of Life
At 10th level, a Forester can use Breath of Life as a spell-like ability.

#### Terrain Expertise
At 11th level, a Forester can choose one of her favored terrains. While in that terrain, she gains a favored enemy bonus against creatures there. She may choose additional terrains at 15th and 19th levels.

#### Hide in Plain Sight
At 14th level, a Forester can use Camouflage in any terrain type.

#### Aversion to Spellcasting
A Tandem Executioner does not gain spells and does not have a caster level.

#### Studied Target
This is identical to the Slayer ability. The Tandem Executioner shares the bonuses with her companion as long as it is within 60'.

#### Tandem Executioner Techniques
At 4th level and every three subsequent levels, a Tandem Executioner unlocks special techniques for taking down her enemies.

##### Disrupted Focus
The DC to cast spells defensively increases by +4 for enemies within 10' of you or your companion.

##### New Favorite Toy
When your companion hits an enemy with an attack of opportunity, the enemy's movement is interrupted. Your ranged attacks against a target mark it and your companion's next attack deals +1d6 precision damage for every 4 class levels.

##### Vigilant Partners
Once per round you may negate one hit against your companion if you make a Lore (Nature) check against the opponent's attack roll. Similarly, when within 10' of your companion, it can attempt a Mobility check to negate a single hit against you.

##### Working in Tandem
While within 10' of each other and attacking the same creature in melee, you and your companion receive a bonus to attack rolls equal to your class level / 4.

##### Protective Duo
Prerequisites:
Working in Tandem

While within 10' of each other, whenever an enemy adjacent to you makes a successful attack against your companion, you can make an attack of opportunity against the enemy. Similarly, your companion can make an attack of opportunity against creatures that successfully attack you.

##### Push and Bite
Prerequisites:
Favorite New Toy

Once per round when making a ranged attack against an enemy adjacent to your companion, you can attempt a bull rush combat maneuver with a +4 circumstance bonus, using your Dexterity modifier to determine your CMB. If the maneuver is successful, your companion uses an attack of opportunity to make a melee attack which will always hit the enemy.

##### Spellbane
Prerequisites:
Disrupted Focus

Enemies within 10' of you or companion suffer -2 penalties to spell DC and spell attack rolls.

##### Timely Warning
Prerequisites:
Vigilant Partners

Once per round when your companion fails a saving throw, you can attempt a Lore (Nature) check against the effect's DC to negate it. Similarly, when you fail a Fortitude or Reflex save while within 10' of your companion, it can attempt a Mobility check to negate the effect.

##### Elusive Pair
Prerequisites:
Vigilant Partners
Timely Warning
Level 16

While within 10' of each other, you and your companion gain a dodge bonus to AC and competence bonus to Reflex saves equal to your class level / 4.

##### Make It Bleed
Prerequisites:
Working in Tandem
Protective Duo
Level 16

While within 10' of each other and attacking the same creature in melee, you and your companion gain a +4 competence bonus on critical confirmation rolls. If a critical hit is confirmed, the target takes 2d6 bleed damage.

##### Piercing Cascade
Prerequisites:
New Favorite Toy
Push and Bite
Level 16

As a full round action, you can make a ranged attack against all enemies within 15' of a selected point. Enemies within 10' of your companion have a -2 penalty to AC against this attack.

##### Prey of Choice
Prerequisites:
Disrupted Focus
Spellbane
Level 16

When an enemy casts a spell or uses a spell-like ability, you and your companion gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls against that creature until the end of combat. An enemy within 10' of you or companion attempting a concentration check must roll twice and take the worse result.

#### Studied Target (Swift)
At 7th level, a Tandem Executioner can study an opponent as a swift action.

#### Self Reliance
At 6th level, when the companion is more than 10' away from the Marksman, it gains a morale bonus to AC equal to +1 per four class levels.

#### Emergency Swap
At 9th level, as a standard action, a Wandering Marksman may swap positions with her companion if they are within 30' of each other. This does not provoke an attack of opportunity and the ability can be used once per encounter.

#### Imminent Attack
At 15th level, a Wandering Marksman may shoot a special type of ammunition which marks all enemies in a 10' area. The companion gains a +3 morale bonus to attack and damage against marked enemies and its attacks bypass cold iron, mithral and adamantine DR. If the companion kills an enemy, the critical range of its attacks increases by 1 until the end of combat. This effect can stack multiple times.
