# Bard
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Bard has the following characteristics:

- Primary attribute: Charisma
- Medium (3/4) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast arcane spells up to 6th-level
- Spells are cast spontaneously
- 8 HP at level 1, then 5 HP per level (d8 hit die)
- High saving throws for Reflex and Will, low saving throws for Fortitude
- 4 skill points per level.

Class Skills: All

#### Bard Proficiencies
Bards are proficient with simple weapons plus the longsword, rapier, short sword and shortbow. Bards are also proficient with light armor and shields.

#### Bardic Performance
The bard can sing for a total of four rounds + her Charisma modifier per day. She can sing for an additional 2 rounds per class level.

#### Bardic Knowledge
A bard adds half her class level to all Knowledge and Lore checks.

#### Inspire Courage
A bard uses her performance to inspire allies, granting them a +1 morale bonus to saves against charm and fear effects and +1 competence bonus to attack and damage rolls. This increases to +2 at 5th level, +3 at 11th level and +4 at 17th level.

#### Well Versed
At 2nd level, the bard gains +4 to saves against bardic performance, sonic and language-dependent effects.

#### Inspire Competence
At 3rd level, the bard's performance grants all allies within 30' a +2 competence bonus on all skill checks. This increases to +3 at 7th level, +4 at 11th, +5 at 15th and +6 at 19th.

#### Lore Master
At 5th level, a Bard can choose to take 10 on any Knowledge or Lore check that she has ranks in.

#### Fascinate
At 6th level, the bard can cause creatures within 30' to be fascinated and not move if they fail a Will Save (DC 10 + half class level + Charisma modifier). If the save succeeds, the performance has no further effect. Any damage to a fascinated target breaks the effect.

#### Bardic Performance (Move Action)
At 7th level, a bard can start a performance as a move action instead of a standard action.

#### Dirge of Doom
At 8th level, the Fascinate ability inflicts the shaken status.

#### Inspire Greatness
At 9th level, the bard can inspire greatness, giving allies 2d10 hit points, +2 competence bonus on attacks and +1 competence bonus to Fortitude saves.

#### Jack of All Trades
At 10th level, the bard gains +1 bonus on all skill checks.

#### Soothing Performance
At 12th level, the bard can spend 4 rounds of performance for an equivalent of Mass Cure Serious Wounds while removing the fatigued, sickened and shaken conditions from allies.

#### Bardic Performance (Swift Action)
At 13th level, a bard can start a performance as a swift action.

#### Frightening Tune
At 14th level, the Fascinate ability inflicts fear (DC 10 + half class level + Charisma modifier).

#### Inspire Heroics
At 15th level, the bard can inspire heroism, giving allies +4 morale bonus to saves and a +4 dodge bonus to AC.

#### Deadly Performance
At 20th level, any enemy within 30' may be selected. If it hears the bard's performance for 1 round, it must make a save (DC 20 + Charisma modifier) or die. If the save succeeds, the creature is staggered for 1-4 rounds. You can use this ability once per day.

#### Bard Talents
The Bard can select from the Rogue Talent list.

##### Playing a Bard
The bard's role is to make everyone function a little better. Because they are a skilled class with every skill a class skill, they can function as a "skill monkey", taking on the skill checks that your other characters might not be able to cover. The first feat you should take is Lingering Performance since this gives you, in effect, three times as many rounds of performance per day. Since Bards have lots of feats, you may want to take Spell Focus (Enchantment) at some point so that your offensive magic doesn't become completely useless.

The Archaeologist is a rewarding dip and well worth 2, 3 or even 6 levels before branching out. You can use your Luck ability and your magic to make up for any offensive and defensive shortfalls. However, it is indifferent beyond that since it fails at the party support role without giving you anything to replace it.

The Beast Tamer is also lacking in the party support role, losing Inspire Courage, Inspire Competence and Dirge of Doom. Inspire Ferocity isn't much of a replacement since it only works on summons and any animal companions you might have. On the plus side, the summons are very good and are further boosted with Augment Summoning.

The Chelaxian Diva gives up a bunch of useful Bard songs for a bunch of not-very-useful abilities since the enemy-affecting songs aren't as good as Fascinate, for example. Blazing Rondo is OK because it becomes better than Haste from 10th level.

The Dirge Bard gives up a little (Well Versed, basically) for a lot. The ability to apply mind-affecting effects to undead is pretty good and the other abilities are all solid as well.

The Flame Dancer trades two useful abilities (Inspire Competence and Dirge of Doom) for the rather less useful Fire Dance and a pathetic collection of fire spells. Avoid.

The Thundercaller also loses Inspire Competence and Dirge of Doom but gains two decent abilities. The Storm Call ability becomes very nasty with the Lingering Performance feat and Thunder Call is essentially an on-demand stun.

Besides having a silly name, a Tranquil Whisperer gives up a lot in return for very little. Inspire Tranquility doesn't even begin to make up for losing Inspire Courage. Saving Tranquility isn't too bad but Tranquil Senses is perfectly useless.

#### Archaeologist's Luck
For a number of rounds per day equal to 4 + her Charisma modifier, the Archaeologist gains a +1 luck bonus on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks and weapon damage rolls. At each level after 1st, she can use this ability for 2 additional rounds per day. The bonus increases to +2 at 5th level, +3 at 11th level and +4 at 17th level.

#### Clever Explorer
At 2nd level, an Archaeologist gains a bonus equal to half her class level on Trickery and Perception checks.

#### Uncanny Dodge
At 2nd level, the Archaeologist cannot be caught flat-footed or lose her Dexterity bonus to AC if the attacker is invisible. If you gain this ability twice from another class, you gain Improved Uncanny Dodge instead.

#### Danger Sense
At 3rd level, the Archaeologist gains a +1 bonus on Reflex saves to avoid traps and a +1 dodge bonus to AC against attacks made by traps. In addition, she gains a +1 bonus on Perception checks to avoid being surprised by a foe. These bonuses increase by +1 every 3 levels (maximum +6 at 18th level).

#### Confident Explorer
At 6th level, an Archaeologist can always take 10 on Trickery checks.

#### Evasion
At 6th level, if the character makes a Reflex save against an attack that normally deals half damage on a successful save, she instead takes no damage.

#### Advanced Talents
At 10th level, the Archaeologist may select from the Advanced Rogue Talents.

#### Spontaneous Summoning
A Beast Tamer can cast a *Summon Nature's Ally* spell of equivalent level in place of an equivalent-level spell.

#### Beast Call
A Beast Caller can cast *Summon Nature's Ally I* as a spell-like ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + her Charisma modifier. This is standard action and summoned creatures remain for 1 minute per level. At 3rd level and every two levels thereafter, the power increases by 1 level (maximum *Summon Nature's Ally IX* at 17th level).

#### Inspire Ferocity
A Beast Tamer uses her performance to inspire animal companions and summoned allies, granting them a +1 morale bonus to saves against charm and fear effects and +1 competence bonus to attack and damage rolls. This increases to +2 at 5th level, +3 at 11th level and +4 at 17th level.

At 10th level, affected allies gain the effect of the Magic Fang spell. All natural attacks count as though they were silver and cold iron. At 14th level, attacks by affected allies count as if they were adamantine. Finally, at 18th level, affected allies gain the effects of the Greater Magic Fang spell.

#### Beast Tamer Bonus Spells
It's not shown in the character creator, but a Beast Tamer gains bonus nature-related spells from 2nd level:

- Magic Fang at 2nd level
- Aspect of the Bear at 5th level
- Greater Magic Fang at 8th level
- Aspect of the Wolf at 11th level
- Animal Growth at 14th level
- Primal Regression at 17th level.

#### Famous
A Chelaxian Diva adds half her class level to Persuasion (Bluff and Diplomacy) checks.

#### Devastating Aria
From 2nd level, a Chelaxian Diva can use her voice to deliver sonic damage to an object equal to 1d6 + her class level. Living creatures take half damage. This damage increases by +1d6 at 7th level and every four additional class levels (max 5d6 at 19th level). Creatures affected by The Tragedy of False Hope become vulnerable to this damage.

#### The Tragedy of False Hope
From 3rd level, the Diva's performance can leave enemies within 30' flat-footed for the song's duration. A Will saving throw (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + CHA) negates the effect and enemies with Uncanny Dodge or similar abilities are immune as creatures immune to mind-affecting effects.

#### Prima Donna
From 5th level, a Chelaxian Diva can spend 1 round of Performance to increase the save DC of her songs' effects by +2.

#### Deadly Vibrato
From 6th level, a Chelaxian Diva can spend an additional round of performance to add 1d8 sonic damage to her song's effects. Enemies can attempt a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + CHA) to negate the damage. At 14th level, the damage increases to 2d8 and at 20th level it increases to 3d8.

#### Blazing Rondo
From 9th level, the Diva's performance can haste allies within 60'. The bonuses to AC, attack rolls and reflex saves are one fifth her class level. Allies hasted for 3 rounds or more must makes a Fortitude save or become fatigued for twice as long as they were hasted.

#### Vindictive Soliloquy
From 12th level, the Diva's performance can produce the effects of *Call Lightning Storm*, although the strikes inflict sonic damage. When calling down a bolt, the Diva can attempt a Persuasion (DC 30) check to have the bolt deal 5d10 damage rather than 5d6.

#### Banshee's Requiem
From 16th level, the Diva's performance can inflict 2 negative levels on living creatures within 30' unless they make a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + CHA) to negate the effect. This is a death and sonic effect.

#### Haunted Eyes
At 2nd level, a Dirge Bard gains a +4 bonus on saves against fear, curses, death effects and necromantic effects.

#### Secrets of the Grave
At 2nd level, a Dirge Bard gains a bonus equal to half her level on Lore (Religion) checks. Her mind-affecting spells work on undead as if they were living creatures and undead are treated as humanoids for the purpose of determining which spells affect them. Finally, she adds a number of necromantic spells to her list of known spells:

- Cause Fear at 2nd level
- Command Undead at 6th level
- Fear at 10th level
- Waves of Fatigue at 14th level
- Banshee Blast at 18th level.

#### Haunting Refrain
At 5th level, a Dirge Bard gains a bonus on Intimidate checks equal to half her class level. She also gains a +2 bonus to DC on spells with a fear effect. This increases by +1 every five subsequent levels (maximum +5 at 20th level).

#### Dance of the Dead
At 10th level, a Dirge Bard's performance can animate dead bodies as in the *Animate Dead* spell.

#### Bound to the Land
A Thundercaller gains a bonus equal to half her class level on Lore (Nature) checks.

#### Thunder Call
At 3rd level, the Thundercaller can use one of her performances to unleash a clap of thunder identical to the Sound Burst spell, dealing 1d8 sonic damage to creatures in a 10' radius and stunning them for 1 round unless they make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + class level / 2 + Charisma modifier). The damage increases to 3d8 at 7th level and by 2d8 every four additional class levels (maximum 9d8 at 19th level).

#### Incite Rage
At 6th level, the Thundercaller can induce Rage in allies and / or enemies that fail a Will save (DC 10 + half class level + Charisma modifier). Enraged creatures gain +2 Strength and Constitution, +1 morale bonus on will saves. They also take a -2 AC penalty and cannot cast spells. You can enrage allies, enemies or both.

#### Storm Call
At 8th level, the Thundercaller can use her performance to call down Lightning that deals 3d6 electricity damage, one per round for as long as her performance lasts. The lightning bolts do 3d10 damage in a storm.

#### Storm Call, Greater
At 14th level, the Stormcaller's lightning bolts do 5d6 damage (5d10 in a storm).

#### Inspire Tranquility
Affected allies receive a +1 morale bonus to AC and any rage effects are suppressed. At 5th level and every six class levels thereafter, the bonus increases by +1 (maximum +4 at 17th level).

#### Saving Tranquility
At 7th level, Inspire Tranquility also grants a bonus to saves against mind-affecting spells equal to the AC bonus.

#### Tranquil Senses
At 13th level, Inspire Tranquility also grants blindsense with a 10' range.

#### Tranquil Apotheosis
At 20th level, a Tranquil Whisperer gains immunity to mind-affecting effects and is classed as an outsider.
