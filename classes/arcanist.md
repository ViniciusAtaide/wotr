# Arcanist
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Arcanist has the following characteristics:

- Primary attributes: Dexterity, Intelligence and Charisma
- Slow (1/2) AB progression
- Full caster level progression
- May cast arcane spells up to 9th-level
- Spells must be prepared in advance
- Prepared spells may be cast in any combination
- Uses the Wizard spell list
- 5 HP per level
- High saving throws for Will, low saving throws for Fortitude and Reflex
- 2 skill points per level.

Class Skills: Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Lore (Religion) and Use Magic Device

#### Arcanist Proficiencies
Arcanists are proficient with all simple weapons.

#### Arcane Reservoir
The Arcanist has a reservoir of magical energy. Its maximum capacity is her class level +3 and when she rests, the reservoir is partially filled to half her class level +3. When casting a spell, the Arcanist may spend a point from her reservoir as a free action to increase either the effective caster level or the DC of the spell by +1.

#### Consume Spells
The Arcanist may sacrifice a casting of a memorised spell to add points to her Arcane Reservoir, gaining 1 point for each level of the sacrificed spell.

#### Arcanist Exploits
Exploits cost a varying number of Arcane Reservoir points. When an exploit requires a saving throw, the DC is 10 + half the Arcanist's level + the Arcanist's Charisma modifier.

##### Acid Jet
Cost: 1

The Arcanist makes a ranged touch attack to launch a jet of acid at a target within close range. The jet does 1d6 acid damage + the Arcanist's Charisma modifier. For every 2 levels beyond 1st, the jet does an additional 1d6 damage (max 10d6 at 19th level). The target is also sickened for 1d4 rounds unless it makes a Fortitude saving throw.

##### Arcane Barrier
Cost: 1

The Arcanist creates a barrier that provides a temporary number of HP equal to her class level + her Charisma modifier. The barrier lasts for 1 minute per class level. If the Arcanist uses this exploit more than once, the cost goes up by 1 each time to a maximum of 3.

##### Armored Mask
Cost: 1

The Arcanist gains the benefits of *Mage Armor* with a caster level equal to her class level. If she already has *Mage Armor* active, she receives the benefits of *Shield of Faith* instead.

##### Dimensional Slide
Cost: 1

The Arcanist can use her Move action to move to any location within close range.

##### Energy Shield
Cost: 1

The Arcanist gains resistance 10 against an energy type for 1 minute per class level. This increases by 5 per 5 class levels (maximum 30 at 20th level).

##### Flame Arc
Cost: 1

The Arcanist launches a 30' line of flame which does 1d6 fire damage + the Arcanist's Charisma modifier to every creature it touches. For every 2 levels beyond 1st, the flame does an additional 1d6 damage (max 10d6 at 19th level). Affected creatures may attempt a Reflex save to halve the damage received.

##### Force Strike
Cost: 1

The attack automatically strikes one target within 30' and does 1d4 points of Force damage + 1 per class level. Spells and effects which negate *Magic Missile* also negate this ability.

##### Holy Water Jet
Cost: 1
Prerequisites:
Good alignment

The Arcanist launches a 30' jet of holy water which does 1d8 holy damage + the Arcanist's Charisma modifier to every susceptible creature it touches. For every 2 levels beyond 1st, the flame does an additional 1d8 damage (max 10d8 at 19th level). Affected creatures may attempt a Reflex save to halve the damage received.

##### Ice Missile
Cost: 1

The Arcanist makes a ranged touch attack to launch a freezing projectile at a target within close range. The missile does 1d6 cold damage + the Arcanist's Charisma modifier. For every 2 levels beyond 1st, the missile does an additional 1d6 damage (max 10d6 at 19th level). The target is also staggered for 1 round unless it makes a Fortitude saving throw.

##### Lightning Lance
Cost: 1

The Arcanist makes a ranged touch attack to launch a charged projectile at a target within close range. The projectile does 1d6 electricity damage + the Arcanist's Charisma modifier. For every 2 levels beyond 1st, the projectile does an additional 1d6 damage (max 10d6 at 19th level). The target's vision is also impaired for 1 round (20% miss chance) unless it makes a Fortitude saving throw.

##### Potent Magic
When the Arcanist uses a point from her reservoir to increase the caster level or DC of a spell, the bonus is +2 rather than +1.

##### Shadow Veil
Cost: 1

The Arcanist gains concealment (20% miss chance) and +5 bonus to Stealth for a number of rounds equal to 1 + her Charisma bonus.

##### Sonic Blast
Cost: 1

The Arcanist makes a ranged touch attack to strike a target within 30' with a blast of sonic energy. The blast does 1d6 sonic damage + the Arcanist's Charisma modifier. For every 2 levels beyond 1st, the blast does an additional 1d6 damage (max 10d6 at 19th level). The target may attempt a Fortitude save to halve the damage received.

##### Spell Resistance
Cost: 1

The Arcanist gains 6 + class level spell resistance for a number of rounds equal to her Charisma modifier.

##### Swift Consume
The Arcanist can use *Consume Spells* as a swift action rather than a move action.

##### Wooden Flesh
Cost: 1

The Arcanist gains a +2 natural armor bonus to AC and slashing DR equal to her Charisma modifier for 1 minute per class level.

This ability used to change your creature type to plant, giving you a bunch of immunities but this is no longer the case. You only count as a plant for the purpose of spells and abilities which have special effects on plants.

#### Greater Exploits
These unlock at 11th level.

##### Burning Flame
Cost: 2
Prerequisites:
Flame Arc

Affected targets catch fire if they fail their saving throws, taking an additional 3d6 fire damage at the start of subsequent turns. The target may attempt a Reflex save each round to extinguish the flames.

##### Dancing Electricity
Cost: 2
Prerequisites:
Lightning Lance

Creatures adjacent to the target take half the amount of electricity damage dealt to the target. They may attempt a Reflex save to halve this damage.

##### Greater Spell Resistance
Cost: 1
Prerequisites:
Spell Reistance

The Arcanist gains 11 + class level spell resistance.

##### Hellfire Ray
Cost: 2
Prerequisites:
Flame Arc

Half the damage inflicted is unholy damage which is not affected by fire resistance. Good creatures struck by the ray are sickened for 1d4 rounds unless they make a Will save.

##### Icy Tomb
Cost: 2
Prerequisites:
Icy Missile

If the target fails its Fortitude save against the Ice Missile, it is covered in ice for 1 minute per caster level. The target is entangled and takes 1 point of Dexterity damage at the start of each of its turns. The target can break free by making a Strength check with a DC equal to 10 + the Arcanist's Charisma modifier.

##### Lingering Acid
Cost: 2
Prerequisites:
Acid Jet

If the target fails its saving throw against the Acid Jet, it takes additional acid damage over time. On each subsequent round, the target takes 1d6 points of acid damage for each 2d6 damage inflicted in the previous round (e.g. at 19th level, the damage inflicted will be 5d6 on the second round, 2d6 on the third round and 1d6 on the fourth).

#### Magical Supremacy
At 20th level, the Arcanist can cast any spell she has prepared by expending a number of points from her Arcane Reservoir equal to the level of the spell to be cast.

##### Playing an Arcanist
An Arcanist plays similarly to a Wizard or a Sorcerer. Compared to a Sorcerer, you don't have to wait until you level up to learn a new spell - you just copy a scroll. Compared to a Wizard, you can cast your favourite spells until you run out of juice. However, compared to a bloodline or five bonus feats combined with some of the specialist wizard abilities, a handful of exploits aren't that great. You will also find the number of spell slots (no more than 3 for higher level spells) extremely limiting.

Besides the limitations of how Arcanists prepare their spells, the Arcane Reservoir is bugged and will randomly drop to 0 for no apparent reason and you won't notice until you try to increase the caster level or DC of a spell.

Some of the archetypes are straight-up worse than the base class. The Nature Mage and Unlettered Arcanist trade the wizard spellbook for inferior spell selections (Druid and Witch respectively) and get nothing or not enough in return. An Eldritch Font is no better than your ability to deal with the fatigued condition. Yes, you have a Paladin companion but burning all her mercies on your Eldritch Font PC will get tiresome.

A White Mage is slightly superior to the base class. Trading one exploit for spontaneous healing is a very fair trade but if anything that highlights the weakness of most of the exploit abilities. Also, if you need to call on the services of a White Mage to heal your party, you may want to take a long, hard look at your party lineup or at least retreat and rest.

A Magic Deceiver has a unique class mechanic. Despite the (highly) restricted spellbook, some Spell Fusion combinations are very powerful. A simple AoE Hideous Laughter can disable enemies en-masse more effectively than Best Jokes. Later on, you can combine Phantasmal Killer with a Fireball or Greater Dispel Magic with Chain Lightning. Indeed, since dispelling enemy buffs is the difference between an unbeatable and easy boss fight, Magic Deceivers can be the best dispellers, especially since they should have the feat capacity for (Greater) Dispel Focus (Metamagic feats are more or less useless and you may find that you can get away without taking Spell Penetration feats). The best subclass is Living Deity since the Nature Mystery, for example, can give you an animal companion and the capstone abilities are excellent. The best Mythic path is Azata since fused spells benefit from Favorable Magic and Zippy Magic.

A Phantasmal Mage is an interesting and flavourful choice. However, given that a gnome receives a bonus to her *Charisma*, an Arcane Sorcerer with a School Power choice of Illusion is simply better in every way.

Finally, the Brown-Fur Transmuter *should* be good. Casting *Legendary Proportions* on a high-DPR party member is already one of the best uses of a 7th-level spell slot and Powerful Change supercharges it. The ability to cast spells with personal range on allies is one shared with an Alchemist and the BFT has the entire Wizard spell book to play with so that you can stick Dragonkind III on an animal companion, for example, or Frightful Aspect on a frontliner. However, a couple of factors make it not fun to play. One of them is the bug with the Arcane Reservoir dropping randomly to 0 which affects a BFT worse because the class's signature abilities use the reservoir. The other is that using the signature abilities is simply tedious: selecting a modifier, finding the spell that you want to modify and then clicking on the target gets very old very quickly.

#### Powerful Change
When the Transmuter casts a Transmutation spell, she may bolster it with 1 point from her Arcane Reservoir. If the spell grants a bonus to an ability score, the bonus increases by 2.

The duration of this ability is *extremely* short. Activate it, pause the game, click on the spell you want to boost and unpause the game. Otherwise, it is liable simply to waste a point from your Arcane Reservoir.

#### Shared Transmutation
The Transmuter can expend 1 point from her Arcane Reservoir to change the range of a Transmutation spell from Personal to Touch.

You cannot use Shared Transmutation *and* Powerful Change on the same spell.

#### Transmutation Supremacy
Whenever the Transmuter casts a Transmutation spell, it is treated as though it were affected by Extend Spell Metamagic. When using Powerful Change, the ability score bonus increases to +4. The range of Share Transmutation increases to close.

#### Font of Power
An Eldritch Font gains one additional slot for each level of spell she can cast. However, the number of spells of each level that she can prepare each day is reduced by 1. If this is 0, she can still cast spells of that level, but these need to be lower level spells enhanced by Metamagic.

#### Eldritch Surge
As a swift action, an Eldritch Font can increase her effective caster level and DC of a spell by +2 or add +2 to her effective level when using an exploit. This leaves her fatigued (or exhausted if already fatigued). If she is exhausted, she cannot use this ability at all.

#### Improved Surge
An Eldritch Font can use her Eldritch Surge ability to make two attack rolls associated with a spell and take the better result.

#### Greater Surge
An Eldritch Font can use her Eldritch Surge ability to force a creature to roll its saving throw against a spell or exploit twice and take the worse result.

#### Bottomless Well
Once per day, as a full-round action, an Eldritch Font can prepare new spells and regain Arcane Reservoir points equal to half her level.

#### Magic Fusion
A Magic Deceiver may craft up to 10 new spells by combining the effects and characteristics of two spells that they know.

The following rules apply:

- The level of the new spell is the higher level of the component spells
- The DC of the new spell is the higher DC of the component spells
- The school is either of the component spells' schools
- Spell Focus and / or Elemental Focus applies to the resulting spell DC
- If both component spells target different saving throws, you may choose which saving throw to target
- The shape is either of the component spells' shape (single target, line, cone, burst)
- The fused spell cannot have metamagic applied to it
- A spell which has had metamagic applied cannot be a component spell.

#### Ways of the Deceiver
At 1st level, a Magic Deceiver must choose one of six subclasses: Fake Celebrity, Living Deity, Magic Inventor, Razmiri Infiltrator, Time Manipulator or Way of the Charlatan.

##### Inhibit Courage
From 1st level, a Fake Celebrity can use her performance to inflict a -1 penalty on attack and damage rolls and saving throws against charm and fear effects. At 5th level and every six subsequent levels, this penalty increases by -1 to a maximum of -4 at 17th level. She can use this ability for a number of rounds equal to her class level + her Charisma modifier.

Fake Celebrity performance abilities benefit from the Extra Performance and Lingering Performance feats but not from the Mythic Inspiration ability.

##### Trick Magic
From 2nd level, a Fake Celebrity gains a bonus to concentration checks equal to the number of ranks she has in Persuasion.

##### Spell Fabrication
From 4th level, a Fake Celebrity can fabricate a spell from her spell book by making a Persuasion (Bluff) check (DC = 10 + twice the spell level). She can use this ability once per day plus an additional time for every four additional levels (maximum five times per day as 20th level).

##### Inhibit Greatness
From 12th level, a Fake Celebrity can use her performance to inflict a -2 penalty to attack rolls, a -1 penalty to saving throws and a -3 penalty to caster level checks to overcome spell resistance. At 17th level, the caster level penalty increases to -4.

##### Inhibit Heroics
From 20th level, a Fake Celebrity can use her performance to inflict a -4 penalty to AC and saving throws.

##### Mysterious Gift
A Living Deity can only be an Atheist. She gains an Oracle's Mystery and Curse class features (although she receives no benefits from the curse when levelling up). Her Charisma attribute is used for Lore (Religion) checks.

##### Mysterious Gift - Mystery
A Living Deity must select a Mystery and receives Revelations appropriate to the Mystery at 1st, 4th, 12th and 20th levels along with the Final Revelation.

##### Mysterious Gift - Curse
A Living Deity must select a curse from the Oracle list.

If you take the Pranked curse, you do not receive the *Faerie Fire* spell. A Living Deity's curse does not unlock the Beneficial Curse Mythic ability.

##### Pious Guile
From 2nd level, a Living Deity gains a bonus to concentration checks equal to the number of ranks she has in Lore (Religion).

##### Godly Fraud
From 12th level, a Living Deity receives a bonus to caster level checks to overcome spell resistance equal to half the number of ranks she has in Lore (Religion).

##### Hollow Spark
At 20th level, a Living Deity may select two domains, gaining any abilities granted by these domains.

##### Advance Time
At 1st level, as a swift action, A Time Manipulator can speed up time for herself or an ally. The duration of all effects affecting the target are reduced by a number of rounds equal to the Time Manipulator's class level. She can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 1 + her Charisma modifier.

##### Chronomancy
From 4th level, a Time Manipulator can restore her spells and abilities or those of an ally as if they had taken a full rest. However, the DCs of spells and abilities are reduced by -1 until the recipient rests properly and the recipient cannot be targeted again during this time. She can use this ability once per day plus an additional time for every four additional levels (maximum five times per day as 20th level). This ability cannot be used in combat.

##### Split Second
From 12th level, a Time Manipulator can make Persuasion (Bluff) checks in place of Reflex saving throws.

##### Split Timeline
At 20th level, a Time Manipulator can return herself or an ally back to the state they were in after a complete rest, restoring all spells, abilities and HP and getting rid of any effects that are removed on rest. This ability can only be used once per combat.

#### Arcane Metamagic
The Phantasmal Mage cannot use Arcane Reservoir points to increase her effective caster level. However at levels 5, 11 and 17 she can apply increasingly powerful metamagic abilities to Illusion spells.

##### Arcane Metamagic I
The Phantasmal Mage can spend 1 point from her Arcane Reservoir to apply Extended, Reach or Selective to the next Illusion spell she casts.

##### Arcane Metamagic II
The Phantasmal Mage can spend 2 points from her Arcane Reservoir to apply Persistent or Empowered to the next Illusion spell she casts.

##### Arcane Metamagic III
The Phantasmal Mage can spend 3 points from her Arcane Reservoir to apply Maximized to the next Illusion spell she casts or 4 points to apply Quickened.

#### Metamagic Mastery
The cost of the Phantasmal Mage's metamagic abilities is reduced by 1 point.
