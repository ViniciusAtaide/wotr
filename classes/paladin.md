# Paladin
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Paladin has the following characteristics:

- Primary attributes: Strength, Constitution and Charisma
- Full AB progression
- Caster level progression from level 4
- May cast divine spells up to 4th-level
- Spells are prepared
- 7 HP per level
- High saving throws for Fortitude and Will, low saving throws for Reflex
- 2 skill points per level
- Alignment must be Lawful Good.

Class Skills: Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Lore (Religion), Persuasion

#### Paladin Proficiencies
Paladins are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, all types of armour and shields.

#### Alignment Restriction
A Paladin who strays from Lawful Good loses all class powers until she changes her alignment back.

#### Smite Evil
Once per day, the Paladin may Smite Evil as a swift action. If the target is evil, the Paladin adds her Charisma modifier to attack rolls and her Paladin levels to damage rolls until the enemy dies. Smite Evil bypasses any DR the foe might possess and the Paladin also gains a deflection bonus to AC equal to her Charisma modifier against attacks made by the target of the smite.

At 4th level and every 3 levels thereafter, the Paladin gains an additional use of Smite Evil (max 7/day at 19th level).

#### Divine Grace
At 2nd level, the Paladin gains a bonus equal to her Charisma modifier on all saving throws.

#### Lay On Hands
At 2nd level the Paladin may use her Lay On Hands ability to heal 1d6 damage per two class levels as a standard action (others) or swift action (self). Alternatively, she may make a melee touch attack to do equivalent damage to undead. The Paladin may use this ability a number of times per day equal to half her class level + her Charisma modifier.

#### Aura of Courage
At 3rd level, the Paladin is immune to fear. Allies within 10' gain a +4 morale bonus on saves against fear effects.

#### Divine Health
At 3rd level, the Paladin is immune to all diseases.

#### Mercy
At 3rd level and every three levels thereafter, the Paladin may select a Mercy to heal a negative status when she uses her Lay on Hands ability.

#### Channel Positive Energy
At 4th level, the Paladin may channel energy in the same way as a Cleric of her deity. Using this ability consumes two uses of the Lay on Hands ability.

#### Divine Bond
At 5th level, a Paladin may choose to gain a mount or form a bond with her weapon.

##### Animal Companion
The Paladin gains a horse companion. The companion's level is the same as her class level. At 11th level, the mount gains celestial resistances and the ability to Smite Evil. At 15th level, the mount gains spell resistance equal to class level + 11.

##### Divine Weapon Bond
At 5th level, once per day, the Paladin may enhance her weapon as a standard action, granting a +1 enhancement bonus for 1 minute per class level. For every three levels above 5th, the weapon gains another +1 enhancement bonus (maximum +6 at 20th level). The bonus stacks with any existing weapon enhancement to a maximum of +5.

Alternatively, the Paladin may sacrifice some or all of the enhancement bonus to add properties to her weapon. The available properties and the enchantment cost of each one are shown in the table below.

The maximum enchantment of any weapon is +5 so you will need to spend any left over enchantment points on properties. The ability description also mentions "defending" but this property is not implemented. Weapon properties are detailed in the items section.

#### Aura of Resolve
At 8th level, a Paladin is immune to charm spells and spell-like abilities. Allies within 10' gain a +4 morale bonus on saving throws against charm effects.

#### Mark of Justice
At 11th level, the Paladin may expend two uses of her Smite Evil ability to grant all the bonuses of Smite Evil to her allies for 1 minute.

The Paladin herself does not benefit from Mark of Justice.

#### Aura of Faith
At 14th level, a Paladin's weapons are treated as good-aligned for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction. The bonus applies to an ally's attacks if they are within 10'.

#### Aura of Righteousness
At 17th level, the Paladin gains DR 5/evil and is immune to compulsion spells and spell-like abilities. Allies within 10' gain a +4 morale bonus on saving throws against compulsion effects.

#### Holy Champion
At 20th level, the Paladin's DR increases to 10/evil and the effects of Lay on Hands and Channel Energy are maximised.

##### Playing a Paladin
Smite Evil is incredibly powerful against evil foes and Mark of Justice gives this benefit to the whole party. In *this* game, most enemies are evil so this is a bona-fide superpower. That aside, a Paladin is badly feat-starved so any build will end up slightly compromised.

The Paladin is a good class for dipping into. The prize is Divine Grace at level 2. A 3rd level also brings benefits but that feels more like a career than a dip. If you dip into Divine Hunter, you get Precise Shot for free.

A Divine Guardian overcomes the feat starvation (to a degree - only two of the bonus feats are essential) at the cost of disabling spellcasting. The Paladin spell list isn't amazing for the most part but there are a handful of worthwhile spells that you'd miss out on. Two of those confer sacred AC bonuses so being able to use Divine Troth on yourself partly makes up for it.

A Divine Hunter gives up a lot (Mark of Justice and the possibility of a mount) for - essentially - the Precise Shot feat. The other class abilities aren't great since it would be a rather strange party line-up that gained much benefit from Shared Precision and Hunter's Blessing.

Without the auras, which are OK, a Divine Scion feels like a cut-down, feat starved Slayer. It loses the best bits of a Paladin and the talents of a Slayer and simply feels compromised.

Death immunity from the Hospitaler archetype is good, but this can be replicated while Mark of Justice cannot be. You also lose half your Smites which is not good.

The Martyr feels like a very masochistic class to play. The fact that it's the whole point of the class doesn't stop it from being weird. The songs are nice if you only wanted a Bard for Inspire Courage anyway but losing Smite Evil, immunities and the save bonuses is a major downgrade from the base class.

The Stonelord isn't the worst race-specific prototype by a long shot but Paladin seems like a poor choice for a race with a Charisma penalty. However, since you don't get the Charisma-based save bonuses, the penalty isn't as bad as it might be. The Stonelord trades the class superpowers for lots of defensive bonuses. +6 AC and immunity to critical hits is good but the end result is an alignment-restricted Stalwart Defender and that class is distinctly mid-tier.

A Tortured Crusader is less party-friendly than a regular Paladin but is otherwise quite stacked. Her smite ability is not as strong but can be used against non-evil enemies (not that there are a lot of those). The 11th level upgrade, Final Justice, is weaker than Mark of Justice but the capstone ability is a bona fide superpower. A Tortured Crusader also receives bonus combat feats while retaining spellcasting for the best of all possible worlds.

Finally, the Warrior of Light trades spellcasting and the useful Aura of Faith for a rather useless AoE attack. Extra uses of Lay on Hands are OK, but I can't see why you'd pick the archetype over the base class.

#### Divine Troth
The Divine Guardian pledges her protection to a willing creature for the day. In combat, you sacrifice one of your attacks of opportunity to grant your troth a +4 Dodge bonus to AC.

You can cast Divine Troth on yourself to grant yourself a +4 Dodge bonus to AC for the day. Unless you have Combat Reflexes, you won't be getting any attacks of opportunity but the benefit is very worthwhile.

#### Guarding Hands
Starting at 2nd level, this ability is identical to Lay on Hands except that healing your divine troth is a swift action as well.

#### Courageous Defense
This is supposed to allow you to intercept attacks aimed at your divine troth but the feats that it emulates are not implemented. It does grant the Divine Guardian immunity to fear, but allies do not receive a save bonus.

#### Divine Guardian Bonus Feat
At 7th, 10th and 13th levels, the Divine Guardian may choose bonus feats from the following list: Combat Reflexes, Diehard, Dodge, Endurance, (Improved) Great Fortitude, (Improved) Iron Will, (Improved) Lightning Reflexes, Missile Shield, Shield Focus, Toughness, Tower Shield Proficiency.

#### Divine Hunter Proficiencies
Divine Hunters are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, light and medium armour and shields.

#### Shared Precision
At 3rd level, when a Divine Hunter hits a creature with a ranged attack, she grants her allies within 10' the benefit of Precise Shot against that target for 1 round.

#### Divine Hunter Bond
Starting at 5th level, this behaves identically to Divine Weapon Bond, except that it is applied to the Divine Hunter's ranged weapon.

#### Distant Mercy
At 6th level, the Divine Hunter can expend two uses of her Lay On Hands ability to heal a distant target no further away than 5' per class level.

#### Aura of Clear Sight
At 8th level, a Paladin is immune to blindness. Allies within 10' gain a +4 morale bonus on saving throws against blindness effects.

#### Hunter's Blessing
At 11th level, a Divine Hunter can expend a use of her smite evil ability as a swift action to grant herself and all non-evil allies within 10' the Deadly Aim, Precise Shot and Improved Precise Shot feats for 1 minute.

#### Righteous Hunter
At 14th level, a Divine Hunter's ranged weapons are treated as good-aligned for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction. The bonus applies to an ally's ranged attacks if they are within 10'.

#### Stigmata
As a standard action, a martyr can chant devotional hymns for a number of rounds per day equal to 4 + her Charisma modifier plus 1 round for each additional level after 1st. While chanting, she takes 1 point of bleed damage per round.

At 7th level, Stigmata becomes a move action and at 13th level, it becomes a swift action.

#### See No Evil, Hear No Evil
At 2nd level, the Martyr and allies within 20' gain a +4 morale bonus to saves against sonic effects and gaze attacks.

#### Aura of Health
At 3rd level, the Martyr and allies within 20' gain a +4 morale bonus to saves against disease.

#### Aura of Courage (Martyr)
At 3rd level, the Martyr and allies within 20' gain a +4 morale bonus to saves against fear.

#### Aura Mastery
The Martyr's auras of courage, resolve and righteousness have an increased radius but the Martyr does not gain immunity to the effects.

#### Martyr's Mercy
At 3rd level, a Martyr can remove any condition if she meets the level requirement. However, if she doesn't have the related Mercy, she takes the condition onto herself.

#### Stonestrike
Once per day per class level, a Stonelord treats her attacks as magical and adamantine for 1 round. She gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls, increasing by +1 at 5th level and every five levels thereafter (max +5 at 20th level).

#### Heartstone
At 2nd level, a Stonelord gains a +1 natural armour bonus to AC and adamantine DR equal to her class level. The AC bonus increases by +1 at 6th level and every four levels thereafter (max +5 at 18th level).

#### Stoneblood
At 3rd level, a Stonelord gains a 25% chance to ignore a critical hit or precision damage. At 9th level, this chance increases to 50% and she becomes immune to petrification. At 15th level, this chance increases to 75% and she becomes immune to bleed.

#### Stone Body
At 20th level, a Stone Lord becomes immune to paralysis, poison and stun. She is also immune to critical hits and precision damage.

#### All is Darkness
Once per day, the Tortured Crusader may smite an enemy as a swift action. The Crusader adds half her Wisdom modifier to attack rolls and her class level to damage rolls until the enemy dies. The attack bypasses any DR the foe might possess and the Paladin also gains a +4 deflection bonus to AC against attacks made by the target of the smite.

At 5th level and every 5 levels thereafter, the Crusader gains an additional use of this ability (max 5/day at 20th level).

#### Self-Sufficient
A Tortured Crusader gains 3 skill points per level and bonus combat feats at 2nd, 8th, 12th and 16th levels. Her lay on hands ability and auras do not affect others.

#### Torment
A Tortured Crusader uses Wisdom for spellcasting and her lay on hands ability.

#### Second Chance
At 2nd level a Tortured Crusader can restore 1d6 damage per two class levels as a swift action. This ability can only heal her own wounds and she can use this ability a number of times per day equal to half her class level + her Wisdom modifier. She can convert two uses of this ability into an additional smite.

From 8th level, she restores 1d8 damage per two class levels and at 15th level, the healing is maximised.

#### Alone in the Dark
From 2nd level, a Tortured Crusader gains a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls and a +1 bonus to AC for 1 minute whenever an ally within 50' drops to 0 HP.

#### Final Justice
At 11th level, a Tortured Crusader can expend two uses of her smite ability to double the bonus damage. The duration of the effect is reduced to 1 minute.

#### Unbroken Paragon
At 20th level, a Tortured Crusader gains three additional uses of her smite and lay on hands abilities. When she smites an enemy, the target must make a Fortitude save (DC = 30 + WIS) or be stunned for 2d4 rounds. In addition, when making a full attack, she gains an additional attack at full BAB. This attack stacks with Haste and weapons with the speed ability.

#### Power of Faith
At 4th level, the Warrior gains an additional use of Lay on Hands and gains one additional use every four subsequent levels. As a standard action, she can spend a use of Lay on Hands to create a 30' radius nimbus for 1 minute. Allies in the area of effect receive a +1 morale bonus to AC, attack rolls, damage rolls and saving throws against fear effects.

#### Shining Light
At 14th level, as a standard action, a Warrior of Holy Light can create a 30' burst of white light. Evil creatures in the area of effect take 1d6 damage per 2 class levels and are blinded for 1 round. A Reflex save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + Charisma modifier) halves the damage and negates the blindness. Evil dragons, outsiders and undead are blinded for 1d4 rounds. Good creatures are healed 1d6 damage per two class levels and receive a +2 sacred bonus to ability checks, attack rolls, skill checks and saving throws for 1 round.

The Warrior gains an additional use of this ability at 17th and 20th levels.
