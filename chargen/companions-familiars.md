## Animal Companions and Familiars
A number of classes may select an Animal Companion at character creation:

- Mad Dog Barbarian
- Beast Rider Cavalier
- Cavalier, Fearsome Leader, Gendarme, Knight of the Wall (Horse only)
Cavalier of the Paw (Dog or Wolf)
- Ghost Rider (Ghost Mount)

- Primal Druid (Mastodon, Smilodon, Triceratops or Velociraptor)
- Winter Child (Blizzard Servant)

- Divine Hound (Dog or Wolf)
- Urban Hunter (Dog or Horse)

Certain other classes will gain an Animal Companion at class level 4:

- Cleric (Animal Domain)
- Inquisitor (Animal Domain)
- Ranger

A Bloodrider gains a Horse at class level 5. A Paladin (but not Tortured Crusader) may use Divine Bond to acquire a Horse at class level 5 as well. An Oracle with the Nature mystery gains a companion via a Revelation and a Shaman with the Nature spirit gains a companion of choice at class level 16.

Animal companions increase in level each subsequent class level. This means that a Ranger's animal companion will be three levels below a Sacred Huntmaster's companion. You can fix this by taking the Boon Companion feat. At a certain level (generally 7 but horses and leopards grow at level 4), an animal will grow and become more powerful. Besides the physical attribute changes, the companion's base weapon damage goes up by a size category (so that a large dog's bite will do a base 1d8 damage before modifiers).

At the very least, your companion is another body to throw on the battlefield but a companion that trips opponents will also help you control the battlefield. If they fall in battle, they will return after resting.

The second set of attributes are for the evolved form.

Special companion abilities are as follows:

- **Ferocity**
When boars fall to 0 HP, they do not immediately fall unconscious. They lose 1 HP per round and are Staggered but remain in the fight. When negative HP equal its Constitution score, the companion will die.
- **Pounce**
A leopard, smilodon or velociraptor companion can perform a full attack at the end of a charge.
- **Rake**
When hitting with both claw attacks, the companion will perform a third claw attack.
- **Trample**
A mastodon can perform the Overrun combat manoeuvre, moving through an opponent's square, inflicting damage and knocking it over.

### Companion Feats
All companions gain feats at odd-numbered levels just like their masters and may gain bonus feats depending on the chosen archetype.

### Companion Skills
Companions receive 2 skill points / level. Class skills are Athletics, Mobility, Stealth and Perception. You may teach a companion any other skill if you're so inclined.

### Companion Gear
An animal companion can equip items in the head, neck, cloak, arm and belt slots. With the right armour feats, they can equip barding as well.

#### Enhanced Attacks
A companion's attacks are treated as magical for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction. For every 3HD the companion has, it gains a +1 enhancement bonus on attack rolls and damage and a +1 resistance bonus to saving throws.

#### Devotion
A companion gains a +4 morale bonus on Will saves vs. enchantment spells and effects.

#### Multiattack
If a companion has at least three attacks, attacks with secondary weapons are made at a -2 AB penalty rather than -5.

#### Bleeding Strike
If the first attack in a round succeeds, the enemy bleeds for an additional 1d6 damage each round. The Aggressor gains +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls against an affected enemy. This bonus increases by an additional +1 at levels 10, 15 and 20.

#### Bullying Thrill
Whenever the companion succeeds at a bull rush, overrun or trip combat maneuver check, it gains a +2 morale bonus to attack and damage rolls until the end of its next turn.

#### Artful Acrobat
The companion gains a competence bonus to Mobility checks equal to half its level.

#### Devil May Care
The companion can't be flanked.

#### One Foot in the Grave
The companion gains a +4 morale bonus to Fortitude saves against effects that could not normally affect undead.

#### Fast Movement
The companion's speed is 10' greater than normal when wearing no armour and carrying a light load. This increases to 20' at 6th level.

#### Destructive Wrecker
The companion's natural attacks ignore an inanimate object's DR and count as adamantine for the purposes of overcoming a construct's DR.

### Familiars
The following classes can gain a familiar:

- Unlettered Arcanist
- Arcane Sorcerer
- Shaman (not Spirit Hunter or Wildland Shaman)
- Witch (not Ley Line Guardian)
- Wizard (any)
- Kineticist (via Elemental Whispers)
- Rogue / Archaeologist / Slayer (via Advanced Talents)
- Loremaster (via Rogue Secret).
