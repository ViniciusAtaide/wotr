# Bloodlines
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*
_Part of chargen/: races, backgrounds, attributes-skills, domains, bloodlines, companions-familiars._

## Bloodlines
You must select a bloodline for the following classes:

- Bloodrager
- Dragonheir Scion Fighter
- Eldritch Scion Magus
- Sorcerer

Note that Empyreal Sorcerers, Sage Sorcerers and Sylvan Sorcerers have fixed bloodlines.

If you are intending to take Dragon Disciple levels, you *must* select one of the Draconic bloodlines.

At level 3 and at odd-numbered levels thereafter, Sorcerers and Eldritch Scions may select a bonus spell related to their bloodline which adds to the spells they learn from levelling up normally. Bloodragers gain bonus spells from level 7. At levels 7, 13 and 19 a Sorcerer character selects a bonus feat related to their bloodline.

### Abyssal Bloodline
You have demonic ancestry. The bloodline has the following characteristics:

- Class Skill: Athletics
- Bloodline feats: Augment Summoning, Bull Rush, Cleave, Empower Spell, Great Fortitude, Power Attack, Skill Focus (Lore: Religion), Sunder Armor.
- Bonus spells:

#### Abyssal Bloodline Arcana
Whenever you cast a spell from the summoning subschool, the creatures summoned gain DR/good equal to half your class level.

#### Grow Claws (Abyssal)
This is a free action and grants two attacks that do 1-4 base damage. At level 5, the claws are considered magical for the purposes of overcoming DR. At level 7, the damage increases to 1-6 and at 11th level, the claws deal an additional 1-6 points of fire damage. The ability can be used a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.

#### Demon Resistances
At 3rd level, you gain resist electricity 5 and a +2 bonus to saves vs. poison. At 9th level, the resistance increases to 10 and the save bonus increases to +4.

#### Strength of the Abyss
At 9th level, you gain a +2 inherent bonus to Strength. This increases to +4 at level 13 and +6 at level 17.

#### Added Summonings
At 15th level, you increase the number of creatures you summon like the Superior Summoning feat.

#### Wings (Abyssal)
At 15th level, you gain the Wings feat.

#### Demonic Might
At 20th level, you gain immunity to electricity and poison. You also gain resistance to acid 10, cold 10 and fire 10.

### Arcane Bloodline
You are descended from a long line of spellcasters. The bloodline has the following characteristics:

- Class Skill: one of Knowledge (World), Lore (Nature), Lore (Religion).
- Bloodline feats: Combat Casting, Improved Initiative, Iron Will, Reach Spell, Skill Focus (Knowledge: Arcana), Spell Focus, Greater Spell Focus, Spell Penetration.
- Bonus Spells:
LvlSpellSchool1Magic MissileEvocation2InvisibilityIllusion3Dispel MagicAbjuration4Dimension DoorConjuration5Break EnchantmentAbjuration6True SeeingDivination7BanishmentAbjuration8Power Word StunEnchantment9Clashing RocksConjuration

#### Arcane Bond
Gain a bonded object or a familiar.

#### Arcane Bloodline Arcana
When you apply a metamagic feat (other than Heighten Spell), increase the spell's DC by 1.

#### Combat Casting Adept
At 3rd level, gain +2 bonus on concentration checks. This increases to +3 at level 9 and +4 at level 15. At 20th level, the ability is replaced by Arcane Apotheosis.

#### New Arcana
At 9th, 13th and 17th levels, you may add another spell to your list of spells known.

#### School Power
At 15th level, pick one school of magic. The DC for any spells you case from this school increases by +2. This bonus stacks with the bonus granted by Spell Focus.

#### Arcane Apotheosis
At 20th level, all concentration checks to cast a spell or use a spell-like ability succeed automatically.

### Celestial Bloodline
You have angelic ancestry. The bloodline has the following characteristics:

- Class Skill: Lore (Religion)
- Bonus Feats: Agile Maneuvers, Augment Summoning, Dodge, Extend Spell, Iron Will, Skill Focus (Lore: Religion), Superior Summoning, Weapon Finesse.
- Bonus spells:
LvlSpellSchool1BlessEnchantment2Resist EnergyAbjuration3Protection from EnergyAbjuration4Remove CurseConjuration5Flame StrikeEvocation6Dispel Magic, GreaterAbjuration7BanishmentAbjuration8SunburstEvocation9Summon Monster IXConjuration

#### Celestial Bloodline Arcana
Whenever you cast a spell from the summoning subschool, the creatures summoned gain DR/evil equal to half your class level.

#### Heavenly Fire
Unleash a ray that does 1-4 damage to evil creatures + half your class level. The ray heals good creatures by the same amount. The ability can be used a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.

#### Celestial Resistances
At 3rd level, you gain resist acid 5 and resist cold 5. At 9th level, the resistances increase to 10.

#### Aura of Heaven
At 9th level, you gain the Aura of Heaven ability. All allies within 20' gain a +4 deflection bonus to AC and a +4 resistance bonus to saving throws against attacks from evil creatures for one minute. You can use this a number of times a day equal to your class level.

#### Conviction
At 15th level, you gain spell resistance against evil spells and spells cast by evil creatures equal to 11 + your class level.

#### Wings (Celestial)
At level 15, you gain the Wings feat.

#### Ascension
At 20th level, you gain immunity to acid, cold and petrification, resist electricity 10, resist fire 10 and +4 bonus to saves vs. poison.

### Draconic Bloodline
You can only take a single draconic bloodline so that you cannot take, for example, Red Dragon as your main bloodline and Gold Dragon via the Second Bloodline mythic ability to gain twice the damage bonus from the Bloodline Arcana.

You have draconic blood somewhere in your ancestry. Every Draconic bloodline has certain characteristics in common:

- Class Skill: Perception
- Bloodline feats: Blind Fight, Great Fortitude, Improved Initiative, Power Attack, Quicken Spell, Skill Focus (Mobility), Skill Focus (Knowledge: Arcana), Toughness.
- Bonus spells:
LvlSpellSchool1Mage ArmorConjuration2Resist EnergyAbjuration3Dispel MagicAbjuration4FearNecromancy5Spell ResistanceAbjuration6Dragonkind ITransmutation7Dragonkind IITransmutation8Dragonkind IIITransmutation9Overwhelming PresenceEnchantment

#### Draconic Bloodline Arcana
When casting a spell that does damage of the bloodline's element, add +1 point of damage per die rolled

#### Grow Claws (Draconic)
This is a free action and grants two attacks that do 1-4 base damage. At 5th level, the claws are considered magical for the purposes of overcoming DR. At 7th level, the damage increases to 1-6 and at 11th level, the claws deal an additional 1-6 points of elemental damage. The ability can be used a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.

#### Dragon Resistances
At 3rd level, you gain resist 5 vs. your bloodline's element and a +1 natural armour bonus. At 9th level, the resistance increases to 10 and the armour bonus increases to +2. At 15th level, the armour bonus increases to +4.

#### Wings (Draconic)
At level 15, you gain the Wings feat.

#### Power of Wyrms
At 20th level, you gain immunity to paralysis, sleep and element damage. You also gain blindsense with a range of 60'.

### Elemental Bloodline
An elemental outsider is somewhere in your ancestry. The element linked to each specific bloodline is shown in the following table:

| Elemental | Element |
| --- | --- |
| Air | Electricity |
| Earth | Acid |
| Fire | Fire |
| Water | Cold |

Every Elemental bloodline has certain characteristics in common:

- Class Skill: Mobility
- Bloodline feats: Dodge, Empower Spell, Great Fortitude, Improved Initiative, Lightning Reflexes, Power Attack, Skill Focus (Knowledge: Arcana), Weapon Finesse.
- Bonus Spells
LvlSpellSchool1Burning HandsEvocation2Scorching RayEvocation3Protection from EnergyAbjuration4Elemental Body ITransmutation5Elemental Body IITransmutation6Elemental Body IIITransmutation7Elemental Body IVTransmutation8Summon Monster VIIIConjuration9Elemental SwarmConjurationThe *Burning Hands* and *Scorching Ray* spells that are unlocked through the bloodline actually do damage corresponding to the bloodline's element.

#### Elemental Bloodline Arcana
All spells that deal energy damage that you cast will deal damage of a type matching the bloodline's element instead. This also changes the spell's type to match the energy type of your element.

#### Elemental Ray
Unleash an elemental ray as a ranged touch attack, doing 1-6 points of elemental damage plus 1 point for every two class levels. The ability can be used a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.

#### Elemental Resistances
At 3rd level, you gain resistance 10 vs. your bloodline's element. At 9th level, this increases to 20.

#### Elemental Blast
At 9th level, you can unleash a blast of elemental power, 60' range, 20' radius, which does 1-6 damage per class level. Creatures in the blast radius receive a Reflex save (DC 10 + half class level + Charisma modifier) and if they fail, they are vulnerable to your element until the end of your next turn. At 17th level, you can use this ability twice per day. At 20th level, you can use it three times per day.

#### Elemental Movement
At 15th level, you gain a movement ability related your bloodline's element:

- Air: immune to entanglement, slow and difficult terrain
- Earth: None
- Fire: +30' base speed
- Water: Immune to combat maneuvers.

#### Elemental Body
At 20th level you gain immunity to sneak attacks, critical hits and your bloodline's element.

### Fey Bloodline
You have fey ancestry. The bloodline has the following characteristics:

- Class Skill: Lore (Nature)
- Bloodline feats: Agile Maneuvers, Dodge, Improved Initiative, Lightning Reflexes, Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Quicken Spell, Skill Focus (Lore: Nature).
- Bonus spells
LvlSpellSchool1EntangleTransmutation2Hideous LaughterEnchantment3Deep SlumberEnchantment4PoisonNecromancy5VinetrapConjuration6Dispel Magic, GreaterAbjuration7ChangestaffTransmutation8Summon NereidConjuration9Summon HamadryadConjuration

#### Fey Bloodline Arcana
Whenever you cast a spell from the compulsion subschool, increase the spell's DC by +2.

#### Laughing Touch
Cause a creature to burst out laughing for 1 round as a melee touch attack. The affected creature is able to defend itself but can only take a move action. It does not work against creatures immune to mind-affecting spells. The ability can be used a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.

#### Woodland Stride
At 3rd level, you can move through any sort of difficult terrain at normal speed.

#### Fleeting Glance
At 9th level, you can turn invisible for a number of rounds per day equal to your sorcerer level. The ability functions as Greater Invisibility.

#### Fey Magic
At 15th level, you gain +2 on caster level checks to overcome spell resistance. This stacks with (Greater) Spell Penetration.

#### Soul of the Fey
At 20th level, you gain immunity to poison and DR 10/cold iron. Once per day, you can cast Cloak of Dreams using your class level as your caster level.

Whenever you cast a spell from the charm subschool, increase the spell's DC by +2.

#### Corrupting Touch
Cause a creature to become shaken for a number of rounds equal to half your class level round as a melee touch attack. The ability can be used a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.

#### Infernal Resistances
At 3rd level, you gain resist fire 5 and a +2 bonus to saves vs. poison. At 9th level, the resistance increases to 10 and the save bonus increases to +4.

#### Hellfire
At 9th level, you can unleash a column of hellfire, 60' range, 10' radius, which does 1-6 fire damage per class level. Creatures in the blast radius receive a Reflex save (DC 10 + half class level + Charisma modifier) and if they fail, they are shaken for a number of rounds equal to your class level. At 17th level, you can use this ability twice per day. At 20th level, you can use it three times per day.

#### Wings (Infernal)
At level 15, you gain the Wings feat.

#### Power of the Pit
At 20th level, you gain immunity to fire and poison. You also gain resistance to acid 10 and cold 10.

### Serpentine Bloodline
You are in part descended from an ancient serpent race. The bloodline has the following characteristics:

- Class Skill: Stealth
- Bloodline feats: Combat Casting, Combat Reflexes, Deceitful, Deft Hands, Persuasive, Reach Spell, Skill Focus (Persuasion), Stealthy.
- Bonus spells
LvlSpellSchool1HypnotismEnchantment2Delay PoisonConjuration3Summon Monster IIIConjuration4PoisonNecromancy5Hold MonsterEnchantment6TransformationTransmutation7Summon Monster VIIConjuration8Frightful AspectTransmutation9Polar MidnightTransmutation

#### Serpentine Bloodline Arcana
Whenever you cast a mind-affecting or language-dependent spell, it affects animals, magical beasts, monstrous humanoids and vermin the same as humanoids who understand your language.

#### Serpent's Fang
This is a free action and grants an attack that does 1-4 base damage + poison (DC 10 + half class level + Constitution modifier) that does 1 Con damage per round for 6 rounds. At level 5, the fangs are considered magical for the purposes of overcoming DR and the poison damage increases to 1d2 Con. At level 7, the poison requires two successful saves to cure. At level 11, the damage increases to 1d4 Con. The ability can be used a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.

#### Snakeskin
At 9th level, you gain a +1 bonus to natural armour, a +2 racial bonus to saves against poison and a +2 bonus to Mobility checks. At 13th level and 17th level, these bonuses increase by +1.

#### Den of Vermin
Once per day, cast Creeping Doom with differences that the poison does Con damage and that creatures in the area of effect are entangled.

#### Scaled Soul
At 20th level, you can turn yourself into a large Spirit Naga (as Beast Shape III) at will. You become immune to poison and paralysis, may use Serpent's Fangs at will and may choose an attribute to damage.

You have ancestors who became vampires or liches or perhaps you were stillborn but brought back to life. The bloodline has the following characteristics:

- Class Skill: Lore (Religion)
- Bloodline feats: Combat Casting, Diehard, Great Fortitude, Iron Will, Skill Focus (Lore: Religion), Spell Focus, Greater Spell Focus, Toughness.
- Bonus spells

#### Undead Bloodline Arcana
You treat undead creatures as humanoids for the purposes of mind-affecting spells.

#### Grave Touch
Cause a creature to become shaken for a number of rounds equal to half your class level round as a melee touch attack. If you touch a shaken creature, it becomes frightened for 1 round if has fewer HD than your sorcerer level. The ability can be used a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.

#### Death's Gift
At 3rd level you gain resist cold 5 and DR 5/magic. At 9th level, this increases to resist cold 10 and DR 10/magic.

#### Grasp of the Dead
At 9th level, you can cause skeletal arms to burst out of the ground in a 20' radius, causing 1-6 slashing damage per class level. Creatures in the blast radius receive a Reflex save (DC 10 + half class level + Charisma modifier) and if they fail, they are shaken for a number of rounds equal to your class level. At 17th level, you can use this ability twice per day. At 20th level, you can use it three times per day.

#### Incorporeal Form
At 15th level, you can become incorporeal for 1 round per class level. You take no damage from non-magic weapons and half damage from any source not dealing Ghost Touch, Holy, Divine or Force damage.

#### One of Us
At 20th level, you gain immunity to to cold, paralysis and sleep along with DR 5/-. You receive a +4 morale bonus on saving throws vs. spells and spell-like abilities cast by undead.
