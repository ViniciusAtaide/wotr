## Deities and Domains
Any character may choose a deity (or, alternatively, choose atheism) which is something that will crop up in conversation. Certain character classes are powered by their beliefs and gain domain powers derived from their chosen deity.

The following character classes choose one or two domains during character creation:

- Cleric, Angelfire Apostle, Ecclesitheurge, Separatist (choose two)
- Crusader, Demonbane Priest, Herald Caller, Priest of Balance
- Druid, Defender of the True World, Feyspeaker (when not choosing an Animal Companion)
- Blight Druid
- Divine Hunter (Hunter archetype)
- Inquisitor (all apart from Faith Hunter)

#### Air Domain
You can use Lightning Arc (3 + Wisdom modifier) times per day. This is a ranged touch attack, 30' range which does 1-6 lightning damage + 1 for every two class levels. At 6th level, you gain 10 electrical resistance. This increases to 20 at 12th level. At 20th level, you become immune to electricity.

| Lvl | Spell | School |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Shocking Grasp | Evocation |
| 2 | Protection from Arrows | Abjuration |
| 3 | Lightning Bolt | Evocation |
| 4 | Shout | Evocation |
| 5 | Cloudkill | Conjuration |
| 6 | Chain Lightning | Evocation |
| 7 | Elemental Body IV (Air) | Transmutation |
| 8 | Shout, Greater | Evocation |
| 9 | Elemental Swarm (Air) | Conjuration |

The Air Domain expands a cleric's spellbook with some decent spells. Lightning Arc will quickly become obsolete and while the lightning resistance is OK, it is likely to be redundant with Resist Lightning (Communal).

#### Animal Domain
At 4th level, you gain an Animal Companion. Your class level - 3 is used to determine your companion's effective level. Lore (Nature) becomes a Class Skill.

The Animal Companion is one of the best abilities - take Animal Domain with a Monster Tactician to really see the fur fly. You'll want to take the Boon Companion feat (at level 5) to make best use of it. Note that a Sacred Huntsmaster character is unable to select this domain.

#### Artifice Domain
You can maintain an Aura of Efficiency for a number of rounds equal to your class level. This grants allies within 30' +4 to saves against fatigue and exhaustion. At 8th level, this provides 1/- Damage Resistance, increasing by 1 every three additional levels to a maximum 5/- at level 20.

#### Chaos Domain
You can use Touch of Chaos (3 + Wisdom modifier) times per day. This is a melee touch attack and causes the target to roll twice for the next round, taking the less favorable result. At 8th level, you gain Chaos Blade, allowing you to imbue a weapon with the Anarchic trait for a number of rounds equal to half your class level. You can do this twice at level 12, three times at level 16 and four times at level 20.

#### Charm Domain
You can use Dazing Touch (3 + Wisdom modifier) times per day. This is a melee touch attack which causes the target to become dazed for 1 round. Creatures whose hit dice exceed your class level are unaffected. At 8th level, you gain Charming Smile, allowing you to cast Charm Person as a swift action with a DC equal to 10 + half your class level + your Wisdom modifier. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to your class level.

#### Community Domain
You can use Calming Touch (3 + Wisdom modifier) times per day. This heals 1-6 damage + 1 point per class level and removes the fatigued, shaken and sickened conditions. At 8th level, you gain the Guarded Hearth ability which grants allies in a specified area a competence bonus to their attack rolls and a resistance bonus to their saving throws equal to your Wisdom modifier. The aura is a full round action and lasts for one hour per class level. You can use this ability once per day.

You can do this twice per day at level 12, three times at level 16 and four times at level 20.

At 8th level you gain Death's Embrace which causes you to be healed by channeled negative energy or, if you are undead, to not be damaged by positive energy.

At 8th level you gain the Destructive Aura ability which empowers all creatures (allies and enemies) in a 30' radius, gaining a morale damage boost on melee attacks equal to half your class level and automatically confirming all critical hits. You can maintain the aura for a number of rounds per day equal to your class level.

#### Glory Domain
If using Channel Positive Energy to damage undead, the DC to saves is increased by 2. You can use Touch of Glory (3 + Wisdom modifier) times per day. This grants the recipient a bonus equal to your class level to a single Charisma-based skill or ability check and lasts until used (or one hour per class level). At 8th level you gain the Aura of Heroism ability which bestows the effects of the Heroism spell to all allies in a 30' radius as a swift action. You can maintain the aura for a number of rounds per day equal to your class level.

At 8th level, you gain Holy Lance, allowing you to imbue a weapon with the Holy trait for a number of rounds equal to half your class level. You can do this twice per day at level 12, three times at level 16 and four times at level 20.
