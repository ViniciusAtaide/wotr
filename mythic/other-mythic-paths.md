# Other Mythic Paths
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

### Legend
You have rejected the power offered to you and have become more powerful as a result.

#### Legend's Corruption Resistance
You can rest 5 times before Abyssal Corruption starts kicking in.

#### Legend
Your ability scores are increased by 4. Your level cap is 40 and the amount of XP needed to level up is reduced.

##### Playing a Legend
The Legend mechanics are not obvious. Before choosing Legend, you will have followed another mythic path to rank 8. While completing the Path of the Legend, you lose the following:

- All rank 3 - 8 mythic abilities and feats
- Your mythic spellbook (including a merged spellbook if you were following the Angel or Lich paths)
- Any further mythic progression - your rank is stuck at 3.

When progressing beyond level 20 (which you do immediately after completing the Legend quest), things work like this:

- You can only take 20 levels in any class (or 10 levels in a prestige class) so you must multiclass
- BAB increases past 20 up to a theoretical maximum of 40
- You gain an additional main hand attack each additional +5 BAB, up to a maximum 8 attacks at 36+ BAB
- Caster level can increase past 20 up to a theoretical maximum of 28
- Saving throws continue to increase
- You continue to acquire skill points.

You retain the abilities and feats that you gained as a Mythic Hero so you want to make these count. One possibility is to take *Enduring Spells* followed by *Greater Enduring Spells* as an extra mythic ability so that you have long-lasting buffs. Abilities that depend on mythic rank, such as *Mythic Weapon Specialization* and *Archmage Armor* are almost worthless but abilities like *Limitless Rage* or *Mythic Power Attack* are undiminished.

You cannot increase caster level past 20 with one of the regular classes - a Wizard's caster level is 20 at class level 20 and cannot go higher. However, if you take a prestige class that gives you caster progression during your first 20 levels and then start taking levels in the original casting class at level 21, you will be able to increase caster level further. For example, Fighter (2), Wizard (8), Eldritch Knight (10) gives you caster level 17 at 20th level. If you then take Wizard levels again, your caster level will increase again. Another 11 levels will take you to the caster level cap.

The main advantage of a caster level >20 comes at CL 25 if you have Greater Enduring Spells and Extend Metamagic. The combination makes spells that normally last 1 round / level (such as *Haste*) last 24 hours.

If you follow the Trickster path between ranks 3 and 8 and take feats unlocked via Perception 2 *before* becoming a Legend, you will keep them. However, if you subsequently respec, you will not be able to take them again.

Be aware that you only follow the Legend path for the final two chapters of the game. Therefore, you should ensure that you start with a good 20 level build since you will spend most of the game using it.

### Devil
You are an emissary of Hell.

Devil is a late-game mythic path that you begin at mythic rank 8. You must have followed either the Aeon or Azata paths previously. If you were following the Azata path, you lose your dragon companion.

| Rank | Abilities | Feats | Special 1 | Special 2 | Special 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | - | Mythic Feat | Hell's Dominance | Hell's Decree | Wings |
| 9 | Mythic Ability | - | Hell's Vengeance | Hell's Seal | - |
| 10 | - | Mythic Feat | Hell's Rule | Hell's Authority | - |

#### Hell's Dominance
You are able to use Dominate Monster as a spell-like ability at will. You also gain fire resistance 20 and +4 to saves vs. poison.

#### Hell's Decree
You may issue a decree a number of times per day equal to your mythic rank. If a saving throw is required, the DC is 10 + twice your mythic rank.

##### Decree of <SCHOOL>
You command an ally to only use spells of a single school. The DC of those spells is increased by your mythic rank.

##### Decree of Hell's Fires
Enemies within 50' take damage equal to 2d10 + your mythic rank (half fire, half holy). If their remaining HP is less than ten times your mythic rank, they must make a Fortitude save or die.

##### Decree of Hunt
You nominate one ally as the hunter and one enemy as the quarry. The hunter gains bonus to attack and damage equal to your mythic rank but you lose control over them.

##### Decree of Neverending War
Enemies within 50' must make a Will save or begin attacking creatures indiscriminately.

##### Decree of Vengeance
You command an ally to attack enemies indiscriminately. They gain temporary HP equal to 10x your mythic rank. Each time they kill an enemy, they gain +5 DR/good, +2 CMD and a +2 bonus to saves against movement-impairing effects. This bonus stacks up to five times.

#### Hell's Vengeance
You gain the ability to use Hellfire Ray and Power Word: Stun as spell-like abilities at will. You gain immunity to fire, acid and cold resistance 10 and your weapons count as lawful and evil for the purpose of overcoming damage resistance.

#### Hell's Seal
You gain the ability to place seals on your allies. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to your mythic rank but only one seal may be placed on all ally at any given time.

##### Seal of Burning Flames
When the ally's HP falls below 15x your mythic rank, the seal deals 1d8 damage per mythic rank to all enemies who must make a Reflex save or start burning for 2d6 damage per round.

##### Seal of Devouring Flames
When the ally's HP falls below 5x your mythic rank, they die, inflicting 2d10 damage per mythic rank to enemies within 30'.

##### Seal of Fear Eater
When the ally would become shaken or frightened, they instead gain the benefits of the Heroic Invocation and Rage spells for a number of rounds equal to your mythic rank.

##### Seal of Fire Eating
When the ally receives fire damage, they are healed 3d6 HP per mythic rank and their attacks deal an additional 2d6 unholy damage for a number of rounds equal to your mythic rank.

##### Seal of Reflective Mind
When the ally makes a saving throw against an enchantment or illusion effect, they automatically succeed and the effect is reflected back at the caster with the DC increased by half your mythic rank.

#### Hell's Rule
You are able to use Overwhelming Presence and Power Word: Kill as spell-like abilities at will.

#### Hell's Authority
Three times per day, you can use Sword of Heaven with one improvement or Demonic Rage with one aspect for 10 minutes.

##### Playing a Devil
The path requires some unlocking. If your path is Aeon, you can accept an advisor named Melies while if your path is Azata, you will acquire an advisor named Early Sunset. If you follow their "advice", you will be able to choose the Devil path in Chapter 5. As to whether this is something you want to do, you need to balance Hell's Decree against whatever you would receive if you stuck with your original path: either a superpower (Azata) or unlimited use of Enforcing Gaze (Aeon). Since the only really decent decree is the one that boosts spell DC, you might want to stick with your original path regardless. The rank 9 abilities are good but they come very late in the game.

### Swarm That Walks
You are the swarm and everything else is food.

Swarm That Walks is a late-game mythic path that you begin at mythic rank 8. You lose any powers you gained at ranks 3 - 7, retaining only your mythic abilities and feats. You also lose all companions.

| Rank | Abilities | Feats | Special 1 | Special 2 | Special 3 | Special 4 | Special 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | Swarm Clone | Mythic Feat | Devour | Host of the Great Swarm | Swarm Form | Swarm Feast | Swarm Infest |
| 9 | Mythic Ability | - | - | Eye of the Swarm | - | - | - |
| 10 | - | Mythic Feat | - | The Swarm | - | - | - |

Many of your abilities are dependent on your swarm size which starts at 0 but which increases as you devour corpses and living creatures.

#### Swarm Clone
You gain the ability to make clones of yourself. Clones have your abilities but their own inventory. You can create 1 clone for every 3 points of swarm size but no more than 1 at mythic rank 8, 2 at rank 9 and five at rank 10.

#### Devour
You devour a corpse to increase the size of your swarm. The increase is a function of the CR of the devoured corpse. To gain a size increase, you must devour a number of CR equal to 100 x the next size up (i.e. 100 to grow to size 1, another 200 to grow to size 2 and so forth; size 15 requires you to devour a total of 12,000 CR).

#### Host of the Great Swarm
You gain fast healing and additional HP depending on the size of your swarm.

#### Swarm Form
You gain DR 5/- + your swarm size and a bonus to natural armour equal to +4 plus your swarm size. This ability cancels Swarm Feast and Swarm Infest.

#### Swarm Feast
Enemies within 50' receive a number of 4d6 physical damage per round, increasing by 1d6 for each point of swarm size. If they die, they are automatically devoured. Any ranged attacks against you have a 50% miss chance. This ability cancels Swarm Form and Swarm Infest.

#### Swarm Infest
The targeted enemy is blinded and receives 8d8 physical damage per round, increasing by 2d8 for each point of swarm size. The creature must make a Will save (DC = 15 + twice your mythic rank) or become panicked. Any creature killed by this effect is automatically devoured. The next time you use Swarm Form to cancel the ability, you will heal the damage dealt by the Infest ability. Using Swarm Infest cancels Swarm Form and Swarm Feast.

#### Eye of the Swarm
At 9th rank, you gain the damage reduction of a swarm and are immune to attacks of opportunity. Your Strength and Dexterity ability scores increase by 4 + your swarm size. You are immune to poison, disease, paralysis and stun.

While Swarm Form is active, your attacks deal an additional 4 + swarm size damage. Any creature which attacks you in melee combat receives 4d6 damage + 1d6 for each point of swarm size.

#### The Swarm
At 10th rank, your Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma ability scores increase by 4 + your swarm size. You gain spell resistance equal to 25 + your swarm size. You also gain immunity to most harmful conditions, including fatigue and exhaustion.

When taking the Camouflage role while resting on the world map, you gain a +10 profane bonus to Stealth. Alternatively, you gain a +10 profane bonus to Perception when taking the Night Watch role.

##### Playing a Swarm
In many ways, the Swarm That Walks path simply adds artificial difficulty: you are basically soloing the game (initially, at least) without an appreciable boost in power. In fact, since you lose all your previous mythic abilities, your power is almost certainly greatly reduced. Since you also have to do a number of unspeakable acts to unlock the path in the first place, you would only choose this for RP reasons.
