# Angel
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

An Angel is an emissary of the celestial powers. His mythic progression is as follows:

| Rank | Abilities | Feats | Special 1 | Special 2 | Summons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mythic Ability | - | Close to the Heavens | Bypass Epic DR | - |
| 2 | - | Mythic Feat | Heaven's Breath | Hard to Kill | Spirit Paladin |
| 3 | Mythic Ability | - | Sword of Heaven | Angelic Halo | Spirit Paladins |
| 4 | - | Mythic Feat | Improved Sword of Heaven | - | Movanic Deva |
| 5 | Mythic Ability | - | - | Improved Angelic Halo | Movanic Devas |
| 6 | - | Mythic Feat | Improved Sword of Heaven | - | Astral Deva |
| 7 | Mythic Ability | - | Halo's Holy Aura | Improved Angelic Halo | Astral Devas |
| 8 | - | Mythic Feat | Greater Sword of Heaven | Wings | Astral Devas x4 |
| 9 | Mythic Ability | - | - | Improved Angelic Halo | Spirit Guardian |
| 10 | - | Mythic Feat | Greater Sword of Heaven | Sword of Holy Healing | Spirit Guardians |

#### Close to the Heavens
The character can launch a ray of heavenly light. When targeting an ally, the ray heals 4d6 damage + 2d6 per mythic rank. When used against an enemy, it is a ranged touch attack which does the same amount of damage. This ability can be used three times per day + 1 for each additional mythic rank.

#### Heaven's Breath
At 2nd mythic rank, the character gains acid and cold resistance 20 and immunity to petrification.

#### Summon Spirit Paladin
At 2nd rank, you can summon a Spirit Paladin for 1 minute.

#### Sword of Heaven
At 3rd rank, you can imbue your weapons a number of times per day equal to 1 + half your mythic rank. Imbued weapons deal an additional 2d6 holy damage on attacks. Spells and abilities used while holding imbued weapons deal an additional 2 dice of damage or heal an additional 2 dice of damage.

#### Angelic Halo
Allies within 50' gain a bonus equal to 1 + mythic rank / 2 to AC and saving throws against attacks made by evil creatures.

#### Summon Spirit Paladins
At 3rd rank, you can summon a pair of Spirit Paladins for 1 minute.

#### Improved Sword of Heaven
At 4th and 6th ranks you can empower your Sword of Heaven ability:

##### Abolish Disease
You are immune to disease while wielding the Sword of Heaven. Healing spells remove all diseases from the target along with the fatigued, exhausted, sickened and nauseated conditions. Creatures which apply a disease effect with their attacks take an additional 50% damage from the wielder as holy damage.

##### Abolish Guile
You are immune to compulsion while wielding the Sword of Heaven. Healing spells remove all mind affecting effects from the target. Creatures which apply a compulsion effect with their attacks take an additional 50% damage from the wielder as holy damage.

##### Abolish Poison
You are immune to poison while wielding the Sword of Heaven. Healing spells remove all poisons from the target along with ability damage and ability drain. Creatures which apply poison with their attacks take an additional 50% damage from the wielder as holy damage.

##### Abolish Undeath
You are immune to death effects and energy drain while wielding the Sword of Heaven. Healing spells remove paralysis and all negative levels from the target. Undead creatures take an additional 50% damage from the wielder as holy damage.

##### Bestow the Divine
You can imbue the weapons of an ally with holy power.

##### Everlasting Flame
Sword of Heaven now lasts for 1 minute per mythic rank.

Note that this ability does not work with Greater Enduring Spells.

##### Grand Blessing
Additional damage and healing from the Sword of Heaven are maximized.

##### Guide the Faithful
Attacks with the imbued weapon mark the target for 1 minute, making it visible and giving it a -40 penalty to Stealth and a -4 penalty to AC.

#### Summon Movanic Deva
At 4th rank, you can summon a Movanic Deva for 1 minute.

#### Improved Angelic Halo
At 5th, 7th and 9th ranks, you can empower the Angelic Halo ability with one of the following effects:

##### Blinding Light
Any enemy attacking you for the first time must make a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + half character level + mythic rank + highest ability modifier) or be blinded for 1d4+1 rounds. Creatures making a save are dazzled for 1 minute.

##### Burning Bright
All demons and undead in the area suffer 2d6 + mythic rank holy damage per round.

##### Flame of Life
While Angelic Halo is active, an ally receives fast healing equal to your mythic rank for 1 round upon taking damage.

##### Piercing Rays
All enemies in the halo's area suffer a penalty to spell resistance equal 1 + half your mythic rank. In addition, they suffer a penalty to saving throws equal to half the penalty to spell resistance.

##### Solar Winds
All allies gain energy resistance as follows:

| Energy Type | Resistance |
| --- | --- |
| Acid, cold, electricity, fire | 5 / rank |
| Sonic, negative energy | 3 / rank |
| Divine, force, holy, unholy | 1 / rank |

##### Unfailing Beacon
You are not damaged by allies' AoE spells. If you are the main target of such as a spell, it becomes empowered and the DC is increased by 2.

#### Summon Movanic Devas
At 5th rank, you can summon a pair of Movanic Devas for 1 minute.

#### Summon Astral Deva
At 6th rank, you can summon an Astral Deva for 1 minute.

#### Halo's Holy Aura
At 7th rank, Your Angelic Halo creates the effect of the Holy Aura spell. Spell resistance is 20 + your mythic rank.

#### Summon Astral Devas
At 7th rank, you can summon a pair of Astral Devas for 1 minute. At 8th rank, you can summon four Astral Devas.

#### Greater Sword of Heaven
At 8th and 10th ranks, you can add more powerful improvements to Sword of Heaven alongside the earlier ones:

##### Heavenly Host
Prerequisites:
Bestow the Divine

All allies within 50' receive the benefits of Sword of Heaven.

##### Overwhelming Flames
Attacks from the Sword of Heaven deal weapon damage to all enemies within 5'.

##### Speed of Light
While wielding the Sword of Heaven you are under the effects of Haste, granting 2 additional attacks and making spells of 7th level or lower quickened.

#### Summon Spirit Guardian
At 9th rank, you can summon a Spirit Guardian.

#### Sword of Holy Healing
At 10th rank, when you activate Sword of Heaven you are healed 10 HP and any ability damage and the following effects are removed: blind, confused, dazed, dazzled, diseased, fatigued, exhausted, sickened, nauseated, poisoned and stunned. It also dispels the effects of the *Feeblemind* spell.

#### Summon Spirit Guardians
At 10th rank, you can summon three Spirit Guardians.

##### Playing an Angel
Alongside Legend, Angel is the most powerful of the paths and has the advantage of being playable for most of the game. Assuming your entry point is a full divine casting class, you'll unlock 6th level spells right away. *Ward Against Harm, Communal* combined with the Greater Enduring Spells ability basically makes your whole party immune to everything annoying permanently. No other mythic path gives you anything like this. By 4th rank, you should be able to cast 8th level spells. You can, of course, follow the Angel path from any class but then the power curve will be rather more gradual.
