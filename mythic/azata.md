# Azata
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

An Azata is an avatar of freedom and goodness. She gains a spellbook with unique spells up to 7th level. Besides the unique spells which she learns automatically at the appropriate rank, she learns a number of spells of various levels as shown in the table.

#### Azata Performance
From 3rd rank, you unlock songs. You can use performance for a number of rounds per day equal to your mythic rank. If your main class is a Bard, this is added to the number of rounds per day that you can use Bardic Performance and you can use Azata performances in place of your normal Bard songs.

This does not apply if your main class is a Skald (or Sensei, Martyr, etc).

#### Instrument of Freedom
You can launch a bolt of energy. When targeting an ally, the bolt empowers their weapons for a number of rounds equal to 2 + your mythic rank. Empowered weapons inflict an additional 2d6 holy damage and count as good for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction. When used against an enemy, it is a ranged touch attack which does 4d6 holy damage + 2d6 per mythic rank. This ability can be used three times per day + 1 for each additional mythic rank.

#### Freedom's Child
At 2nd mythic rank, you gain resist electricity 20 and a +4 bonus to saves against mind-affecting effects.

#### Summon Azata Bralani
At 2nd rank, you can summon an Azata Bralani for 1 minute.

#### Dragon Companion
At 3rd rank, you gain a Dragon Companion which grows as you gain mythic ranks.

#### Song of Heroic Resolve
At 3rd rank, your performance grants allies within 50' a luck bonus to saving throws equal to half your mythic rank. When an ally makes a saving throw, they are healed for a number of HP equal to 2d6 + your mythic rank.

#### Summon Mastodon
At 3rd rank, you can summon a Mastodon for 1 minute.

#### Superpowers
From 4th rank, an Azata can choose from a number of abilities:

##### All-skilled
You gain a bonus to all skill checks equal to your mythic rank. Whenever you make a skill check, roll twice and take the better result. You also become proficient with all types of weapons and armour.

##### Favorable Magic
When an enemy attempts a save against your spells, it must roll twice and take the worse result. When you make a concentration check or roll to overcome spell resistance, you can roll twice and take the better result. If a spell description says that a successful save reduces damage by 50%, it is only reduced by 25%.

##### Incredible Might
You gain a morale bonus equal to your mythic rank on attack and damage rolls. When you hit with a charge attack or confirm a critical hit, the target must make a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + mythic rank + Strength modifier) or suffer one of the following effects:

- Stunned for 1d4 rounds (30%)
- Prone for 1d4 rounds (20%)
- Pushed away 15' and stunned for 1d4 rounds (20%)
- Unable to move and receive -3 penalties to attack rolls and AC until the end of combat (15%)
- Exhausted (10%)
- Killed (5%)

You can instead to choose to bolster your companions' attacks, granting them a +1 morale bonus on attack and damage rolls for every two mythic ranks.

##### Life-Bonding Friendship
You choose a teamwork feat this rank and the next rank. You share teamwork feats with allies within 50'. Allies below 0 HP continue to fight. When combat ends or after receiving a number of attacks equal to your Charisma modifier, the allies in this state fall dead.

##### Marvelous Endurance
You gain fast healing equal to half your mythic rank + 5 per mythic rank after the fourth. Upon receiving a critical hit, you get 1d6 temporary HP per mythic rank and if you have less than 25% HP, you gain DR 10/-.

##### Supersonic Speed
You are always under the effects of Haste in combat. Weapon attacks have a 20% miss chance and spells have a 10% miss chance. Damage from AoE attacks is halved (including when already reduced by a successful saving throw).

##### Zippy Magic
When casting a spell that targets an individual creature, it affects another creature (either an enemy or ally depending on the spell cast) within 30'. Targeted enemies receive an additional 2d6 + mythic rank damage.

This ability only works with spells, not spell-like abilities. In particular, it does nothing for Kinetic Blasts.

#### Summon Movanic Deva
At 4th rank, you can summon a Movanic Deva for 1 minute.

#### Song of Broken Chains
At 5th rank, your performance grants allies within 50' freedom of movement and immunity to compulsion spells and spell-like abilities.

#### Summon Azata Ghael
At 5th rank, you can summon an Azata Ghael for 1 minute.

#### Song of Defiance
At 6th rank, your performance grants all allies within 50' DR 5/lawful and Regeneration 5/lawful.

#### Summon Astral Deva
At 6th rank, you can summon an Astral Deva for 1 minute.

#### Song of Courageous Defender
At 7th rank, your performance grants a companion DR 5/- and immunity to fear. All enemies within 50' must pass a Will save (DC = 10 + half character level + mythic rank + highest ability modifier) or be forced to attack the chosen companion.

#### Summon Astral Devas
At 7th rank, you can summon a pair of Astral Devas for 1 minute.

#### Summon Azata Ghaels and Astral Devas
At 8th rank, you can summon 1d3+1 Azata Ghaels and a pair of Astral Devas for 1 minute.

#### Summon Hamadryads
At 9th rank, you can summon 1d3+1 Hamadryads for 1 minute.

##### Playing an Azata
An Azata hits the ground running. At 3rd rank, you get a unique animal (or rather dragon) companion and can make your party immune to mind-affecting spells and abilities. At 4th rank, you gain a superpower. Favorable Magic on a save-or-else caster is massive. Life-Bonding Friendship is very powerful on a class that receives free teamwork feats. After this, Azata plateaus somewhat. The superpower you can select at 10th rank is one you could have selected at 4th rank. In fact, it's likely to be worse than the one you selected at 4th rank since not all superpowers are equal. Incredible Might, for example, is more or less the same as Greater Heroism (or Heroic Invocation for the party version) since the bonuses are morale bonuses rather than untyped. Supersonic Speed is basically *Blur* and *Haste* which are low-level spells. Zippy Magic pairs up nicely with Hellfire Ray, particularly if you're planning on choosing the Devil path later.

The Azata spellbook has some excellent support spells, *Second Breath* and *Joy of Life* in particular. The spellbook is Enchantment-heavy which gives you an idea as to where you might place any Spell Focus feats.

The class which particularly synergises with Azata is a Bard because you can use your Bard performance rounds (and Lingering Song and so forth) with the Azata songs. If you find the Lich path distasteful, Favorable Magic favours any offensive caster, particularly an Enchantment or Illusion specialist. Weird and Phantasmal Killer are much more effective when you get two chances of making them stick.

### Aivu
Aivu is a baby havoc dragon who joins you at mythic rank 3. She has a number of immunities: sonic energy, Confusion, Sickened, Nauseated, Sleep and Paralysis. In addition, she cannot be tripped. She has a 30' cone breath attack that deals 2d10 sonic damage for every Azata rank.

When she joins you, she is level 10 and has the following attributes: STR 21, DEX 16, CON 17, INT: 16, WIS: 15 and CHA 20. Her size is Small and she has 12 Natural Armor. She doesn't level up alongside you. Instead, she grows with your mythic rank:

| Rank | CHA | Lvl |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 20 | 10 |
| 4 | 21 | 15 |
| 5 | 23 | 20 |
| 6 | 24 | 25 |
| 7 | 25 | 28 |
| 8 | 30 |  |
| 9 | 26 | 32 |
| 10 | 35 |  |

Charisma is the only attribute that gets an increase. Unlike animal companions, her natural armour doesn't increase either.

She already has number of feats when you acquire her: Weapon Focus and Improved Critical with both Claw and Bite attacks, Power Attack, Lightning Reflexes, Improved Initiative and Armor Proficiency (Light Barding). She gains additional abilities at various ranks:

- Armor Proficiency (Medium Barding) and Frightful Presence at mythic rank 5. Her size grows to Medium.
- Improved Lightning Reflexes, Metamagic (Quicken Spell), Critical Focus, Tiring Critical, Armor Proficiency (Heavy Barding), Hammer the Gap and Blind Fight at Mythic Rank 6 along with an Heroic Aura that confers the effects of Heroism. She also grows to Large size.

She also has ranks in the Knowledge (Arcana), Perception, Persuasion and Use Magic Device skills equal to her level.
