# Aeon
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

An Aeon is the living embodiment of cosmic order. She gains a spellbook with unique spells up to 7th level. Besides the unique spells which she learns automatically at the appropriate rank, she learns a number of spells of various levels as shown in the table.

#### Force Reality
Enemies within 30' are subjected to a greater dispel magic affecting illusion spells. Equivalent caster level is your character level + your mythic rank.

At 2nd mythic rank, you can target all allies within 30' with a greater dispel magic affecting all negative effects.

You can use these abilities a number of times per day equal to 3 + your mythic rank.

#### Summon Wood Golem
At 2nd rank, you can summon a Wood Golem for 1 minute.

#### Enforcing Gaze
From 3rd rank, you can adjust reality to benefit allies and hinder enemies. Enforced rules last until the end of combat and enemies can attempt a Will save to resist the effects (DC = 10 + character level / 2 + mythic rank + highest ability modifier). Chaotic enemies have a -2 penalty applied to their saves. You receive one Enforcing Gaze ability at 3rd rank and another at each subsequent mythic rank. The ability can be used a number of times per day equal to 1 + your mythic rank.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Attack
Allies receive a bonus to attack rolls equal to 1 + 1 per two mythic ranks. Enemies who fail their saves suffer an equivalent penalty.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Attacks of Opportunity
Allies able to make more than one attack of opportunity per turn gain an additional attack of opportunity. Allied attacks of opportunity receive a bonus to attack and damage equal to your mythic rank. Enemies who fail their saves lose additional attacks of opportunity and any attacks of opportunity they do make have a penalty to attack and damage equal to your mythic rank.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Caster Level
Allies receive a bonus to caster level equal to 1 + 1 per 3 mythic ranks. The caster level of enemies failing their save is reduced by the same amount.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Combat Maneuvers
Allies receive a bonus to CMB equal to 1 + mythic rank / 2 when attempting combat maneuvers. The CMB of enemies failing their save is reduced by the same amount.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Damage
Allies receive a bonus to damage rolls equal to 1 + 1 per two mythic ranks. Enemies who fail their saves suffer an equivalent penalty.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Damage Reduction
Allies gain DR N/chaotic equal to twice your mythic rank. The DR of enemies who fail their saves is reduced by an equal amount.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Energy Shield
Allies gain resistance to acid, cold, electricity, fire and sonic equal five times your mythic rank. The energy resistance of enemies who fail their saves is reduced by an equal amount.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Save DC
The DC of spells cast by your allies is increased by 1 + 1 per 3 mythic ranks. The DC of spells cast by enemies who fail their save is reduced by the same amount.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Area Effects
Prerequisites:
Rank 5

Your allies become immune to non-instantaneous area effects from your and your allies' spells and abilities. Allies entering an area effect from an enemy spell or ability can dispel it by making a dispel check equal to 1d20 + half your character level + your mythic rank.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Concentration
Prerequisites:
Rank 5

Allies receive a bonus to concentration checks equal to your mythic rank. Enemies that fail their saves have to make a concentration check each time they attempt to cast a spell.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Hit Points
Prerequisites:
Rank 5

Allies receive temporary HP equal to twice your mythic rank. Enemies that fail their saves suffer 1d6 damage per 3 mythic ranks every time they land an attack on an ally.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Normalize Size
Prerequisites:
Rank 5

Allies become medium size. They lose all size penalties but retain all bonuses apart from reach. Enemies that fail their saving throws become medium size, losing all size bonuses but retaining any size penalties.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Power of Law
Prerequisites:
Rank 5

When allies make a roll and get a result less than or equal to 1 + half your mythic rank, the result is corrected to 2 + half your mythic rank. When an affected enemy makes a roll and gets a result higher than 21 - half your mythic rank, the result is lowered to 20 - half your mythic rank. The save DC for this effect is 15 + your mythic rank.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Primordial Magic
Prerequisites:
Rank 5

This has three effects:

- Spells cast by allies deal bonus damage equal to your mythic rank. The damage from spells cast by enemies that fail their saves is reduced by the same amount.
- Healing spells cast by allies restore additional damage equal to twice your mythic rank. The healing from spells cast by enemies that fail their saves is reduced by the same amount.
- You and your allies can cast a second spell per round as a move action if they only affect friendly targets. If an enemy casts a spell that affects one of its allies, the casting time becomes a full-round action.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Relativity of Space
Prerequisites:
Rank 5

All attacks against you and your allies have a miss chance equal to 5% per mythic rank. Whenever your attacks would be subject to a miss chance, the probability is reduced by 5% per mythic rank.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Summoner's Gaze
Prerequisites:
Rank 5

Allied summons receive bonuses to Strength, Dexterity and Constitution equal to half your mythic rank. Enemy summoned units which fail their saving throws are banished.

##### Enforcing Gaze - Temporal Jump
Prerequisites:
Rank 8

Once per round after an ally misses an attack, they can reroll it and keep the better result. Once per round when an enemy that failed its save hits with an attack, they must reroll and take the worse result.

#### Aeon Immunities
At 3rd rank, you become immune to poison, disease and confusion. At 5th rank, you also become immune to bleed, mind-affecting effects and ability damage. At 7th rank, you additionally become immune to curse, death effects and energy drain. Finally, at 10th rank, and vs. chaotic enemies, you automatically roll 20 on initiative rolls and saving throws. In addition, your attacks and spells ignore any damage reduction, energy resistance and immunities of chaotic creatures.

#### Summon Axiomite
At 3rd rank, you can summon an Axiomite for 1 minute.

#### Aeon Bane
At 4th rank, you can grant your weapons the Bane property for a number of rounds per day equal to twice your mythic rank. While this is active, you gain a bonus to spell penetration equal to your mythic rank and every weapon attack dispels effects from the target, equivalent to *Dispel Magic*. If you have the Inquisitor's Bane ability, the number of rounds stack and you gain the benefits of the Aeon Bane ability.

#### Summon Stone Golem
At 4th rank, you can summon a Stone Golem for 1 minute.

#### Mythic Suppression
At 5th level, as a standard action, you can suppress the mythic powers of mythic demons unless they make a Will save. Non-distorted mythic demons receive a +4 bonus to their save. At 7th rank, distorted mythic demons no longer receive a saving throw and non-distorted demons don't receive a save bonus. Finally, at mythic rank 9, mythic demons cannot save against this ability.

#### Summon Iron Golem
At 5th rank, you can summon an Iron Golem for 1 minute.

#### Enforcing Gaze - Extra Gaze
At 6th rank, an Aeon can activate two enforcing gazes simultaneously. At 10th rank, this increases to three.

#### Improved Aeon's Bane
At 6th rank, the Aeon Bane ability adds an additional +1 to attack rolls and 2d6 + 1 to damage rolls. Attacks add the effects of *Greater Dispel Magic* and any creatures you summon receive the benefits of this ability.

#### Summon Gold Golem
At 6th rank, you can summon a Gold Golem for 1 minute.

#### Aeon Companion Immunities
At 7th rank, all allies within 30' gain immunity to poison, disease and confusion. At 9th rank, they also gain immunity to mind-affecting effects.

#### Summon Gold Golems
At 7th rank, you can summon a pair of Gold Golems for 1 minute.

#### Enforcing Gaze - Perpetual
At 8th rank, you can use Enforcing Gaze any number of times per day.

#### Summon Adamantine Golem
At 8th rank, you can summon an Adamantine Golem for 1 minute.

#### Greater Aeon's Bane
At 9th rank, the Aeon Bane ability adds an additional +1 to attack rolls and 2d6 + 1 to damage rolls. Your first attack each round automatically dispels an effect from the target. Finally, your spells and abilities can be used as a move action rather than a standard action.

#### Summon Adamantine Golems
At 9th rank, you can summon a pair of Adamantine Golems for 1 minute.

##### Playing an Aeon
An Aeon does get powerful abilities but they come on very late. By rank 4, you have a couple of gazes that you can use five times a day and a few rounds of Bane. This is poor in comparison to an Azata who will have a superpower by this point let alone an Angel who can make the entire party immune to everything.

Your power ramps up from rank 5 when you unlock better gazes and then the ability to combine them. Eventually, you can use them an unlimited number of times per day.

The spell book is based on the Inquisitor's so not the best. It starts getting good when you unlock *Zero State* at rank 5. However, you'll want to use this with Selective Metamagic.

If you take the Greater Enduring Spells ability, you can use the Caster Level gaze to help you reach CL 25. *Perfect Form* for 24 hours is pretty sweet.

The obvious class to go with the Aeon mythic path is some sort of Inquisitor because you can get to use the Aeon's Bane ability for up to 30 rounds per day which is a lot better than 10 rounds. Any summoned creatures get the benefits of Aeon Bane which makes Monster Tactician a good choice.
