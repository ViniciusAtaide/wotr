# Lich
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The Lich is an undead lord of immeasurable power. His mythic progression is as follows:

| Rank | Abilities | Feats | Special 1 | Special 2 | Summons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mythic Ability | - | Danse Macabre | Bypass Epic DR | - |
| 2 | - | Mythic Feat | Negative Energy Insight | Hard to Kill | Spectre |
| 3 | Mythic Ability | - | Lich Powers | Skeletal Champion | Fallen |
| 4 | - | Mythic Feat | Lord Beyond the Grave | Skeleton Upgrade | Plague Beast Mastodon |
| 5 | Mythic Ability | - | Dark Rites | - | Plague Beast Mastodons |
| 6 | - | Mythic Feat | Lich Powers | - | Ecorche |
| 7 | Mythic Ability | - | - | - | Nightshade Nightcrawler |
| 8 | - | Mythic Feat | - | Skeleton Upgrade | Nightshade Nightcrawler and Ecorche |
| 9 | Mythic Ability | - | Lich Powers | - | Ravener Dragon |
| 10 | - | Mythic Feat | - | Skeleton Upgrade | - |

#### Danse Macabre
The character can channel negative energy, inflicting 2d6 damage + 1d6 per mythic rank to living creatures within 30'. Affected creatures can attempt a Will save for half damage (DC = 10 + mythic rank + highest mental attribute modifier). Undead creatures in the area of effect are healed.

#### Negative Energy Insight
At 2nd mythic rank, the character gains a +2 bonus to saving throws vs. death effects. They also gain a +2 bonus to AC, saving throws, attack rolls and damage rolls against creatures with negative energy affinity. The DCs of class abilities against such creatures increases by +2.

#### Summon Spectre
At 2nd rank, you can summon a Spectre for 1 minute.

#### Lich Powers
At 3rd, 6th and 9th levels, you can select from the following powers:

##### Deadly Magic
For three rounds per day, any spells you cast ignore spell resistance. Creatures affected by your spells lose the ability to cast spells for 1d3 + 1 rounds.

##### Death of Elements
You can change any elemental damage into negative energy. For 1 round per 2 mythic ranks, you can convert any incoming elemental damage into negative energy.

##### Death Rush
When charging or using a combat manoeuvre, a successful attack inflicts 1d6 points of damage per mythic level.

##### Decaying Touch
Your touch attacks, unarmed attacks and natural attacks deal an additional 1d6 + 1 / mythic rank unholy damage. The affected creature suffers 1 point of Strength damage per hit. Plant creatures take an additional 2d6 + 1 / mythic rank unholy damage.

##### Eclipse Chill
For three rounds per day, you can imbue your spells with Eclipse Chill. Enemies affected by an imbued spell must make a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + half character level + mythic rank + highest ability modifier) or become blinded and suffer 2d8 + mythic rank cold damage, becoming vulnerable to cold and negative energy damage until the end of combat.

##### Fear Control
You gain a +4 profane bonus to attack and a +1 profane bonus per mythic rank to damage against frightened creatures or creatures under any mind-affecting effects. Enemies within 10' must make a Will save (DC = 10 + half character level + mythic rank + highest ability modifier) or become shaken until the end of combat.

##### Indestructible Bones
You gain DR 10/-. When an enemy lands a hit on you, it receives a non-stacking -2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of combat.

##### Magic Devourer
You gain spell resistance equal to 25 + your mythic rank. When an enemy fails a check to overcome your spell resistance, the next spell or kinetic blast that you cast will be quickened.

##### Negative Energy Mastery
All your kinetic blasts and alchemist bombs inflict an additional 1d6 + 1 per mythic rank negative energy damage and inflict a negative level on the target.

##### Tainted Sneak Attack
When you land a successful sneak attack, the target must make a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + mythic rank + Intelligence modifier) or become vulnerable to all weapon and elemental damage as well as suffering -2 penalties to attack and weapon damage rolls until the end of combat.

##### Undead Mount
Your animal companion gains undead traits, DR 10/good and spell resistance 15.

##### Weapon of Death
You can enchant your weapons with the Living Bane property. If a creature is killed with a weapon of death, all creatures within 15' suffer a penalty to caster level checks to overcome spell resistance equal to your mythic rank until the end of combat.

##### Withering Life
When a living creature takes damage from one of your spells, it also takes ability damage, either 1 point of Strength, Dexterity and Constitution damage or 1 point of Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma damage.

#### Skeletal Champion
At 3rd level, you gain a Skeletal Champion as a companion:

##### Skeletal Marksman
The skeletal champion specialises in archery.

##### Skeletal Assassin
The skeletal champion attacks with two weapons.

##### Skeletal Vanguard
The skeletal champion is defence-focused.

##### Skeletal Executioner
The skeletal champion attacks with two-handed weapons.

#### Summon Fallen
At 3rd rank, you can summon a Fallen for 1 minute.

#### Lord Beyond the Grave
At 4th rank, you can use *Command Undead* at will. All undead under your command receive a bonus to Strength, Dexterity and Charisma equal to half your mythic rank + 1. Their attacks ignore damage reduction except N/-.

#### Skeleton Upgrade
At 4th, 8th and 10th ranks, you can choose to upgrade your skeletal champion:

##### Skeletal Barbarian I
The skeletal champion gains Rage, Fast Movement and 2 Rage Powers.

##### Skeletal Bard I
The skeletal champion gets Dirge of Doom, Lingering Performance and number of level 1-4 spells.

##### Skeletal Cleric I
The skeletal champion can channel negative energy, gets the Death and Destruction domains and knows all Cleric spells.

##### Skeletal Fighter I
The skeletal champion gains a bonus combat feat, armour training, weapon training and its BAB is equal to its full HD.

##### Skeletal Inquisitor I
The skeletal champion gets Judgment twice per day, Bane and a teamwork feat.

##### Skeletal Magus I
The skeletal champion gets an Arcane Pool, Spell Combat and Magus spells up to third level.

#### Summon Plague Beast Mastodon
At 4th rank, you can summon a Plague Beast Mastodon for 1 minute.

#### Dark Rites
At 5th rank, you and your allies within 30' gain a +2 bonus to effective caster level.

#### Summon Plague Beast Mastodons
At 5th rank, you can summon a pair of Plague Beast Mastodons for 1 minute.

#### Summon Ecorche
At 6th rank, you can summon an Ecorche for 1 minute.

#### Summon Nightshade Nightcrawler
At 7th rank, you can summon a Nightshade Nightcrawler for 1 minute.

#### Summon Nightshade Nightcrawler and Ecorche
At 8th rank, you can summon a Nightshade Nightcrawler and an Ecorche for 1 minute.

#### Summon Ravener Dragon
At 9th rank, you can summon a Ravener Dragon for 1 minute.

##### Playing a Lich
The obvious way into the Lich mythic path is playing a Charisma-based Sorcerer. This is because you get the option to merge spellbooks, giving you huge power at ranks 3 and 4. Even better for a Sorcerer, rather than a Wizard or Arcanist, say, is that Charisma takes over the role of Constitution so it's win-win. Lich abilities have something for most classes (even a bomb throwing Alchemist) but especially for martial classes (Indestructible Bones and Weapon of Death). This suggests that the perfect route to Lich is an Eldritch Knight.

You gain a minion from the off and can add other unique party members as you progress. Taking the Bard upgrade for your skeletal champion synergises nicely with the Fear Control ability.

The spellbook - unsurprisingly - consists mostly of Necromancy spells. *Repurpose* is great for turning enemies into thralls.
