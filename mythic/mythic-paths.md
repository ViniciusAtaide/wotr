# Mythic Paths
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

You are a Mythic Hero set apart from your contemporaries, capable of amazing feats of courage in the face of overwhelming odds. Your mythic powers grow alongside your normal powers,

#### Mythic Rank
You gain a mythic rank at certain fixed points in the game.

| Rank | Event | Lvl |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Deal with the Wardstone in the Gray Garrison | 6 |
| 2 | Defeat Nulkineth in the Lost Chapel | 8 |
| 3 | Raise the Sword of Valor over Drezen | 9 |
| 4 | Obtain the *Lexicon of Paradox* in Areelu's Lab | 13 |
| 5 | Step through the portal to The Nexus | 15 |
| 6 | Meet Areelu in the House of Silken Shadows | 17 |
| 7 | Defeat Baphomet | 18 |
| 8 | Decide your Mythic path after returning to Drezen | 18 |
| 9 | Complete your Mythic transformation | 20 |
| 10 | Meet Areelu in her demiplane in Threshold | 20 |

The ninth rank comes at different points for different paths and requires you to complete the Chapter 5 mythic quest.

#### First Ascension
When the PC gains their first mythic rank, they must select an ability that is linked to one of the Mythic Paths (but which does not force them down any particular route) alongside a single mythic ability. At 2nd rank, you will gain a second ability related to your earlier choice, your first mythic feat and two fixed abilities:

##### Bypass Epic Damage Reduction
At 1st mythic rank, you can bypass epic damage reduction.

##### Hard to Kill
At 2nd mythic rank, you won't die until your negative HP is equal to twice your Constitution.

#### Inevitable Fate
If you have completed the Inevitable Excess expansion, you will unlock the **Inevitable Fate** ability at the end of Chapter 1, when you acquire your first mythic rank. This can be used 1/day and applies the following effects for 10 minutes:

- +1 mythic bonus to attack rolls per 3 mythic ranks. Attacks and offensive abilities deal +10% divine damage.
- 10% fortification per 2 mythic ranks, DR 1/- per 2 mythic ranks, 2 elemental (acid, cold, electricity, fire and sonic) resistance per 2 mythic ranks.
- +1 DC and +1 to rolls to overcome spell resistance per 2 mythic ranks.
- Healing spells heal +2 damage per mythic rank and grant Fast Healing equal to mythic rank for 5 rounds.

#### Mythic Path
When you reach 3rd mythic rank, you will have to commit to a particular path: Aeon, Angel, Azata, Demon, Lich or Trickster. Note that only Angel and Demon are automatically available to you - the other paths have to be unlocked.

At 8th rank, you get a chance to rethink your commitments. You can always choose to relinquish your mythic powers and become a Legend. Depending on your choices through the game, you can also choose Devil, Gold Dragon or Swarm That Walks.

#### Mythic Spellbooks
At 3rd rank, each of the six main mythic paths (Aeon, Angel, Azata, Demon, Lich and Trickster) gain spellbooks with unique spells.

| Path | Max Level | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aeon | 7 | S |
| Angel | 10 | P |
| Azata | 7 | S |
| Demon | 5 | S |
| Gold Dragon | 10 | S |
| Lich | 10 | P |
| Trickster | 7 | S |

'S' in the Type column means that spells are cast spontaneously while 'P' means that spells must be prepared. By default, there is no associated casting attribute - i.e. a Fighter doesn't need high Charisma to cast spells if he follows the Trickster path. There is, however, a user interface bug which says that you cannot cast 5th level spells, for example, if you only have 14 Charisma. Your effective caster level is twice your mythic rank and spell DC when a save is required is 10 + twice your mythic rank.

When the casting type is spontaneous, you get a bunch of non-mythic spells at each spell level that you learn as you progress. At each rank that a new spell level opens up, you always learn all unique mythic spells.

If your main class is a casting class, you keep your existing spellbook and it is separate from your mythic spellbook. For example, if your PC is an Eldritch Scion following the Azata path, he has his Magus spellbook and spells cast from that use Charisma as a casting attribute and modifier to spell DC. He *also* has an Azata spellbook and casting spells from that does not require Charisma and only mythic rank affects spell DC. The maximum caster level for both of these is 20.

Three of the late-game paths don't have mythic spellbooks. If you choose Gold Dragon or Swarm That Walks, you lose access to your previous mythic spellbook (although a Gold Dragon gains his own spellbook). If you choose Devil, you retain your existing spellbook but don't advance further. Finally, if you choose Legend, you lose most of your mythic abilities altogether, including your spellbook.

##### Merged Spellbooks
When you choose the Angel or Lich paths you can choose to merge spellbooks so that you don't have a separate Angel / Lich spellbook at all. Instead, you have, for example, a *combined* Cleric / Angel spellbook or Wizard / Lich spellbook. The rules and mechanics of merging spellbooks are as follows:

- Classes able to cast 9th level divine spells (Cleric, Druid, Oracle, Shaman) can merge spellbooks with Angel
- Classes able to cast 9th level arcane spells (Arcancist, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard) can merge spellbooks with Lich
- Prestige classes which don't block casting progression (Arcane Trickster, Eldritch Knight, etc.) do not prevent spellbook merging
- Your caster level when using your combined spellbook is your caster level from your main class + your mythic rank
- The casting attribute for your combined spellbook is the casting attribute of your main class so that Sorcerer -> Lich would use Charisma while Sage Sorcerer -> Lich would use Intelligence.

The combined caster level means that you gain higher level spells much earlier. For example, you will reach mythic rank 3 at around character level 9. This would allow a Cleric / Angel to memorise 6th level spells while she would unlock 7th level spells when she reaches character level 10 early in Chapter 3. Later in the chapter, she will unlock 9th level spells when she reaches level 13 / mythic rank 4. Finally at character level 20 / mythic rank 8, she would be able to cast the Angel's 10th level spells. A maximum caster level of 30 is great for boosting spell penetration, dispel magic and the damage output from uncapped spells. Also, having a casting attribute allows you to increase spell DC further with gear.

There is an additional benefit to CL 25. If you have Greater Enduring Spells and a source of Extend Metamagic, the combination can make 1 round / level spells (such as *Greater Invisibility*) last 24 hours.

### Mythic Abilities
Mythic abilities are gained at odd-numbered mythic ranks.

#### Melee Abilities
##### Always a Chance
You don't automatically miss when rolling 1 on an attack roll.

##### Brutality Incarnate
Your natural attacks ignore all damage resistance other than DR/-.

##### Ever Ready
Whenever you make an attack of opportunity, you gain a bonus on the attack and damage rolls equal to half your mythic rank. The number of attacks of opportunity you can make in a given round increases by 2.

##### Leading Strike
Every time you hit a target in melee combat, you leave a mark. An ally hitting the marked target deals an additional 1d6 Divine damage for each of your mythic ranks. This consumes the mark.

##### Mythic Charge
Your charge attacks deal an additional 1d6 Divine damage per mythic rank.

##### Thundering Blows
Once per round when your melee attack misses you deal sonic damage to enemies within 10' equal to 2d6 plus 1d6 per two mythic ranks.

##### Unrelenting Assault
Every turn, so long as you make a melee attack, you gain a stacking +2 bonus to damage rolls with melee weapons (max +10) which lasts until the end of combat.

#### Ranged Abilities
##### The Bigger They Are
You gain a bonus to attack rolls with ranged weapons equal to the target's AC penalty due to size.

##### Cleaving Shot
When dealing critical damage with a ranged weapon, or reducing a target to 0 HP, you deal your weapon damage to all other enemies within 10'.

##### Distracting Shots
When you hit an enemy with a ranged weapon attack, they receive an AC penalty against melee attacks equal to half your mythic rank for 1 round.

##### Expose Vulnerability
Every third hit with a ranged weapon against the same enemy deals an additional 1d6 per two mythic ranks divine damage.

##### Ranging Shots
Every time you miss with a ranged weapon attack, you gain a stacking +1 AB bonus up to your mythic rank. Every successful hit reduces this bonus by 1.

#### Magic Abilities
##### Abundant Casting
You can cast four more spells per day of 1st, 2nd and 3rd levels.

##### Improved Abundant Casting
Prerequisites:
Abundant Casting

You can cast four more spells per day of 4th, 5th and 6th levels.

##### Greater Abundant Casting
Prerequisites:
Improved Abundant Casting

You can cast four more spells per day of 7th, 8th and 9th levels.

##### Archmage Armor
When you cast *Mage Armor* on yourself, you receive an additional armour bonus equal to your mythic rank.

This ability does not trigger from items and does not work if another party member casts *Mage Armor* on you.

##### Ascendant Element
The chosen element (acid, cold, electricity, fire, sonic or negative energy) is no longer affected by an enemy's resistance or immunity.

##### Ascendant Summons
Summoned creatures receive bonuses to Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom and Charisma equal to half your mythic rank +1. Attacks from summoned creatures ignore all forms of DR other than universal.

##### Best Jokes
When an enemy fails its save against *Hideous Laughter*, the effect spreads to a random enemy within 30'. This continues as long as enemies keep failing their saving throws.

This ability appears to be bugged (or at least less effective than you might reasonably expect). If you use Heighten Metamagic to increase the effective spell level, only the first target has to make the higher DC save. The DC for other targets is the same as if you cast a 2nd-level spell.

##### Elemental Barrage
Every time a spell deals energy damage to a creature, it is marked. If you cast a spell that deals another type of energy damage during the next 3 rounds, the target will take an additional 1d6 divine damage per mythic rank.

This ability only works with spells, not with, for example, elemental properties applied to a melee weapon.

##### Enduring Spells
Spells which last longer than 1 hour but less than 24 hours now last 24 hours.

##### Greater Enduring Spells
Spells which last longer than 5 minutes but less than 24 hours now last 24 hours.

##### Favorite Metamagic
The spell level cost for the selected metamagic type decreases by 1.

##### Magic Nullification
Activating this ability grants you magic immunity and dispels any spell effects on you.

This is a modal ability and can be used an unlimited number of times. Note that it affects friendly magic.

##### Powerful Shadows
Your Shadow spells are 20% more real.

#### Healing Abilities
##### Boundless Healing
Healing spells are treated as if boosted by Reach metamagic, so that touch range spells, for example, become close range. In addition, the maximum effective caster level for any spell is lifted.

##### Emergency Help
Three times per day, as a move action, you can teleport to an ally with <30 HP. Until the end of the next round, the first healing spell you cast is empowered and maximized.

#### Miscellaneous Abilities
##### Battle Meditation
As a standard action, you grant allies within 30' a +1 morale bonus to attack and damage rolls and saves against fear effects. This bonus increases by +1 per three mythic ranks. While meditating, you cannot move and lose your Dexterity bonus to AC.

##### Corruptor
Your poisons and diseases ignore immunities.

##### Dimensional Retribution
When targeted by a spell, you gain the ability to teleport to the caster as a move action for the next two rounds.

##### Enforced Vigor
When you damage an ally, they gain a +2 mythic bonus to attack and damage rolls and a +1 mythic bonus to saving throws. The bonuses last for a number of rounds equal to half your mythic rank.

##### Inspirational Leader
Allies within 50' gain a bonus equal to half your mythic rank + 1 to initiative, saving throws and concentration checks.

##### Last Stand
Once per day, you become unkillable. For two rounds, you become immune to damage that would knock you out.

##### Master Shapeshifter
Whenever you are the target of a polymorph effect, all your physical attributes are increased by +4. If you have the Wild Shape ability, you can use it any number of times per day.

##### Rupture Restraints
Three times per day, you automatically dispel any effects that make you confused, dazed, shaken, frightened, paralysed, sickened, nauseated, staggered or stunned.

##### Unstoppable
Any time you would become paralysed, you become staggered. You become sickened rather than nauseated and shaken rather than frightened.

##### Mythic Inspiration
All bonuses granted by your inspirational abilities are increased by 1 + 1 for every 3 mythic ranks above 1st. Inspirational abilities are: Inspire Courage, Inspire Competence, Archaeologist's Luck, Inspire Ferocity, Inspire Tranquility, Inspired Rage and Insightful Contemplation.

This is very good on a Court Poet because each rank increases Intelligence and Charisma by +2, not +1.

##### Mythical Beast
Prerequisites:
Animal Companion

Your companion gains a bonus to Strength, Dexterity and Constitution equal to half your mythic rank + 1. Its attacks count as ghost touch weapons and ignore all damage reduction other than universal.

##### Over-Infused Blasts
You can simultaneously apply two substance infusions to your kinetic blasts.

##### Perfect Cavalry
Moving while mounted does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

##### School Tolerance
You no longer suffer penalties from opposing magical schools.

##### Second Bloodline
Prerequisites:
Bloodline

You cannot use this ability to select multiple Draconic bloodlines.

##### Second Bloodrager Bloodline
Prerequisites:
Bloodrager Bloodline

You can select a second bloodline, gaining all its benefits.

A Primalist can swap second bloodline abilities for Rage Powers. Other Bloodrager archetypes are erroneously given the option to do this with the second bloodline as well. However, you can only select the lowest ranks of Rage Powers (the lesser Totem abilities, the base Stances, etc.) and never any upgrades.

##### Second Mystery
Prerequisites:
Mystery

You gain a second Oracle Mystery.

##### Second Spirit
Prerequisites:
Spirit

You gain a second Shaman Spirit.

##### Spellcaster's Onslaught
After casting a spell, your next weapon attack receives an attack and damage bonus equal to half your mythic rank.

##### Throw Elixir
Prerequisites:
Infusion

Increases the range of an Alchemist's extracts from touch to close.

##### Witch Wandering Hex
Prerequisites:
Hex

You can select any hex for which you meet the prerequisites.

### Mythic Feats
You take mythic feats at even-numbered mythic ranks.

#### Melee Feats
##### Dazzling Display (Mythic)
Prerequisites:
Dazzling Display

You can use Dazzling Display as a standard action with a -5 penalty applied to Persuasion. Alternatively, you can use it as a move action at a -10 penalty or as a swift action with a -20 penalty.

##### Destructive Shockwave
When you miss with a melee attack, the target takes damage equal to your Strength modifier.

##### Flawless Attacks
You only suffer a -4 penalty on iterative attacks rather than -5.

##### Improved Critical (Mythic)
Prerequisites:
Improved Critical

The critical multiplier of your chosen weapon increases by +1.

##### Improved Unarmed Strike (Mythic)
Prerequisites:
Improved Unarmed Strike

Add half your mythic rank to unarmed strike damage.

##### Piranha Strike (Mythic)
Prerequisites:
Piranha Strike

The damage bonus increases to +3.

##### Power Attack (Mythic)
Prerequisites:
Power Attack

The damage bonus increases to +3. When using a two-handed weapon, a one-handed weapon in two hands or a primary natural weapon that adds 1.5x your Strength modifier, the damage bonus increases to +5.

##### Stunning Fist (Mythic)
Prerequisites:
Stunning Fist

The DC of your Stunning Fist attacks increases by half your mythic rank.

##### Two-Weapon Fighting (Mythic)
Prerequisites:
Two-Weapon Fighting

The attack roll penalties for fighting with two weapons are reduced by 2.

##### Vital Strike (Mythic)
Prerequisites:
Vital Strike

##### Weapon Finesse (Mythic)
Prerequisites:
Weapon Finesse

You may use your Dexterity modifier on your damage rolls.

##### Weapon Focus (Mythic)
Prerequisites:
Weapon Focus

Double the AB bonus from (Greater) Weapon Focus.

##### Weapon Specialization (Mythic)
Prerequisites:
Weapon Specialization

You gain an additional bonus on damage rolls equal to half your mythic rank.

#### Armour and Shield Feats
##### Mythic Armor Focus (Light Armor) - Assault
Prerequisites:
Armor Focus (Light)

While wearing light armour and wielding a weapon that can be used with Weapon Finesse, you gain an equipped armor bonus to attack rolls equal to half your armour's AC.

##### Mythic Armor Focus (Light Armor) - Avoidance
Prerequisites:
Armor Focus (Light)

While wearing light armour, add your mythic rank to the maximum Dexterity bonus allowed by your armour. All attacks against you suffer a 5% miss chance, increasing by an additional 5% at 4th, 7th and 10th mythic ranks.

##### Mythic Armor Focus (Light Armor) - Endurance
Prerequisites:
Armor Focus (Light)

While wearing light armour, you gain spell resistance equal to your armour's AC. This spell resistance stacks with other sources of spell resistance.

##### Mythic Armor Focus (Medium Armor) - Assault
Prerequisites:
Armor Focus (Medium)

While wearing medium armour, deal an additional 1d6 piercing damage + your armour's AC to targets that you hit with a melee attack.

##### Mythic Armor Focus (Medium Armor) - Avoidance
Prerequisites:
Armor Focus (Medium)

While wearing medium armour, add your Constitution modifier to the maximum Dexterity bonus allowed by your armour.

##### Mythic Armor Focus (Medium Armor) - Endurance
Prerequisites:
Armor Focus (Medium)

While wearing medium armour, your movement speed is not reduced and you gain an equipped armour bonus to AC equal to half your armour's AC.

##### Mythic Armor Focus (Heavy Armor) - Assault

##### Mythic Armor Focus (Heavy Armor) - Avoidance

Armor Focus (Heavy)

##### Mythic Armor Focus (Heavy Armor) - Endurance

##### Mythic Buckler - Flying Shield Style

##### Mythic Light Shield - Deflection

##### Mythic Heavy Shield - Aggressive Block
Prerequisites:
Shield Focus

##### Mythic Tower Shield - Fortress
Prerequisites:
Shield Focus

While wielding a tower shield, you gain DR N/- equal to your shield's AC. This DR stacks with other sources of DR.

##### Shield Focus (Mythic)

Shield Focus
