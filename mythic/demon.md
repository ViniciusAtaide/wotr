# Demon
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

A Demon is barely able to control the abyssal rage that is the essence of her being. She gains a spellbook with unique spells up to 5th level. Besides the unique spells which she learns automatically at the appropriate rank, she learns a number of spells of various levels as shown in the table.

#### Close to the Abyss
The character gains a secondary gore attack which deals 1d8 piercing damage. This counts as cold iron and magic for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction.

#### Demonkind
At 2nd mythic rank, the character gains resist electricity 20 and immunity to poison.

#### Summon Babau
At 2nd rank, you can summon a Babau for 1 minute.

#### Demonic Rage
At 3rd rank, you can enter Demonic Rage as a free action. While raging, you gain +2 bonus on attack rolls, damage rolls, caster level checks and Reflex saves. The DCs of your spells and abilities increase by 2. You can use this ability three times per day.

You gain an additional daily use and the bonuses increase by +1 at 6th and 9th ranks.

#### Demonic Charge
At 3rd rank, you can teleport to an enemy as a move action, dealing 2d6 unholy damage per mythic rank to all enemies within 10' of the start and end points.

#### Demonic Claws
At 3rd rank, you can grow claws at will. These are primary attacks and do 1d8 damage. If used with a weapon in the main-hand, the offhand claw is a secondary attack.

#### Summon Vrock
At 3rd rank, you can summon a Vrock for 1 minute.

#### Demonic Aspect
From 4th rank, when raging, you can adopt the aspect of a demon.

##### Aspect of Babau
You gain a bonus on Mobility, Trickery and Stealth skill checks equal to half your mythic rank + 1. You do an additional +2d6 sneak damage and any creature striking you in melee with a non-reach weapon takes 2d6 acid damage. Sneak damage and acid damage increases by +1d6 at 6th and 9th mythic ranks.

##### Aspect of Brimorak
You gain a bonus on Mobility, Trickery and Use Magic Device skill checks equal to half your mythic rank + 1. When you cast a spell that does damage, the damage is increased by +2 per damage die. This increases by +1 at 6th and 9th ranks. Any creature striking you in melee with a non-reach weapon takes 2d6 fire damage, increasing by +1d6 at 6th and 9th ranks.

##### Aspect of Incubus
You gain a bonus on Athletics, Lore (Nature) and Persuasion skill checks equal to half your mythic rank + 1. When you cast a spell, its DC increases by +1. This increases by +1 at 6th and 9th ranks. You also gain a +4 bonus to damage rolls, increasing by an additional +2 at 6th and 9th ranks.

##### Aspect of Kalavakus
You gain a bonus on Athletics, Perception and Use Magic Device skill checks equal to half your mythic rank + 1. You gain a +2 bonus to CMB, CMD and natural armour bonus to AC, increasing by +1 at 6th and 9th ranks. When you make a successful combat manoeuvre, you can make an attack against that target as a free action. When making a charge attack, you can attempt to disarm the target as a free action.

##### Aspect of Nabasu
You gain a bonus on Athletics, Lore (Religion) and Use Magic Device skill checks equal to half your mythic rank + 1. You gain a +2 bonus to saving throws, increasing by +1 at 6th and 9th ranks. All enemies with 30' receive a -1 penalty to Fortitude saves and attack rolls. These penalties increase by 1 at 6th and 9th ranks.

##### Aspect of Schir
You gain a bonus on Athletics, Mobility and Lore (Nature) skill checks equal to half your mythic rank + 1. You gain a +2 bonus on melee attack rolls, increasing by +1 at 6th and 9th ranks, You also gain a Powerful Charge ability, increasing the number of weapon damage dice by two and applying an additional 1.5x your Strength bonus to damage when charging.

##### Aspect of Succubus
You gain a bonus on Knowledge (World), Perception and Persuasion skill checks equal to half your mythic rank + 1. Enemies within 30' receive a -1 penalty to Will saves and attack rolls. Allies within 30' gain a +1 bonus to Will saves and attack rolls and immunity to compulsion and fear effects. The penalties and bonuses increase by 1 at 6th and 9th ranks.

##### Aspect of Vrock
You gain a bonus on Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World) and Lore (Religion) skill checks equal to half your mythic rank + 1. You gain +2 to ranged attack rolls, increasing by 1 at 6th and 9th ranks. All spells and non-physical abilities count as Reach spells. All enemies within 30' receive a -1 penalty to Reflex saves and move as if on difficult terrain. The save penalty increases by 1 at 6th and 9th ranks.

#### Summon Gibrileth
At 4th rank, you can summon a Gibrileth for 1 minute.

#### Extra Minor Aspect
At 5th and 8th ranks, you can use an additional minor aspect simultaneously.

#### Major Demonic Aspect
From 5th rank, when raging, you can assume the aspect of a major demon.

##### Aspect of Balor
You gain a bonus to Constitution equal to half of your mythic rank +1. Allies within 50' assume Demonic Rage alongside you.

##### Aspect of Coloxus
You gain a bonus to Intelligence equal to half of your mythic rank +1. You can use abilities and cast spells as move actions.

##### Aspect of Omox
You gain DR N/- equal to half of your mythic rank +1. All attacks and abilities make your target grappled unless they make a Reflex save (DC = 10 + half character level + mythic rank + highest ability modifier).

##### Aspect of Shadow Demon
You gain a bonus to Wisdom equal to half of your mythic rank +1 and become incorporeal.

##### Aspect of Vavakia
You gain a bonus to Strength equal to half of your mythic rank +1. Enemies within 50' receive 1 + mythic rank / 3 (rounded up) unholy damage per round.

##### Aspect of Vrolikai
You gain fast healing equal to half of your mythic rank +1. Your melee attacks and spells inflict one negative level on enemies failing a Will save (DC = 10 + half character level + mythic rank + highest ability modifier).

#### True Demonkind
At 5th mythic rank, you gain immunity to poison and electricity.

#### Summon Oolioddroo
At 5th rank, you can summon an Oolioddroo for 1 minute.

#### Summon Painajai
At 6th rank, you can summon a Painajai for 1 minute.

#### Deadly Natural Weapons
At 7th rank, your natural weapons ignore demons' damage reduction and count as having +2 enhancement bonus. In addition, all attacks count as primary attacks.

#### Summon Lilitu
At 7th rank, you can summon a Lilitu for 1 minute.

#### Summon Vrolikai
At 8th rank, you can summon a Vrolikai for 1 minute.

#### Demon Lord Aspect
From 9th rank, when raging, you can assume the aspect of a demon lord.

##### Aspect of Areshkagal
Any attack targeting you has a 50% miss chance.

##### Aspect of Deskari
All your energy damage becomes unholy and your physical damage ignores physical damage reduction and damage immunity.

##### Aspect of Kabriri
You heal every time you kill an enemy.

##### Aspect of Nocticula
Once per rage, you grant an ally Demonic Rage including the effects of your active aspects and major aspects.

##### Aspect of Pazuzu
Your speed increases by +30' and you gain an additional attack which stacks with rage.

##### Aspect of Socothbenoth
Any ability or spell you use forces the target to roll its save twice and take the worse result.

#### Summon Balor
At 9th rank, you can summon a Balor for 1 minute.

##### Playing a Demon
If you were expecting Demon to be, for want of a better word, a monster, you may be disappointed. Essentially, the problem with the Demon path is that its core ability has very limited uses. For the majority of your time as a Demon, you can rage no more three times a day. Even at 10th rank, you are limited to five uses. This is compounded by the party-unfriendly nature of many demonic abilities (i.e. "All creatures" rather than "All enemies").

Demon has no obvious class synergy - certainly not with a Barbarian or Bloodrager like you might expect. Despite the - mostly - indifferent spellbook, the best synergy is an Intelligence-based save-or-else caster. This is because Demonic Rage can give you very high DC bonuses (the rage itself gives you +2 and the aspects of Incubus, Succubus and Coloxus increase DC, lower enemy saves and increase your casting attribute). Alternatively, the Demonic Charge ability (which isn't limited) favours a Vital Strike build.
