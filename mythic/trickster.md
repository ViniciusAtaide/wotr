# Trickster
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

A Trickster is adept at bending reality to conform to how he thinks the world *should* behave. He gains a spellbook with unique spells up to 7th level. Besides the unique spells which he learns automatically at the appropriate rank, he learns a number of spells of various levels as shown in the table.

#### Bit of Fun
The character gains the ability to create three illusionary copies of themselves. The copies last for 1 minute per mythic rank and the character can use this ability three times per day.

#### Creative Approach
At 2nd mythic rank, the character gains a +3 circumstance bonus to all skill checks.

#### Summon Will-o'-Wisp
At 2nd rank, you can summon a Will-o'-Wisp for 1 minute.

#### Sneak Attack
At 3rd rank, a Trickster gains +2d6 sneak damage. This increases by +1d6 at 5th, 7th and 9th ranks.

#### Sneaky Quack
At 3rd rank, if an ally is denied a Dexterity bonus, any heal spell you use on them restores an additional 1d6 damage for each of your sneak attack dice.

For the purposes of triggering this ability, you are an ally and fast healing (from, for example, the Sword of Valor) is treated as a spell that you cast on yourself which means that you can potentially have very high regeneration. Something like Uncanny Dodge will prevent it from triggering at all, however.

#### Mythic Trick
From 3rd rank and each subsequent rank, you can use your skills in creative ways.

##### Athletics I
Each round, you can make an Athletics check to remove a negative condition. The DC is the original save DC + 15.

##### Mobility I
Your movement speed increases by half.

##### Trickery I
You can attempt to dispel any effects on a target or on the ground, equivalent to *Dispel Magic*. The caster level check is replaced by a Trickery skill check with a -10 penalty.

Note that none of the Trickery abilities are ranged.

##### Stealth I
You can enter stealth as a move action. You gain total concealment against any creature that fails to make a Perception check against you.

##### Knowledge (Arcana) I
Every item with an enhancement bonus that you identify has its enhancement bonus increases by 1.

Note that it has to be *you* who identifies an item to increase its enhancement bonus so you will need to invest in the Knowledge (Arcana) skill.

##### Knowledge (World) I
When you or an ally within 50' make a skill check, you add half your mythic rank to the roll.

##### Lore (Nature) I
Every time you rest, you find a random potion, a random scroll and enough food to cook a beneficial meal.

##### Lore (Religion) I
Treat Affliction removes not only poisons and diseases but any negative conditions affecting the target.

##### Perception I
You are under the constant effect of *See Invisibility*.

##### Persuasion I
Any enemy beginning combat against you is automatically affected by your demoralise ability.

##### Infuse Magic Device
The caster level of any ability from magical items that you use is increased by your mythic rank.

#### Summon Nixie Prankster
At 3rd rank, you can summon a Nixie Prankster for 1 minute.

#### Improved Mythic Trick
At 4th, 6th, 8th and 9th ranks, you can upgrade one of your level 1 mythic tricks.

##### Athletics II
When you fail a saving throw, you can make an Athletics check with a DC equal to the original save DC + 20 to pass the saving throw.

##### Mobility II
When you succeed at a Mobility check to avoid an attack of opportunity, you make an attack of opportunity and the enemy is caught flat-footed.

##### Trickery II
You can attempt to dispel all effects on a target or on the ground, equivalent to *Greater Dispel Magic*. The caster level check is replaced by a Trickery skill check with a -20 penalty.

##### Stealth II
Stealth in combat works like *Greater Invisibility*.

##### Knowledge (Arcana) II
Every item that you identify gets an additional random minor effect.

##### Knowledge (World) II
When you or an ally roll a 1 on a d20, the roll becomes 20 instead.

##### Lore (Nature) II
Every time you hunt, you receive gold, potions, scrolls and other valuables.

##### Lore (Religion) II
You can select two domains. You gain the domain abilities and can use spells from those domains once per day if the level of those spells is lower than your mythic rank.

This mythic trick does not allow you to take the *Domain Zealot* and *Impossible Domain* mythic abilities.

##### Perception II
You automatically detect creatures who attempt to use Stealth. You and your companions can now select feats from the Trickster feat list.

##### Persuasion II
Enemies affected by your demoralise ability become paralysed unless they make a Will save (DC = 10 + Persuasion rank). They may attempt another saving throw against this condition each round.

##### Reuse Magic Device
Using a wand doesn't consume a charge and you can equip any magical items regardless of requirements.

#### Summon Redcaps
At 4th rank, you can summon a bunch of Redcaps for 1 minute.

#### Summon Wild Hunt Archer
At 5th rank, you can summon a Wild Hunt Archer for 1 minute.

#### Summon Wild Hunt Scout
At 6th rank, you can summon a Wild Hunt Scout for 1 minute.

#### Greater Mythic Trick
At 7th and 10th ranks, you can upgrade one of your level 2 mythic tricks.

##### Athletics III
Your BAB is at least your Athletics rank. If your BAB is equal to your character level, it is at least your Athletics rank + 5.

##### Mobility III
When you are hit, you can attempt a Mobility check with a -10 penalty against the attack result. If you succeed, the attack misses.

##### Trickery III
You can attempt to disable a living creature. If they fail a Fortitude save (DC = 15 + ranks in Trickery), they die.

##### Stealth III
You gain spell resistance equal to your Stealth bonus - 10.

##### Knowledge (Arcana) III
Every item that you identify gets an additional random major effect.

##### Knowlege (World) III
You and your allies can ignore feat prerequisites when levelling up.

##### Lore (Nature) III
Every time you hunt, you find random magic items.

These are generic items and worthless by the time that you can unlock this ability.

##### Lore (Religion) III
You can select two additional domains.

##### Perception III
You gain two rank 1 mythic tricks that you can change daily.

##### Persuasion III
Enemies failing their saving throw against your demoralise ability must spend their first round making a Coup de Grace against themselves.

##### Use Magic Device III
You gain a spellbook and can copy spells from scrolls into it as if you were a wizard of your character level.

#### Summon Wild Hunt (I)
At 7th rank, you can summon a Wild Hunt Archer and a Wild Hunt Scout for 1 minute.

#### Summon Wild Hunt Monarch
At 8th rank, you can summon a Wild Hunt Monarch for 1 minute.

#### Summon Wild Hunt (II)
At 9th rank, you can summon a Wild Hunt battle party for 1 minute.

##### Playing a Trickster
Whichever mythic path you intend to take, Creative Approach is the best 2nd rank ability so you'll generally want to start off down the Trickster route.

If you choose to take things further, plan the mythic path around which of the Mythic Tricks are most important to you. I suggest that you decide which greater tricks you want and work backwards. Persuasion III is obviously one to go for and Athletics III gives you as much as +10 BAB. Use Magic Device III can be really strong if you're an Intelligence-based caster (for example, most Witch archetypes). The really strong improved tricks are Perception (II), which you'll probably want at rank 4, Knowledge (World) II, Trickery II and Lore (Religion) II. Knowledge Arcana (I) doesn't just increase the enhancement bonuses of weapons and armour but also of equipment that boosts attributes, allowing you to take advantage of odd-numbered ability scores.

Not all tricks require investment in the skill itself. However, if you're going to build around the Mobility and Trickery tricks, for example, you need to invest them in heavily.

The sneak dice gained by a Trickster stack with any sneak attacks gained from your main class. You receive 2d6 sneak damage at rank 3 which qualifies you for Arcane Trickster if you wanted to go down that route.

The spellbook has a couple of useful spells, including *Mirror Image*, However, the unique spells are mostly joke garbage. You will maybe a summon a perpetually annoyed wizard once. The 7th level *Trick Fate* is extremely powerful but rank 9 is close to the end of the game.

Because the Trickster feats (greatly) boost critical hits, Trickster is good for any martial build. Of course, the right combination of Mythic Tricks can make *any* build a martial build!

### Trickster Bonus Feats
At Perception II, a Trickster opens up access to a number of powerful feats that both your character and other party members may select:

#### Improved Improved Critical
Prerequisites:
Improved Critical

When using the selected weapon, its critical threat range is increased by +1.

#### Improved Improved Improved Critical
Prerequisites:
Improved Improved Critical

When using the selected weapon, its critical threat range is increased by an additional +1.

#### Improved Improved Improved Critical Improved
Prerequisites:
Improved Improved Improved Critical

When using the selected weapon, its critical multiplier is increased by +1.

#### Metamagic (Completely Normal Spell)
The spell has its level decreased by 1.

This allows you to cast 1st level spells as cantrips.

#### Stat Focus
You gain a +1 Mythic bonus to one of: AC, Attack, Attacks of Opportunity, CMB, CMD, Damage, HP, Initiative, Fortitude, Reflex, Will or Speed.
