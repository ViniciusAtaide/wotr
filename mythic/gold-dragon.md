# Gold Dragon
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

You are an avatar of the celestial dragon.

Gold Dragon is a late-game mythic path that you begin at mythic rank 8. You lose any powers you gained at ranks 3 - 7, retaining only your mythic abilities and feats.

#### Gold Dragon Breath
You receive a 50' cone breath attack. Enemies caught in the area of effect take holy damage equal to 1d8 multiplied by your character level + mythic rank / 2 with a Reflex save (DC = 10 + character level / 2 + mythic rank + highest ability modifier) for half damage. Any enemy hit by the breath attack receives a -1 AB penalty or -2 if they failed their save. You can use this attack every d4 rounds. At rank 9, the cooldown is reduced to 1d2 rounds. At rank 10, you can use this attack every round.

#### Improved Gold Dragon Breath
At each Mythic Rank, you can select an additional breath effect (three additional effects at rank 10).

##### Disrupting Breath
Undead enemies struck by your breath take additional positive energy damage equal to your breath attack damage. A successful Fortitude save halves this damage.

##### Empowered Breath
Your breath damage increases by a number of damage dice equal to your Mythic Rank.

##### Mind-Clearing Breath
Your breath dispels all mind-affecting effects applied by enemies from allies in the area effect. Additionally, you dispel all mind-affecting effects from enemies struck by the breath.

##### Overwhelming Breath
You add twice your Mythic Rank to the save DC of your breath attack.

##### Scorching Breath
Enemies failing their saving throw against you breath attack catch fire, taking your breath damage each subsequent round until they make a successful Reflex save against the DC of your breath.

##### Spellbane Breath
Enemies struck by your breath take a penalty to the save DC of all spells they cast equal to half your Mythic Rank for 1d4+1 rounds.

##### Annihilating Breath
Prerequisites:
Mythic Rank: 9
Vow of Dahak

Living enemies struck by your breath take additional negative energy damage equal to your breath attack damage. A successful Fortitude save halves this damage.

##### Decaying Breath
Prerequisites:
Mythic Rank: 9
Vow of Dahak

Living enemies struck by your breath take 1d4 + Mythic Rank / 2 Constitution damage. A successful Fortitude save halves this damage.

##### Enlightening Breath
Prerequisites:
Mythic Rank: 9
Uncorrupted

Your breath dispels negative levels affecting allies in the area of effect and cures all ability damage.

##### Rejuvenating Breath
Prerequisites:
Mythic Rank: 9
Uncorrupted

Your breath heals allies in the area of effect by an amount equal to your breath damage.

#### Perfect Body
You are immune to poison, disease, fear, confusion, paralysis and sleep. You gain resistance to all types of energy equal to your character level + mythic rank / 2.

Your unmodified saving throws are at least 5 + your mythic rank. All of your attributes are at least 14 and your highest ability score gets a +4 increase. Your hit dice are d12. Any spells with damage dice now deal at least 1d6 damage per die. If the damage die was already 1d6 or higher, the spell deals +1 damage per die. Finally, all your weapons count as +5 weapons.

#### Dragon Feats
At 9th rank, you can select any three feats without having to meet the prerequisites.

#### Gold Dragon Form
At 9th rank, you can assume the form a Gold Dragon, gaining a +10 size bonus to Strength, +8 size bonus to Constitution, +8 natural armour bonus and blindsense to 60'. You gain a 2d8 bite attack, two 2d6 claw attacks, two 1d8 wing attacks and a 2d6 tail slap.

#### Perfect Mind
At 9th rank, you gain immunity to mind-affecting effects and spell resistance equal to your character level + mythic rank / 2. Your base attack bonus is at least 15. All skills are class skills and your rank in any skill is at least your mythic rank. All of your attributes are at least 18 and your highest ability score gets an additional +2 increase. Any spells with damage dice now deal at least 1d8 damage per die. If the damage die was already 1d8 or higher, the spell deals +1 damage per die.

#### Perfect Soul
At 10th rank, you gain immunity to cold, fire, electricity, acid, energy drain and negative energy. All damage is now holy damage. Any spells with damage dice now deal at least 1d10 damage per die. If the damage die was already 1d10 or higher, the spell deals +1 damage per die. Finally, your highest ability score gets an additional +2 bonus (for a total of +8).

##### Playing a Gold Dragon
Note that there are two Gold Dragon paths: one where you remain uncorrupted and another where you become sworn to the god of evil dragons. The main differences between the two are your spell selections and breath weapon improvements.

Although you lose existing mythic abilities. the Gold Dragon abilities are decent, particularly if you are playing an offensive spellcaster. If you don't want to go Legend, Gold Dragon also gives you a way out of the Demon and Lich paths if you don't want to commit atrocities.
