# Appendix - Ascension
*Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

The secret ending takes the whole game to unlock and is easily missable in many ways. If you follow the "Ascension" callouts in the walkthrough, you will unlock the secret ending and the related trophy. If you are looking for a reference or a cheat-sheet, read on:

#### Prove to Areelu that you are not simply a failed experiment
After you defeat Areelu, you must be able to present five arguments that you are not the failed experiment she believes you to be. There are actually seven arguments that you can make, so you have a little leeway here. The things you have to do to be able to make these arguments are as follows:

- (Prologue) In Kenabres square, when given the choice between a crossbow and an escape scroll, take the crossbow.
- (Chapter 2) When meeting Yaniel in the Drezen Citadel, she mentions that we have a lot to learn from demons. Give the (Evil) or non-committal response.
- (Chapter 4) When meeting Areelu in Noticula's Palace, do not simply accept the power offered you. Instead, pump her for information.
- (Chapter 5) Go to the secret areas in Areelu's Laboratory. When interacting with the child's things, do so in the following order: crib, drawings, textbook. You'll know you've got it right if you find a pair of gloves. Grab the Vial of Magical Formula and when you find yourself in the past, use it in the summoning circle and kill the demons that appear. In the final area, interact with the ghosts, decide what to do with them and be prepared to justify your actions to Areelu. Do NOT break the projector that Areelu uses.
- (Chapter 5) Reach Mythic rank 9. Do NOT choose the Legend path.

#### Gather Nahyndrian crystals
- (Chapter 1) Pick up the Purple Stone Knife in the market square in Kenabres. Do not take the options at the end of the chapter which will result in you losing it.
- (Chapter 4) Shoot Baphomet in the Colyphyr Mines with a Midnight Bolt.
- (Chapter 5) Shoot Nocticula with a Midnight Bolt (Trickster-only)
- (Chapter 5) Shoot Deskari in Iz and Areshkagal in the Enigma with Midnight Bolts.
- (Chapter 6) Shoot Baphomet and Deskari again in the Threshold when Areelu summons them.

##### Midnight Bolt Locations
Note that there are seven bolts in the game, but you only need five to create Nahyndrian crystals.

- (Chapter 1) Found alongside the Purple Stone Knife in the market square in Kenabres.
- (Chapter 3) Found in the northwestern section of the Ivory Sanctum.
- (Chapter 3) Found on the upper level of Areelu's Laboratory.
- (Chapter 4) Found in the library in the House of Silken Shadows.
- (Chapter 4) Found in a torture chamber in the lower level of the Colyphyr Mines.
- (Chapter 5) Found in the ruined house in Iz.
- (Chapter 5) Found in the southern section of the Ineluctable Prison.

#### Learn that Areelu has a softer side
- (Chapter 3) In Areelu's Lab, do NOT be hostile to Suture when you meet him.
- (Chapter 4) Free Suture from the Fleshmarkets. When meeting Areelu in Noticula's Palace, point out that Suture is there as well. Ask about the ploy in the lab and make the Perception check to note that you provoked a reaction.

#### Do your homework
This is not possible for the Swarm That Walks Mythic path because you cannot issue the required research decrees.

- (Chapter 3) Issue the *Studying the Sword of Valor* decree.
- (Chapter 3) Grab the first half of the *Lexicon of Paradox* from Areelu's Lab.
- (Chapter 4) Grab the second half of the *Lexicon of Paradox* from Noticula's Palace.
- (Chapter 5) Issue the *Studying the Unusual Crystal* decree,
- (Chapter 5) In Pulura's Fall, do not allow Mutasafen to escape with the research. Afterwards, issue the *Records of Pulura's Stargazers* decree. Note that if your mythic path is Angel, you can complete this research in Chapter 3.
- (Chapter 5) In Iz, head to the library first and grab the "Key" Crystal and Glass Key before Deskari's goons smash the place up.
- (Chapter 5) Use the "Key" crystal to access the secret areas in Areelu's Laboratory. Use the Glass Key to get into the area with Areelu Vorlesh's Drafts. Afterwards, issue the *Studying Areelu Vorlesh's Drafts* decree.
- (Chapter 5) Before leaving the Ineluctable Prison, backtrack and find the spirit of Hepzamirah. Speak to her to learn that demon lords must come when summoned by their high priest or priestess.
- (Chapter 6) Read the Lexicon of Paradox. If you find all the Elven Notes for Storyteller, he can help you. Otherwise, you need to pass stiff Knowledge and Lore checks (38 - 41) to make sense of it.

#### Arrive on time
The Records of Pulura's Stargazers give you a date when the Worldwound will be at its strongest: 3rd week of Gozran, 4717. Make sure that you enter Threshold between the 15th and 21st of the fourth month. When you meet Areelu, marshal your arguments and use your knowledge to attain immortality.
