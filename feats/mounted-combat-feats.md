# Feats — Mounted Combat Feats
*5 feats. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

## Mounted Combat
**Prereq:** Mobility 1
Once per round when your mount is hit in combat, you may make a Mobility check vs. the opponent's attack roll to negate the hit.

## Indomitable Mount
**Prereq:** Mobility 5
Once per round when your mount fails a saving throw, it will make its save if you make a Mobility check vs. the DC of the opponent's attack.

## Mounted Shield
**Prereq:** Mounted Combat, Shield Focus
You may add your base shield bonus (but not any enhancement bonus) and the bonus from Shield Focus to your mount's AC.

## Spirited Charge
**Prereq:** Mounted Combat
When mounted and using the charge action, you deal double damage with melee weapons.

## Trample
**Prereq:** Mounted Combat
You gain the ability to trample enemies while mounted. If the trample succeeds, your mount deals damage to the target knocked prone as if it were hit by one natural attack.
