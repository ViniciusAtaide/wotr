# Feats — Class Feats
*39 feats. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

## Ability Focus - Bombs
Prerequisites:
Bomb or Arcane Bombs
Add +2 to the DC for all saving throws against your bombs.

## Accomplished Sneak Attacker
**Prereq:** Sneak Attack: 1
Your sneak attack damage increases by 1d6. Your number of sneak attack dice cannot exceed half your character level.

## Boon Companion
**Prereq:** Animal Companion
The abilities of an animal companion are calculated as though your class level were 4 levels higher to a maximum of your character level.

## Discordant Voice
**Prereq:** Bardic Performance, Character level: 4
When using bardic performance to affect allies, their weapon attacks deal +1d6 sonic damage.

## Diva Style
**Prereq:** Charisma: 13, Feint, Bard level: 1
You can attempt a Persuasion (Bluff) check to feint as a free action when starting a bardic performance.

## Diva Strike
**Prereq:** Charisma: 15, Combat Expertise, Diva Style, Feint, Bard level: 7
When using Diva Style, your first attack in a round against enemies denied their Dexterity bonus to AC deals extra damage equal to your Charisma modifier. This is precision damage and is not multiplied on a critical hit.

## Diva Advance
**Prereq:** Charisma: 17, Combat Expertise, Diva Style, Diva Strike, Feint, Bard level: 11
When using Diva Style, you make an attack of opportunity against an enemy if your feint attempt succeeds. Creatures successfully feinted cannot make attacks of opportunity for 1 round.

## Energized Wild Shape
**Prereq:** Wisdom: 19, Major Form or Wild Shape (Wolf)
When assuming a wild shape form add +1d6 damage of a selected energy type and gain resistance 10 to the same energy type. If the assumed form already provides energy damage, the damage inflicted increases to the next damage die value. If the assumed form already provides energy resistance, the resistance provided increases by 5.

## Extended Aspects
**Prereq:** Shifter Aspect
Add your Wisdom bonus (minimum 1) to the number of minutes per day that you can maintain a minor form.

## Extra Arcana
**Prereq:** Magus Arcana
You gain one additional magus arcana for which you must meet the prerequisites. You can take this feat up to 3 times.

## Extra Arcane Pool
**Prereq:** Arcane Pool or Eldritch Pool
Your arcane pool increases by 2. This feat may be taken multiple times.

## Extra Arcanist Exploit
**Prereq:** Arcanist Exploits
You gain one additional exploit for which you must meet the prerequisites. You can take this feat up to 3 times.

## Extra Bane Weapon
**Prereq:** Bane
You may use the Inquisitor's bane weapon ability for 3 additional rounds per day.

## Extra Bombs
Prerequisites:
Bomb

## Frightful Shape
**Prereq:** Wisdom: 19, Major Form or Wild Shape (Wolf)
Whenever you attack while shifted, enemies within 30' must make a Will save (DC = 10 + class level / 2 + Charisma modifier). On a failed save, creatures with fewer HD than you become shaken for a number of rounds equal to your character level. Creatures with 4HD or fewer become frightened. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

## Harmonic Spell
**Prereq:** Bard level 1
When casting a 1st level spell or higher while using bardic performance, restore one round of performance.

## Lingering Performance
**Prereq:** Bardic Performance
The bonuses and penalties for singing continue for two rounds after you cease performing.

## Natural Spell
**Prereq:** Wild Shape (Wolf), Wisdom: 13
You can cast spells while in shifted form.

## Raging Brutality
**Prereq:** Strength: 13, Power Attack, Base Attack Bonus: 12
While raging and using Power Attack, you may use a swift action to add your Constitution bonus to damage rolls for melee attacks and thrown weapon attacks made in that turn. If a melee weapon is held in both hands, add 1.5x your Constitution bonus. This damage is not multiplied on a critical hit.

## Raking Claws
**Prereq:** BAB: 8, Major Form or Wild Shape (Wolf)
When dealing piercing or slashing damage with natural weapons, your opponent takes 1d6 bleed damage each round.

## Selective Channel
**Prereq:** Channel Energy, Charisma: 13
When you channel energy to heal allies, you do not heal enemies. When you channel energy to damage enemies, you do not affect allies.

## Shifter's Edge
**Prereq:** Shifter Claws, Weapon Finesse, Dexterity 13
When using your Dexterity modifier for attack rolls (but not damage rolls), add half your class level to damage.
This feat is exclusive with Mythic Finesse.

## Shifter's Multiattack
**Prereq:** Shifter level: 6
Secondary attacks with natural weapons take only a -2 penalty instead of -5.

## Shifter's Rush
**Prereq:** Major Form or Wild Shape (Wolf)
When charging or moving more than 10' in a single round, using wild shape is a free action rather than a standard action.

## Warrior Priest
**Prereq:** Ability to cast 1st level divine spells
You gain a +1 bonus on initiative checks and a +2 bonus on concentration checks.
