# Feats — Weapon Feats
*48 feats. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

## Simple Weapon Proficiency
Become proficient in *all* of the following weapons:
Club, Dagger, Dart, Greatclub, Heavy Crossbow, Heavy Mace, Javelin, Light Crossbow, Light Mace, Longspear, Punching Dagger, Quarterstaff, Shortspear, Sickle, Spear.
All classes apart from Druids, Monks and Wizards receive this feat automatically.

## Martial Weapons Proficiency
Become proficient in *all* of the following weapons:
Bardiche, Battle Axe, Earth Breaker, Falchion, Flail, Glaive, Greataxe, Greatsword, Hand Axe, Heavy Flail, Heavy Pick, Heavy Shield Bash, Kukri, Light Hammer, Light Pick, Light Shield Bash, Long Bow, Longsword, Rapier, Scimitar, Scythe, Short Bow, Short Sword, Starknife, Throwing Axe, Trident, Warhammer.
Grenadiers, Barbarians (all), Bloodragers (all), Cavaliers (all), Fighters (all), Hunters (all), Maguses (all), Sohei, Paladins (all), Rangers (all), Rowdy Rogue, Sklads (all), Slayers (all) and Warpriests (all apart from Cult Leader) receive this feat automatically.

## Exotic Weapon Proficiency
**Prereq:** Base Attack Bonus: 1
Become proficient in *one* of the following weapons:
Bastard Sword, Dueling Sword, Dwarven Waraxe, Dwarven Urgrosh, Elven Curve Blade, Estoc, Falcata, Fauchard, Gnome Hooked Hammer, Kama, Nunchaku, Sai, Tongi, Sling Staff, Orc Double Axe, Two-bladed Sword.
Certain non-human races may gain exotic proficiencies for free. For example, an elf character with Martial Weapon Proficiency will also be proficient with with the Elven Curve Blade.
Monk proficiencies include Kama, Nunchaku and Sai. The Aldori Defender receives proficiency in the Dueling Sword as does a character with the Aldori Swordsman background.

## Weapon Finesse
When wielding a light weapon, use your Dexterity modifier for attack rolls rather than your Strength modifier. Note that this only applies to attack rolls but not damage rolls.
The game is not terribly clear as to which weapons this feat applies to. They are:

- Simple: Dagger, Light Mace, Punching Dagger, Sickle
- Martial: Handaxe, Kukri, Light Hammer, Light Pick, Rapier, Short Sword, Starknife
- Exotic: Dueling Sword, Elven Curve Blade, Estoc, Kama, Sai, Sawtooth Saber.

## Weapon Focus
**Prereq:** Base Attack Bonus: 1
Choose a single weapon and gain +1 to attack rolls with that weapon. This can also be selected for unarmed strike (for Monks), natural weapons (for Wildshaped Druids) and touch attacks and rays (for spellcasters) and may be selected multiple times, although it must be applied to a different weapon each time.

## Greater Weapon Focus
**Prereq:** Fighter level: 8, Weapon Focus
Choose a single weapon for which you have already selected Weapon Focus and gain an additional +1 to attack rolls with that weapon.

## Weapon Specialization
**Prereq:** Fighter level: 4, Weapon Focus
Choose a single weapon for which you have already selected Weapon Focus and gain +2 to damage rolls with that weapon.

## Greater Weapon Specialization
**Prereq:** Fighter level: 12, Weapon Specialization
Choose a single weapon for which you have already selected Weapon Specialization and gain an additional +2 to damage rolls with that weapon.

## Advanced Weapon Training
**Prereq:** Fighter level 5, Weapon Training
You can select one of the following Weapon Training options: Armed Bravery, Combat Maneuver Defense, Effortless Dual Wielding, Fighter's Finesse, Fighter's Reflexes, Fighter's Tactics, Trained Initiative.

## Dazzling Display
**Prereq:** Weapon Focus
As a full-round action, attempt to demoralize all foes within 30'. This requires a Persuasion check.

## Shatter Defenses
**Prereq:** Weapon Focus, Dazzling Display, Base Attack Bonus: 6
Any shaken, frightened or panicked opponent struck by you counts as flat-footed until the end of your next turn.
This feat is only useful if you are using iterative attacks since your first attack won't be made against flat-footed AC. Builds based around Vital Strike, for example, will want to steer clear.

## Penetrating Strike
**Prereq:** Fighter level: 12, Weapon Focus
Attacks made with weapons for which you have selected Weapon Focus ignore up to 5 points of damage reduction.

## Greater Penetrating Strike
**Prereq:** Fighter level: 16, Penetrating Strike
Attacks made with weapons for which you have selected Weapon Focus ignore up to 10 points of damage reduction.

## Aldori Dueling Mastery
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 13, Weapon Finesse, Weapon Focus (Dueling Sword)
Add +2 to initiative checks when wielding a dueling sword. Gain +2 shield bonus to AC if not using a shield or an off-hand weapon. The dueling sword is treated as a piercing weapon for the effects of a weapon used by a Duelist.

## Fencing Grace
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 13, Weapon Finesse, Weapon Focus (One-Handed Piercing)
When wielding a finessable, one-handed piercing weapon in which you have taken Weapon Focus (i.e. Dagger, Estoc, Punching Dagger, Rapier, Sai, Starknife), not using a shield or off-hand weapon and not using Flurry of Blows, you can add your Dexterity bonus to weapon damage.

## Slashing Grace
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 13, Weapon Finesse, Weapon Focus (One-Handed Slashing)
When wielding a one-handed slashing weapon in which you have taken Weapon Focus, not using a shield or off-hand weapon and not using Flurry of Blows, you can add your Dexterity bonus to weapon damage. The chosen weapon is treated as a piercing weapon for the effects of a weapon used by a Duelist.
Although the Elven Curve Blade benefits from Weapon Finesse, it is a two-handed weapon so does not benefit from Slashing Grace. If you select non-finessable weapons, such as Longswords and Scimitars, you will get a Dexterity to-hit bonus even though they don't benefit from Weapon Finesse.

## Piranha Strike
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 13, Weapon Finesse
When wielding a finessable weapon, you can choose to take a -1 penalty to attack rolls in return for +2 damage. When your Base Attack Bonus reaches 4 (and every four points thereafter), the penalty increases by -1 and the damage increases by +2 (so that at BAB 8, you suffer -3 to attack rolls but add +6 to damage rolls).
When making an attack with an off-hand weapon, the damage bonus is reduced to +1 per level of Piranha Strike.

## Power Attack
**Prereq:** Strength: 13
You can choose to take a -1 penalty to attack rolls in return for +2 damage. When your Base Attack Bonus reaches 4 (and every four points thereafter), the penalty increases by -1 and the damage increases by +2 (so that at BAB 8, you suffer -3 to attack rolls but add +6 to damage rolls).
If using a two-handed weapon, or a natural weapon that adds 50% to Strength bonus, damage increases to +3 per level of Power Attack. If using an off-hand weapon, damage is reduced to +1 per level.

## Cleave
**Prereq:** Power Attack
As a standard action, make an attack against a single opponent at full attack bonus. If your attack hits, you can make a second attack at full attack bonus against an adjacent opponent. This inflicts a -2 penalty to your AC until your next turn.

## Great Cleave
**Prereq:** Cleave
As a standard action, make an attack against a single opponent at full attack bonus. If your attack hits, you can make a second attack at full attack bonus against an adjacent opponent and continue doing so until you run out of opponents to attack. Any one foe cannot be attacked more than once in this manner. This inflicts a -2 penalty to your AC until your next turn.

## Cleaving Finish
**Prereq:** Cleave
If your attack reduces an opponent to 0 HP, you can make an attack at full attack bonus against any opponent within reach. You can only make one such attack per round.

## Improved Cleaving Finish
**Prereq:** Great Cleave, Cleaving Finish, Base Attack Bonus: 6
You can use Cleaving Finish any number of times per round.

## Cornugon Smash
**Prereq:** Power Attack, Persuasion: 6
If you damage an opponent with a Power Attack, you may make a Persuasion attempt as a free action to attempt to demoralize your opponent.

## Dreadful Carnage
**Prereq:** Strength: 15, Dazzling Display, Power Attack, Base Attack Bonus: 11
When you reduce an enemy to 0 HP, you can make a Persuasion attempt as a free action to attempt to demoralize all foes within 30' who witness the attack.

## Focused Strike
**Prereq:** Strength: 13, Power Attack, Base Attack Bonus: 1
If using a two-handed weapon, or a one-handed weapon with two hands, you do not apply the Power Attack attack roll penalty to the first attack made in a given turn.

## Two-Weapon Fighting
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 15
Reduces the penalties for wielding an off-hand weapon to -4 / -4 (-2 / -2 if the off-hand weapon is light).

## Improved Two-Weapon Fighting
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 17, Two-Weapon Fighting, Base Attack Bonus: 6
You gain a second attack with the off-hand weapon at a -5 penalty.

## Greater Two-Weapon Fighting
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 19, Improved Two-Weapon Fighting, Base Attack Bonus: 11
You gain a third attack with the off-hand weapon at a -10 penalty.

## Double Slice
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 15, Two-Weapon Fighting
Apply your whole Strength bonus to damage rolls made with your off-hand weapon (rather than just half).

## Vital Strike
**Prereq:** Base Attack Bonus: 6
As a standard action, make one attack at your highest base attack bonus and roll the weapon's damage roll twice. Note that damage bonuses (such as from Strength) are added to the combined total, not to each roll.

## Improved Vital Strike
**Prereq:** Vital Strike, Base Attack Bonus: 11
As a standard action, make one attack at your highest base attack bonus and roll the weapon's damage roll three times.

## Greater Vital Strike
**Prereq:** Improved Vital Strike, Base Attack Bonus: 16
As a standard action, make one attack at your highest base attack bonus and roll the weapon's damage roll four times.

## Hammer the Gap
**Prereq:** Base Attack Bonus: 6
Each consecutive hit does additional damage equal to the number of previous consecutive hits that you have made this turn. The bonus damage is multiplied on a critical hit.

## Lunge
**Prereq:** Base Attack Bonus: 6
You increase the reach of your melee attacks by 5' until the end of your turn at the cost of a -2 penalty to your AC.

## Improved Critical
**Prereq:** Base Attack Bonus: 8
Select one weapon type. When using that weapon, the threat range is doubled.
The doubling is applied *before* any other abilities or effects that extend threat range. Rather counterintuitively, this applies to weapons with larger than normal threat ranges (Gravesinger, Peacemaker, Impending Demise). The 18-20 threat range for Gravesinger, for example is implemented as a 5% chance (i.e. 20x3) + 10% . Improved Critical turns this into a 10% chance + 10% giving a threat range of 17-20 rather than the expected 15-20. Whether this is *intended*, I cannot say.

## Critical Focus
**Prereq:** Base Attack Bonus: 9
You receive a +4 circumstance bonus to attack rolls made to confirm critical hits.

## Flaying Critical
**Prereq:** Critical Focus, Base Attack Bonus: 11
When you score a critical hit, you apply a stacking -1 penalty to your opponent's natural armor bonus to AC.

## Sickening Critical
**Prereq:** Critical Focus, Base Attack Bonus: 11
When you score a critical hit, your opponent becomes sickened for 1 minute. Additional critical hits add to the effect's duration.

## Staggering Critical
**Prereq:** Critical Focus, Base Attack Bonus: 13
When you score a critical hit, your opponent is staggered for 2-5 rounds unless it makes a Fortitude save (DC 10 + your BAB) in which case it is staggered for 1 round. Additional critical hits add to the effect's duration.

## Tiring Critical
**Prereq:** Critical Focus, Base Attack Bonus: 13
When you score a critical hit, your opponent becomes fatigued.

## Blinding Critical
**Prereq:** Critical Focus, Base Attack Bonus: 15
When you score a critical hit, your opponent is blinded unless it makes a Fortitude save (DC 10 + your BAB) in which case it is dazzled for 1-4 rounds.

## Exhausting Critical
**Prereq:** Critical Focus, Tiring Critical, Base Attack Bonus: 15
When you score a critical hit, your opponent becomes exhausted.

## Stunning Critical
**Prereq:** Critical Focus, Staggering Critical, Base Attack Bonus: 17
When you score a critical hit, your opponent is stunned for 1d4 rounds unless it makes a Fortitude save (DC 10 + your BAB) in which case it is staggered for 1d4 rounds. Additional critical hits add to the effect's duration.

## Critical Mastery
**Prereq:** Fighter level: 14, Critical Focus, Two of: Blinding Critical, Exhausting Critical, Flaying Critical, Sickening Critical, Staggering Critical, Stunning Critical, Tiring Critical
When you score a critical hit, you apply the effects two critical feats in addition to the damage dealt.

## Feint
**Prereq:** Intelligence: 13, Combat Expertise
Make a Persuasion (Bluff) check against DC = 10 + target's BAB + target's Wisdom modifier. If the check is successful, your next melee attack against the target ignores their Dexterity bonus to AC. If the target is trained in Perception, the DC is 10 + the target's Perception bonus if higher than the base DC.

## Final Feint
**Prereq:** Intelligence: 13, Combat Expertise, Feint, Base Attack Bonus: 6
After a successful feint attempt, the target loses its Dexterity bonus against all melee attacks made that round.

## Slayer's Feint
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 15, Combat Expertise, Feint, Skill Focus - Mobility or Slayer Proficiencies
You can use your Mobility skill instead of Persuasion when making feint attempts.

## Two-Weapon Feint
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 15, Two-Weapon Fighting, Feint
When fighting with two weapons, you can forego your first main-hand attack and make a Persuasion (Bluff) check to feint an opponent.
