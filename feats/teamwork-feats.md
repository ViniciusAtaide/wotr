# Feats — Teamwork Feats
*12 feats. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

## Allied Spellcaster
When adjacent to an ally with this feat, you receive a +2 bonus to concentration checks and caster level checks to overcome spell resistance.

## Back to Back
**Prereq:** Perception: 3
When you are flanked and adjacent to an ally with this feat, you receive a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against attacks from opponents flanking you.

## Coordinated Defense
When adjacent to an ally with this feat, you receive a +2 competence bonus to your Combat Manoeuvre Defense. If the creature attempting the manoeuvre is larger than both you and your ally, the bonus increases to +4.

## Coordinated Maneuvers
When adjacent to an ally with this feat, you receive a +2 competence bonus to all combat manoeuvre checks. If you are attempting to break free from a grapple, the bonus increases to +4.

## Outflank
**Prereq:** Base Attack Bonus: 4
Whenever you and an ally with this feat are flanking the same creature, your flanking bonus to attack rolls increases to +4. Whenever you score a critical hit against the flanked creature, your ally can make an attack of opportunity.

## Precise Strike
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 13, Base Attack Bonus: 1
Whenever you and an ally with this feat are flanking the same creature, you deal an additional 1-6 damage with each successful melee attack. This stacks with sneak attack but is not multiplied on a critical hit.

## Seize the Moment
**Prereq:** Combat Reflexes
When an ally who has this feat scores a critical hit against an opponent that you threaten, you can make an attack of opportunity against that opponent.

## Shake It Off
When adjacent to allies who also have this feat you gain +1 to saves for each such ally (maximum +4).

## Shield Wall
**Prereq:** Shield Proficiency
When wielding a shield and adjacent to an ally who has this feat, the AC bonus from your shield increases. If your ally is wielding a buckler or light shield, the bonus is +1. If your ally is wielding a heavy shield or a tower shield, the bonus is +2.

## Shielded Caster
When adjacent to an ally with this feat, you receive a +4 competence bonus to concentration checks. If the ally is wielding a buckler or light shield, this increases to +5 and +6 if they are wielding a heavy shield or a tower shield.

## Spell Chain
When you make a successful attempt to overcome a target's spell resistance, the next ally with this feat trying to overcome the same target's spell resistance can roll twice and take the better result.

## Tandem Trip
**Prereq:** Trip
When adjacent to an ally with this feat and making a trip attempt, you can roll twice and take the better result.
