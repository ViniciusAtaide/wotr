# Feats — Combat Maneuvers
*14 feats. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

## Defensive Combat Training
Use your character level rather than your Base Attack Bonus when calculating your Combat Maneuver Defense.

## Bull Rush
**Prereq:** Power Attack
A bull rush attempts to push an opponent back (but does no harm). If you are successful, you will push the opponent back 5' (plus an additional 5' for every 5 by which your attack exceeds your opponents CMD).
The feat grants a +2 bonus to CMB when performing this feat and +2 bonus to CMD when defending against it.

## Greater Bull Rush
**Prereq:** Bull Rush, Base Attack Bonus: 6
Gain an additional +2 bonus on checks made to bull rush an opponent. If successful, the opponent's movement triggers attacks of opportunity.

## Dirty Trick
**Prereq:** Combat Expertise or Kinetic Warrior + Intelligence 13
Attempt to hinder an opponent as a standard action. If your attack is successful, the opponent is blinded, entangled or sickened. This condition lasts for 1 round (plus an additional round for every 5 by which your attack exceeds your opponents CMD).
The feat grants a +2 bonus to CMB when performing this feat and +2 bonus to CMD when defending against it.

## Greater Dirty Trick
**Prereq:** Dirty Trick, Base Attack Bonus: 6
Gain an additional +2 on checks made to attempt a dirty trick and the duration increases to 1-4 rounds + 1 round for every 5 points by which your attack exceeds your opponent's CMD.

## Disarm
**Prereq:** Combat Expertise or Kinetic Warrior + Intelligence 13
Attempt to disarm an opponent as a standard action. If your attack is successful, the opponent is unable to use his weapon for 1 round (plus an additional round for every 5 by which your attack exceeds your opponents CMD).
The feat grants a +2 bonus to CMB when performing this feat and +2 bonus to CMD when defending against it.

## Greater Disarm
**Prereq:** Disarm, Base Attack Bonus: 6
Gain an additional +2 on checks made to attempt a disarm and the duration increases to 1-4 rounds + 1 round for every 5 points by which your attack exceeds your opponent's CMD.

## Disarming Strike
**Prereq:** Disarm, Base Attack Bonus: 9
When you score a critical hit with a melee attack, you can additionally disarm your opponent if your confirmation roll exceeds the target's CMD.

## Sunder Armor
**Prereq:** Power Attack
You are able to perform the sunder armor combat maneuver with a +2 to CMB. If successful, your opponent loses its bonuses from armor for 1 round. For every 5 points by which your attack exceeds your opponent's CMD, the penalty lasts an additional round.
You also gain +2 CMD when defending against this maneuver.

## Greater Sunder Armor
**Prereq:** Sunder Armor, Base Attack Bonus: 6
You receive an additional +2 to CMB when performing the maneuver and the duration increases to 1-4 rounds + 1 round for every 5 points by which your attack exceeds your opponent's CMD.

## Trip
**Prereq:** Combat Expertise or Kinetic Warrior + Intelligence 13
Attempt to trip an opponent in place of a melee attack. If your attack is successful, the opponent is knocked prone. If the opponent has more than two legs, add +2 DC to the combat maneuver attack roll for each additional leg. Flying creatures, or creatures without legs cannot be tripped.
The feat grants a +2 bonus to CMB when performing this feat and +2 bonus to CMD when defending against it.

## Fury's Fall
**Prereq:** Trip
When making a trip attack, add your Dexterity bonus to your CMB.

## Greater Trip
**Prereq:** Trip, Base Attack Bonus: 6
Gain an additional +2 on checks made to trip an opponent. When you successfully trip an opponent, that opponent provokes attacks of opportunity.

## Agile Maneuvers
**Prereq:** One of: Bull Rush, Dirty Trick, Sunder Armor, Trip
Add your Dexterity bonus rather than your Strength bonus to your base attack bonus and size bonus when determining your Combat Maneuver Bonus.
