# Feats — Miscellaneous Feats
*19 feats. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

## Blind Fight
If you miss due to concealment, you can reroll your miss percentage roll. Invisible creatures do not receive their normal melee advantages - you retain your Dexterity bonus to AC and the attacker does not receive the +2 invisibility bonus. Finally, you are immune to gaze attacks.

## Improved Blind Fight
**Prereq:** Perception: 10, Blind Fight
Your melee attacks are only affected by total concealment and you may still reroll a miss. Invisible creatures within 30' do not receive their normal ranged attack advantages - you retain your Dexterity bonus to AC and the attacker does not receive the +2 invisibility bonus.

## Greater Blind Fight
**Prereq:** Perception: 15, Improved Blind Fight
Your melee attacks are only affected by total concealment and your miss percentage for total concealment is reduced to 20%. You may still reroll a miss. Invisible creatures do not receive any advantages regardless of range - you retain your Dexterity bonus to AC and the attacker does not receive the +2 invisibility bonus.

## Combat Expertise
**Prereq:** Intelligence: 13
You can choose to take a -1 penalty to melee attack rolls and combat maneuver checks to gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC. When your BAB reaches four (and every +4 thereafter), the penalty increases by -1 and dodge bonus increases by +1.

## Combat Reflexes
You can make a number of additional attacks of opportunity per round equal to your Dexterity bonus. You can also make attacks of opportunity while flat-footed.

## Diehard
When your HP is below 0 (but you are not killed), you can fight for 1 more round as if disabled. At the end of the next round, you will fall unconscious unless brought to above 0 HP.

## Disruptive
**Prereq:** Fighter Level 6 or Arcane bloodline
The DC to cast spells defensively increases by +4 for all enemies within 10'.

## Dodge
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 13
You gain a +1 dodge bonus to your AC. If you lose your Dexterity bonus, you will also lose the benefit of this feat.

## Combat Mobility
**Prereq:** Dodge
You gain a +4 dodge bonus to AC against attacks of opportunity when moving out of or within a threatened area. If you lose your Dexterity bonus, you will also lose the benefits of this feat.

## Endurance
You gain +2 bonus on Athletics checks and +4 bonus on Fortitude saves against fatigue and exhaustion. You may also sleep in light or medium armor without becoming fatigued.

## Frightening Ambush
**Prereq:** Persuasion: 1
When you hit a flat-footed opponent, you can make a demoralize attempt as a free action.

## Great Fortitude
You gain +2 bonus on all Fortitude saving throws.

## Improved Great Fortitude
**Prereq:** Great Fortitude
You gain an additional +2 bonus on all Fortitude saving throws.

## Improved Initiative
You get +4 bonus on initiative checks.

## Iron Will
You gain +2 bonus on all Will saving throws.

## Improved Iron Will
**Prereq:** Iron Will
You gain an additional +2 bonus on all Will saving throws.

## Lightning Reflexes
You gain +2 bonus on all Reflex saving throws.

## Improved Lightning Reflexes
**Prereq:** Lightning Reflexes
You gain an additional +2 bonus on all Reflex saving throws.

## Toughness
Gain +3 HP. Gain an additional +1 HP for each character level above 3.
