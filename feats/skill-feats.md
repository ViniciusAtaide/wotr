# Feats — Skill Feats
*7 feats. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

## Skill Focus
Select one skill to gain a +3 bonus on all checks against that skill. If you have 10 or more ranks in the skill, the bonus increases to +6.

## Alertness
You gain +2 to Perception checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in Perception, the bonus increases to +4.

## Deceitful
You gain +2 to Persuasion and Trickery skill checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in one of these skills, the bonus increases to +4 for that skill.

## Deft Hands
You gain +2 to Trickery and Use Magic Device skill checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in one of these skills, the bonus increases to +4 for that skill.

## Intimidating Prowess
Add your Strength modifier to your Persuasion Skill when making intimidate checks in addition to your Charisma modifier.

## Persuasive
You gain +2 to Persuasion and Perception skill checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in one of these skills, the bonus increases to +4 for that skill.

## Stealthy
You gain +2 to Mobility and Stealth skill checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in one of these skills, the bonus increases to +4 for that skill.
