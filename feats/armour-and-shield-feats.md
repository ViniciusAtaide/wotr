# Feats — Armour and Shield Feats
*18 feats. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

## Armor Proficiency (Light Armor)
You become proficient with Light Armor.
All classes apart from Arcanists (all), Instinctual Warriors, Ecclesitheurges, Monks (all but Sohei), Eldritch Scoundrels, Sword Saints, Sorcerers (all), Witches (all) and Wizards (all) receive this feat automatically.

## Armor Proficiency (Medium Armor)
Prerequisites:
Armor Proficiency (Light Armor)
You become proficient with Medium Armor.
Barbarians (not Instinctual Warrior), Bloodragers (all), Cavaliers (all), Clerics (not Ecclesitheurge), Druids (all), Fighters (all), Hunters (all), Inquisitors (all), Armored Battlemages, Oracles (all), Paladins (all), Rangers (all), Shamans (all), Shifters (all), Skalds (all), Slayers (not Stygian Slayer) and Warpriests (not Cult Leader) gain this proficiency automatically. Maguses other than Spelldancers and Sword Saints gain the ability to wear medium armour at level 7.

## Armor Proficiency (Heavy Armor)
Prerequisites:
Armor Proficiency (Medium Armor)
You become proficient with Heavy Armor.
Armored Hulks, Steelbloods, Cavaliers (not Beast Rider), Fighters (all), Paladins (not Divine Hunter), Warpriests (not Cult Leader) and the Hellknight Signifer prestige class gain this proficiency automatically. An Armored Battlemage gains the ability to wear heavy armour at level 7 while most other Magus archetypes gain the ability at level 13.

## Armor Focus
**Prereq:** Armor Proficiency (Light. Medium or Heavy), Base Attack Bonus: 1
Select one type of armour (light, medium, or heavy) and gain +1 AC when wearing that type of armour.

## Armor Proficiency (Light Barding)
Your companion becomes proficient with Light Barding.

## Armor Proficiency (Medium Barding)
Prerequisites:
Armor Proficiency (Light Barding)
Your companion becomes proficient with Medium Barding.

## Armor Proficiency (Heavy Barding)
Prerequisites:
Armor Proficiency (Medium Barding)
Your companion becomes proficient with Heavy Barding.

## Shield Proficiency
You become proficient with Bucklers, Light Shields and Heavy Shields.
Barbarians (all), Bards (all), Bloodragers (all), Cavaliers (all), Clerics (not Ecclesitheurge), Druids (all), Fighters (all), Hunters (all), Inquisitors (all), Oracles (all), Paladins (all), Rangers (all), Shifters (all), Skalds (all), Slayers (all) and Warpriests (not Cult Leader) gain this proficiency automatically.

## Tower Shield Proficiency
Prerequsities:
Shield Proficiency
You become proficient with Tower Shields.
Only Fighters receive this feat automatically.

## Shield Focus
Prerequsities:
Shield Proficiency (Buckler, Light or Heavy)
The AC bonus granted by your shield increases by +1.

## Greater Shield Focus
Prerequsities:
Fighter level: 8
Shield Focus
The AC bonus granted by your shield increases by an additional +1.

## Missile Shield
Prerequsities:
Dexterity: 13
Shield Focus
When equipped with a shield and not flat-footed, you may deflect one ranged weapon attack once per round.

## Ray Shield
Prerequsities:
Dexterity: 15
Missile Shield
When using a light, heavy or tower shield, add your shield bonus (not including enhancement bonuses) to your touch AC.

## Shield Bash
Prerequsities:
Shield Proficiency (Light or Heavy)
You may attack with light and heavy shields. A light shield deals 1-3 damage and is considered a light weapon. A heavy shield deals 1-4 damage. Two-weapon fighting penalties apply to the shield attack.

## Stumbling Bash
Prerequsities:
Shield Bash
Shield Focus
Fighter level 4 or Base Attack Bonus: 6
Creatures struck by your shield bash take a -2 penalty to their AC for 1 round.

## Toppling Bash
Prerequsities:
Shield Bash
Shield Focus
Stumbling Bash
Fighter level 8 or Base Attack Bonus: 11
Once per round when you hit a creature with shield bash, you can make a trip combat manoeuvre at -5 penalty. This does not provoke an attack of opportunity.

## Shield Master
Prerequsities:
Two-Weapon Fighting
Shield Bash
Base Attack Bonus: 11
You don't suffer penalties to your attack rolls made with a shield and the shield's enhancement bonus is added to attack and damage rolls.

## Bashing Finish
Prerequsities:
Shield Master
When you score a critical hit with a melee weapon, you can make a shield bash attack against the same target as a free action using the same attack bonus.
