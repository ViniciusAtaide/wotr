# Feats — Ranged Feats
*14 feats. Source: GameFAQs WotR Guide (80843).*

## Point-Blank Shot
Gain a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with ranged weapons at ranges of up to 30'.

## Precise Shot
**Prereq:** Point-Blank Shot
Use ranged weapons against a melee opponent without taking -4 penalty on your attack roll.

## Improved Precise Shot
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 19, Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Base Attack Bonus: 11
Your ranged attacks ignore the miss chance granted by anything but total concealment. Total concealment applies its normal miss chance.

## Clustered Shots
**Prereq:** Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Base Attack Bonus: 6
When you make multiple ranged weapon attacks against the same opponent in a round, total the damage from all hits before applying damage reduction.

## Deadly Aim
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 13, Base Attack Bonus: 1
Take a -1 penalty on all ranged attack rolls to gain +2 bonus on all ranged damage rolls. When your base attack bonus reaches 8 (and every +4 thereafter), the penalty increases by -1 and the damage increases by +2. The damage bonus does not apply to ranged touch attacks.

## Focused Shot
**Prereq:** Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Intelligence: 13
Make a ranged attack as a standard action and add your Intelligence modifier to the damage roll. You must be within 30' of your target and creatures immune to critical hits and sneak attacks are immune to this extra damage.

## Point Blank Master
**Prereq:** Weapon Specialization
When using a weapon in which you are specialised, you do not provoke attacks of opportunity when firing the selected weapon while threatened.

## Rapid Shot
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 13, Point-Blank Shot
When making a full attack with a ranged weapon, you can make an additional attack at full bonus but all of your attack rolls take a -2 penalty.

## Manyshot
**Prereq:** Dexterity: 17, Point-Blank Shot, Rapid Shot, Base Attack Bonus: 6
When making a full attack with a bow, the first attack releases two arrows. Damage bonuses (e.g. sneak attack, Strength bonus from using a composite bow, favoured enemy, etc.) apply equally to each arrow but so does Damage Reduction.

## Snap Shot
**Prereq:** Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Base Attack Bonus: 6
You threaten squares within your melee reach with a ranged weapon and you may make attacks of opportunity with that weapon.

## Improved Snap Shot
**Prereq:** Rapid Shot, Snap Shot, Base Attack Bonus: 9
You threaten an additional 5' with Snap Shot.

## Greater Snap Shot
**Prereq:** Improved Snap Shot, Dexterity: 17, Base Attack Bonus: 12
You threaten an additional 5' with Snap Shot. When you make an attack of opportunity, you gain a +2 bonus to damage and critical confirmation rolls. This increases to +4 at BAB 16 and +6 at BAB 20.

## Throw Anything
You receive a +1 circumstance bonus on attack rolls made with thrown splash weapons.

## Ranged Feint
**Prereq:** Feint
You can feint with a ranged weapon. A successful feint attempt denies the target its Dexterity bonus to AC against your ranged attacks.
